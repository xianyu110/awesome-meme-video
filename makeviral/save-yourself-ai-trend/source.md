## What is the Save Yourself trend, and where did it start?

The trend started on TikTok in Kazakhstan and Russia on 2 September 2026. That evening the Kazakh AI creator @unusualaisulu posted the first big one: she clings to a black rock in a stormy sea, and a second version of her, in a black suit, pulls her up and holds her. The lines read "Никто тебя не спасет... кроме тебя самой. Спаси себя!" ("Nobody will save you... except yourself. Save yourself!"). It had 463,400 views by 7 November. In Russian the trend is also called *видео погружение*.

Copies followed within days, many set to the track "Погружение" by Horus and Oxxxymiron. On 10 September a Thai post with the English line "No one will save you, but you" reached 688,300 views, and CapCut tutorials for "Only You Can Save Yourself" began. By October it ran in Arabic as *انقذ نفسك* and in Southeast Asia as the AI cliff rescue trend, and a 21 October post passed 2.1 million views. AI video apps sold it as a one-click "Save Yourself" preset from September, and in November a "Cliffside Rescue" preset was the most liked item on one AI video site.

### Which photo works best?

**Save yourself (1 photo):** one clear photo of you alone, from the waist up, face to the camera, in good light. The video makes two of you from it: the scared you in dark clothes and wet hair, and the calm you in a white shirt.

- **Sharp faces, no sunglasses, no strong filters.** A face that is small or turned away comes out less like you.
- **One person per photo.** Crop a group photo first.
