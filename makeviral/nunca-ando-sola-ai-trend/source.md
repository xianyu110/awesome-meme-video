## What is the Nunca Ando Sola trend, and where did it start?

The trend answers a common question. Someone asks "no te da miedo andar sola?" (aren't you afraid to walk alone?), and the picture answers "no, yo nunca ando sola" (no, I never walk alone): faith walks with you. Next to your shadow stand the shadows of Jesus, of Saint Jude Thaddeus (San Judas Tadeo, the patron of difficult causes, with his staff) and of the Virgin of Guadalupe in her oval of rays, each with a halo.

The first big post we found came on 9 September 2026: a black and white AI picture of a woman's shadow on a wall beside the three holy shadows, with 532,700 views by 19 October. A man's version ("yo nunca ando solo") followed on 14 September. On 15 October a creator in the US posted the version most people copy now: kneeling on a sunlit path at golden hour, seen from behind. A one-photo AI template came next, and Spanish how-to videos for the nunca ando sola ia photo took off: 1.1 million and 962,000 views on 18 October. A second style, hand in hand with the shadow of Jesus, spread in how-to videos from 19 October.
