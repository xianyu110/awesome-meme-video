## What is the I Put My Heart in This trend, and where did it start?

YJ, a rapper from London (TikTok @yeabsrajustice), first posted the song on TikTok on 16 April 2024. It opens with the lines everyone knows: "I put my heart in this. Why does everyone think they're the hardest? Why do you speak for the sake of speaking?"

In August 2024 he took it to the street. In interview after interview across London he took the microphone and sang it instead of answering the question. A clip filmed at Piccadilly Circus, posted by @emzzz_ctb on 18 August 2024, reached 12.1 million views. By October 2024 the song had passed 1 million streams, and YJ sang it at a London arena concert.

The clip this template copies came next. On 6 January 2025 @theysamirollin_ reposted a street interview with the caption "Me when i get asked a question in geography:". The interviewer asks "And what continent is New Zealand in?", YJ looks at the phone, takes the mic and sings. It has 15.3 million views and 2.1 million likes, and its two top comments are "the way he snatched the mic" and "Honey calm down calm down". It still comes back: a post on 24 August 2026 passed 2.4 million views.
