## What is the Office Parkour trend, and where did it start?

The scene opens "Gossip", the season 6 premiere of the US sitcom The Office, which aired on NBC on 17 September 2009. Michael Scott (Steve Carell) rolls over a couch and screams "Parkour!", and Dwight and Andy follow him over the reception counter, the desks and the kitchen fridge until one of them ends up on the floor. Know Your Meme lists it as the "Parkour!" meme, many people search for it as parkour the office, and fans also call it hardcore parkour.

The audio became a TikTok sound of its own: on 9 July 2021, @e_millli posted her three dogs racing around the living room to the parkour scene audio, and Snopes counted more than 8 million views in five days. The clip still travels: on 19 October 2026 @mrjonasdk's Japanese dub of the scene reached 2.4 million views and 360,700 likes on TikTok. In November 2026 the trend moved to AI: motion transfer templates copy the parkour office scene shot for shot and swap the whole cast for the faces in a few photos.
