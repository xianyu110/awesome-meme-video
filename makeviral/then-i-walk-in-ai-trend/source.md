## What is the Then I Walk In trend, and where did it start?

The meme started in September 2026 with one TikTok user who kept posting the same selfie, taken outside a school building, as a comment under other people's videos, always with the caption "Then I walk in." Know Your Meme, which added its entry on 27 October, found the picture spreading by 28 September; a barber meme built on it on 30 September passed 2 million views within a month. People started to call him the then I walk in guy.

In late October it became a format anyone could use. On 24 October the creator **@georgegaraway** posted a deadpan selfie walk past a brick building with the caption "then i walk in": 3.3 million plays and 642,000 likes by 29 October. Edits with "then he walks in" followed, and on 28 October **@cityboyjj** posted a group version, "Then we walked in.", that reached 1.49 million plays in under a day. By 29 October the #theniwalkin tag held 235 videos with 8.5 million views.

In US Google Trends for the 30 days to 29 October, the then I walk in trend averaged 73, about three times "hotel lobby ai". No AI template for it existed before this one.
