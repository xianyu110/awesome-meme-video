### Which photo works best?

Upload **one photo of yourself**. The tiny you copies your face, hair and clothes from it.

- **Face clear and sharp, in good light.** Face the camera or turn a little to it. Sunglasses, a mask or hair over the eyes make the tiny you look less like you.
- **Waist up or knees up is best.** The tiny you wears the clothes from your photo, so show your top.
- **One person in the photo.** In a group photo the AI may shrink the wrong person.
- **A pet works too.** A clear photo of your cat or dog gives a tiny pet that licks the ice cream.
- Use your own photo. Adults and pets only: never a child, a celebrity or someone else's photo without their yes.
