## What is the Dom Perignon Car Trend trend, and where did it start?

The song is "Toca Toca", a Spanish and English dance hit that the Romanian band Fly Project released in 2013. Its hook, "You say take me home, I said Dom Perignon", turned into a TikTok sound again in October 2026: lyric clips of the song by @_lightsongs (12 October) and @.breezyz (16 October) reached 434,400 and 2.1 million views.

On 14 October 2026 @wifin896 posted the clip this page copies, captioned "Bring back summer". He lip-syncs the hook next to his white coupe at a gas station at night, taps the headlight on "no, no, no", touches the plate, the wheel and the tail light on the beat, then sings along at the wheel, while the lyrics pop up in white, green and red. By 4 November it had 3.4 million views and 610,300 likes.

Car and bike owners copied the format within days: @rs_keanu with an estate car and two bottles of champagne (16 October), @neediloi6 on a motorbike (21 October, 468,800 views) and @driftahalle with a drift car (24 October). Car spotters had already set supercar edits to "Toca Toca" since late 2025, so many people call it the Toca Toca car trend.
