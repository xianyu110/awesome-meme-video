### Which photo works best?

Use **two photos of you, one person in each**: an old one from around 2016 (anything from about ten years ago works) and a recent one. The prompt keeps the face, hair and outfit of each photo, so your 2016 clothes come back too.

- Waist up or knees up works best. A tight face crop leaves the model guessing about your body and clothes.
- Sharp, front-facing faces in good light. Photograph an old print flat, with no glare.
- The bigger the change between the two photos (hair, glasses, style), the funnier the meeting.
- One person per photo. Crop your friends out of the old photo.
- Use only photos of yourself. Never a celebrity, an ex or a stranger.

For the Pet version, use a puppy or kitten photo of your pet and a photo of it now, each with the whole body in view.

## What goes wrong, and how do you fix it?

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Both versions look the same age | Use photos that are further apart, or an old photo where your hair or style was clearly different. Then run it again. |
| The two faces swap sides | Check the order: photo 1 is 2016 you, photo 2 is you now. Then run it again. |
| The wrong one says a line, or both talk at once | Two people talking is hard for AI. Run it again or pick Best. |
| The clip cuts to another shot | The prompt asks for one shot. Run it again or pick Best. |
| Your face looks less like your photo | Use a sharper, front-facing photo in good light. |
| A line you typed comes out wrong | Keep each line short and simple, then run it again. |
| The video model refuses the photo | Some photos get refused. The credits come back; try another photo. |

Every run is a new take, so the timing and the faces differ from run to run.
