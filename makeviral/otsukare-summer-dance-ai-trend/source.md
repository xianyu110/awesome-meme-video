## What is the Otsukare Summer Dance trend, and where did it start?

"おつかれSUMMER" (Otsukare Summer) is a song by the Japanese duo HALCALI from their 2003 album "Halcali Bacon", produced by Tomoyuki Tanaka of FPM. It was never a single. More than twenty years later it took off on social media in the UK and the US in spring 2025, and Billboard JAPAN reported on 3 March 2026 that TikTok views of the song had passed 6 billion.

The dance on this page is the 2026 wave. In May 2026 fans of the K-pop group NCT WISH started sharing this part of the song ("このパート流行れ", "let this part catch on", @hallli1n, 811,100 views), and in Korea it became the 꿍싯꿍싯 challenge, the "kkungsit kkungsit challenge that WISH launched" (@trendit041, 15 June). Creators then made it their own: @chu.u_ posted her "kkungsit dance" on 12 June (1.2 million views by 15 October), and @ornkamony captioned hers "daijobu deshou" on 25 June (636,800 views). In Korean people search for 오츠카레 썸머 or 다이죠브이 ("daijo-V") and call it the 오츠카레 썸머 챌린지; in Japanese it is おつかれSUMMER ダンス; in English, the Otsukare Summer trend, the daijoubu dance or the daijoubu deshou dance.

Pets joined in. AI-made dogs in onesies did the moves as early as May (@tarosuke0402), and on 3 August an AI-made white Pomeranian in a little dress danced the Otsukare Summer moves upright in a sunny blue studio (@mongsill.house), the reel this page's Pet copies. By 15 October 2026, #おつかれsummer had 16,459 TikTok videos and 486.8 million views, and #otsukaresummer 8,292 videos and 236.6 million views, counting the song's earlier waves too. New takes still went up that day.

### What is the Otsukare Summer dance?

### What does daijoubu desho mean?

The daijoubu desho meaning is "it's fine, right?" (also written daijoubu deshou; some call it the daijoubu desu dance, but the song says desho). The song writes it 大丈Vでしょ, with a V for the peace sign the dancers make, so it is also called the daijoubu V deshou dance. The lines after it say "it's no big deal, I'm not hurt, I'm not crying", which is the joke of the dance.

### What does Otsukare Summer mean?

"Otsukare" is what people in Japan say to each other after work: "good work" or "you must be tired". So the Otsukare Summer meaning is close to "good work, summer", a goodbye to the summer and to a summer love.

### What song is used in the Otsukare Summer trend?

It is "おつかれSUMMER" (HALCALI Otsukare Summer) by the Japanese duo HALCALI, a track on their 2003 album "Halcali Bacon".
