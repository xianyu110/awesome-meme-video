# Love Me Trend: Your Own LOVE Letters Edit, From One Photo

- Source: [makeviral](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/love-me-ai-trend)
- Slug: `love-me-ai-trend`

The love me trend is the TikTok edit where three big letters, L, V and E, hide in every shot and something round fills the gap to spell LOVE.
