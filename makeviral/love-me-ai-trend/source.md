## What is the Love Me Trend trend, and where did it start?

The love me trend grew out of fan edits on the song "Love Me" by JMSN. In each shot the editor places the letters L, V and E as solid 3D letters, some behind the people and some in front, and finds a round thing in the same shot to be the O: an eye, a tunnel mouth, a lamp or the moon.

The first post of the format we found, and the first with a million views, is an anime edit by the Vietnamese account @okusen67 on 28 September 2026 (4.3 million views). By 7 October a reaction video called it the "Love Me trend", and anime edits from France, Italy, Peru and Congo followed. On 17 October a French editor moved it to live-action film shots, with the caption "you can tell i had a hard time finding these letters" (3.5 million views). Since then, edits from Poland, Germany, Spain, the US and Canada pass a million views almost every day. On 25 October #loveme gained about 1,200 videos a day, up from about 200. Template accounts also sell a phone-editor template as "the viral love Me AI trend" since 19 October.
