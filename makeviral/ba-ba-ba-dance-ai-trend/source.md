## What is the Ba Ba Ba Dance trend, and where did it start?

The dance came on 2 October 2026, when @otr.j5 posted a two-person routine in a library with the caption "Ba ba baa". It had 611,200 views by 5 November, and most of the big posts credit him as the choreographer ("DC: @otr.j5").
