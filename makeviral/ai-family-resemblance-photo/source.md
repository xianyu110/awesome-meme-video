## What is the Split Face Trend trend, and where did it start?

Split family faces are older than AI: in 2011, the Canadian photographer Ulric Collette's "Genetic Portraits" joined the halves of relatives' faces by hand. The AI version started in August 2026. On 7 August, an Instagram user posted color half-and-half portraits of herself with her mother and with her father, made with Meta's AI tool. Later that month, Russian-language how-to videos shared a couple version, a girl and a guy, as the тренд половина лица ("half face trend"), and a long prompt for ChatGPT went around in their comments.

The parent version took off in September. CapCut templates turned it into a short clip, and a how-to by @clzl3vnad2 from 8 September reached 4.8 million views. The prompt came back in English, word for word, now with "the girl and the woman". @idatravels05's "You look like your mom" post passed 779,900 views. US Google searches for "split face trend" went from almost none in August to 1,900 in September 2026, and Know Your Meme added the "Parent Split-Face / Family Resemblance Trend" on 17 September. In mid-October, new posts still go up every day.

### Which photo works best?

Use **two photos with one face each**: photo 1 is you (the left half), photo 2 is your mom, dad or the family member (the right half).

- **Face the camera straight on**, head level, in even light. Two similar photos join best: about the same distance, both looking ahead.
- **Sharp and close, from the shoulders up.** A passport-style photo is ideal.
- **No sunglasses, hats or hair over the eyes,** so the eyes line up across the middle.
- **Color or old photos are fine.** The result is black and white anyway. Photograph an old print flat, with no glare.
- **One person per photo.** Crop a group photo first.
- Use photos of people who are happy to be in it.
