## SaWaDiKa meaning: why the palms come together

What does SaWaDiKa mean? It is LISA's spelling of "sawatdee ka" (สวัสดีค่ะ), the everyday Thai greeting. "Sawatdee" means hello (and goodbye), and "ka" is the polite word a woman puts at the end; a man says "sawatdee krap", which is why people also search "sawadikap". It is also spelled sawadeeka or sawadee ka, so some call this the sawadee ka dance. The SaWaDiKa meaning in English is simply a polite "hello". The greeting comes with the wai, the palms pressed together at the chest with a small bow, and that is the move the SaWaDiKa dance opens and closes with.
