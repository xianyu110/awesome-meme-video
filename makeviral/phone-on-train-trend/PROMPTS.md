# Phone on the Train Trend: Your Face in the Paris Metro Reel

- Source: [makeviral](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/phone-on-train-trend)

The phone on the train trend (also the Training Season train trend, or the phone taped to the train trend) is a 2026 reel joke: you tape or stick your phone on a metro window, sing on the platform while it films you, then run after the train as it leaves with your phone.
