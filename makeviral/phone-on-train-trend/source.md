## What is the Phone on the train trend, and where did it start?

Musicians started it to promote their songs. The earliest clip we found is from 8 June 2026, when the Canadian artist Manafest stuck his phone to a subway train. In August it took off: the Punjabi singer **@iiktaj** taped his phone to a train at Jalandhar (13 August), and the Russian singer @anisiialen did it in a metro (21 August, about 53 million views).

On 26 August **@olliemuhl** (Ollie Muhl) did it on the New York 7 train: "Anyone seen a phone stuck to the train recently?". The caption became the trend's name, and his TikTok of it has 78.7 million plays. On 31 August **@nikhilkochar80** posted the Delhi Metro "desi version", which passed 135 million views; on 5 September the Delhi Metro warned creators to stop.

In mid-September the trend reached Munich, Toronto, London, Istanbul, Berlin and Nigeria, and the live **Training Season** by Dua Lipa became its sound. On TikTok it also runs as the "phone taped on train window trend", and in New York as the "subway iPhone tape trend". On 3 October Ollie Muhl took it to Paris metro Line 6, with a new ending: the phone rides over the Seine to the lit Eiffel Tower. "Wait for the view" is the reel this template copies.

## How do they tape the phone to the train, and how do they get it back?

That is the question in almost every comment, and the reels never show it. Most creators fix the phone to the outside of the window with packing tape or a suction phone mount; @iiktaj used packing tape at Jalandhar. Most clips are staged: the phone is taped on while the train stands, and taken off before the train moves. A friend inside the train films the chase, and the clips are cut together (viewers spotted the friend in the window reflection). Some creators say a friend took the phone off at the next station. The "trend gone wrong" clips show the other side: doors that slide over the phone and trap it.

**Do not stick a real phone on a moving train.** The Delhi Metro warned creators on 5 September 2026 that it can injure other passengers and damage phones, and that "necessary action shall be taken".
