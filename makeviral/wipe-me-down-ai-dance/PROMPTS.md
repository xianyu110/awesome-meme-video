# Wipe Me Down AI Dance: Do the Viral Dance From One Photo

- Source: [makeviral](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/wipe-me-down-ai-dance)
- Slug: `wipe-me-down-ai-dance`

The Wipe Me Down AI dance turns one full-body photo of you into a short clip of you doing the viral Wipe Me Down dance: a low, bouncy stomp with your hands flicking at the floor on the beat, a hop, then arms swinging side to side.

## Prompts

### 1. 1. The video prompt is filled in. Settings: kling-3-motion, 10 s, 720p, 9:16, sound off. The video prompt

*Kind: video_prompt*

```text
One continuous single take, fixed camera, vertical video. Recreate the reference video exactly, but the dancer is the person from the reference image instead of the man: the same person with the same face, hair, skin tone, body and clothes, in the place from the reference image.
The person performs the man's stomp dance frame by frame with the same timing, on the beat: knees bent low, the bouncy stomp from foot to foot, the hands flicking down at the floor on every beat, the shoulders rocking, the two hops, the step toward the camera and the arms swinging out to one side and then the other. Big happy grin, full of energy.
No curly afro wig, no orange headband, no orange wristbands, no white basketball jersey with a number or a team name, no gray cargo shorts, no tattoos, no beard and no glasses unless the reference image shows them. No cuts, no text, no captions or logos on screen. Exactly one person, no extra people.
```
