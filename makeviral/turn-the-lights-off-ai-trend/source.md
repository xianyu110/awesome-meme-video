## What is the Turn the Lights Off trend, and where did it start?

The Turn the Lights Off meme comes from a club scene in the Apple TV+ series "Your Friends & Neighbors" (2025). Jon Hamm's character dances alone on a crowded dance floor, eyes closed and completely lost in the music, and creators cut the clip to "Turn the Lights Off", a 2009 dance track by Kato featuring Jon. According to Know Your Meme, Russian-speaking creators started the format in October 2025, putting the Jon Hamm club scene after a caption about a small win or a total disaster, and it spread in English through November and December 2025. In mid-December the song reached number one on Spotify's Viral 50.

The most viewed post, by @rare_gemz on 9 December 2025, had 5.3 million views. On 26 December the creator @ai_stas posted a tutorial that put his own face into the scene with an AI still and an AI video edit, and the Turn the Lights Off AI trend took off from there. In October 2026 the AI video sites added it as a template, under names like "Lost in the Club" and "Nightlife Dance Swap". By 4 November 2026, #turnthelightsoff had 34,419 TikTok videos and 298.5 million views.
