## What is the Stateside Dance trend, and where did it start?

The dance comes from the remix "Stateside + Zara Larsson", which came out on 10 October 2025 and as a single on 16 January 2026. Most reels of the routine credit the choreography to the Los Angeles dancer Brandt Ski ("dc: @brandt ski").

In December 2025 the Stateside TikTok dance took off. A tutorial by @jasminetxo on 18 December reached 12.7 million views, and a solo reel by @my_lilpriv on 23 December reached 17.9 million. On 19 January 2026 a duo version by @_juliaaanki passed 15.3 million views. In February the figure skater Alysa Liu skated her Winter Olympics gala routine to the remix, and the song reached a new crowd.

Pets joined in too: on 23 February an AI-made golden puppy by @wagsruff danced the routine and reached 2.5 million views. On 2 November 2026 #statesidedance held 1,696 TikTok videos and 39.2 million views.
