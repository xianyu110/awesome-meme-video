## What is the AI Chicken Dance trend, and where did it start?

The dance came first. A Nigerian DJ from Badagry posted his beat as an original TikTok sound on 22 July 2026, and Nigerian dancers turned it into a dance challenge: quick stepping kicks with punchy arms. The take that broke out is @bejay304's of 23 September 2026, which passed 2.1 million views, and two more posts passed 1 million views in October. Then came the hen.
