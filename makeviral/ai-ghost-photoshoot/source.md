## What is the Ghost Photoshoot trend, and where did it start?

The ghost photoshoot started on TikTok on **10 September 2020**, when user jackjanson88 posted photos of a friend in a bedsheet ghost costume set to "Oh Klahoma" by Jack Stauber, according to Know Your Meme. Six days later, #GhostPhotoshoot had more than 523 million views. The look stuck: a plain white sheet with two eye holes, round black sunglasses, everyday jeans and sneakers below, and a fall prop like a coffee cup, a pumpkin or a stack of books.

The trend comes back every Halloween with new ghost pictures, and the dog version comes with it: one by @nolatheiggy reached 29.5 million views in 2023. In 2026 the best friends version is back: a ghost photoshoot by @masonholmes71ff from 4 September had 312,700 plays on 8 October, and a golden retriever in a sheet by @goldenboyreign, posted on 4 October, had 255,200 plays.

People search for it every fall. In the US, Google counted 22,200 searches for "ghost photoshoot" in October 2024 and 18,100 in October 2025. On 8 October 2026 the Google top 10 for it had no AI tool, only photo ideas.

## What goes wrong with an AI ghost photoshoot, and how do you fix it?

We tested these prompts on AI-made people and a golden retriever before we published them. These are the real problems to watch for:

| What goes wrong | What the prompt does about it | If you still see it |
|---|---|---|
| The sheet hides your shoes | Ends the sheet "just above the knees" and keeps the clothes "that show below the sheet" | Use a photo with your feet in frame, then run it again. |
| The two friends get the same jeans | Keeps each person's clothes "exactly the same as in their own photo" | Our test kept light jeans with white sneakers and dark jeans with boots apart. Use two clear full-body photos. |
| Your pet looks too tall under the sheet | Drapes a small sheet to the pet's knees, with its ears through holes | Our dog came out sitting upright and tall but clearly itself. Run it again for a lower pose. |
| The top of the sheet looks pointed | Asks for a top "rounded like a cartoon ghost" | Keep it round and cute. A pointed top can look like a hood. |
| In the video, falling leaves cover the face | Keeps the camera still and the leaves light | Our Veo 3.1 Lite test did this; the default, Seedance 2.0 Mini, did not in two tests. Run it again, or pick Best (Seedance 2.5). |
