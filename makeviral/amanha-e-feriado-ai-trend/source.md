## What is the Amanhã é Feriado trend, and where did it start?

The clip comes from a 2008 report by Triada, a local TV channel in Russia, filmed in a club in Veliky Novgorod. It was online by 28 March 2008 as "Very Bad Music": the young man really complains about the music, then dances to it. Fake subtitles turned it into "Dimitri finds out", and the best-known version, from 2011, has him find out that it is Friday.

Brazil made it its own. "Dimitri descobre que é sexta-feira!" went up on YouTube on 25 March 2011 and passed 2.9 million views, and a holiday version, "Dimitri descobre que amanhã é feriado!", followed in January 2012. It returns before every long weekend.

The 2026 wave started on 11 October, the eve of Brazil's 12 October holiday. TikTok creator @jhonystark8 posted an AI remake with a TV sitcom character in Dimitri's place and a new Portuguese voice dub: "A festa tá boa, mas eu tô meio chateado. Arrumei um emprego e amanhã já tem que trabalhar. Mas amanhã é feriado!" It reached 533,100 views, and a repost by @previasmidia7 reached 569,500 views and 109,800 likes by 14 October. AI video apps started selling the same template that week.
