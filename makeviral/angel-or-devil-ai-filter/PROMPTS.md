# Angel or Devil Filter: Let the Meter Over Your Head Decide

- Source: [makeviral](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/angel-or-devil-ai-filter)
- Slug: `angel-or-devil-ai-filter`

The angel or devil filter is the TikTok face effect that puts a meter over your head: angel wings on one side, a red devil on the other, and two percent numbers that race each other.
