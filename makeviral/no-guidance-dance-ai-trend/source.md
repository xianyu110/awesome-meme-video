## What is the No Guidance Dance trend, and where did it start?

"No Guidance" is a 2019 song by Chris Brown featuring Drake, and its music video turns into a dance battle between the two. In 2020 a remix by the singer Ayzha Nyree took off on TikTok with its own No Guidance remix dance, and the No Guidance challenge followed: people film their own routine to the remix, often with the arms crossed on "play no games". By 21 October 2026, #noguidancechallenge had 8,916 TikTok videos and #noguidance 29,325 videos with 544.6 million views.

The dance never fully left. On 23 March 2026, a duo's street-corner reel from the Bay Area reached 2.4 million views. Then the train came. The Sydney creator @zacjeremiah dances on station platforms when his train leaves, and on 6 October 2026 he did the No Guidance dance on the platform while a friend filmed him from inside the departing train: 1.3 million views and 171,700 likes.

From 18 October, CapCut template posts started to paste famous faces onto a man who dances down a train aisle to the same sound, and on 21 October AI video sites added No Guidance dance templates. This page puts your own face in instead.
