## What is the DJ Cat trend, and where did it start?

The clips copy the look of a stadium DJ's arena highlight reel: a close-up at the turntables, a shot from behind towards a sea of phone lights, and the view from the floor with the DJ tiny under a single spotlight. AI creators began remaking those shots with animals in the DJ's place, and the joke is that the footage takes itself completely seriously.

On 17 October 2026 @beachboymax, an Instagram account for a Havanese called Max, posted "DJ Max is in the house": an AI-made fluffy dog working the decks in a packed arena, tongue out in the close-up and waving to the crowd at the end. By 7 November it had about 839,000 plays and 27,600 likes. Other AI pet versions of the same shots appeared, a Pomeranian in a striped shirt among them. On 30 October an AI template site began to offer the pet DJ arena video, and on 6 November a DJ cat template for cats and dogs followed. People post the clips under tags like #catdj, #djcat and #djdog.
