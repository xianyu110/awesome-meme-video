### Which photo works best?

Upload **one photo** of an ordinary moment. The AI keeps your shot, so the photo decides the framing.

- **Sit down.** A chair, a sofa or a step works for Jet engine, Train tracks and Tsunami. For Car on the tracks any photo works: the AI puts you in a driver's seat.
- **Face to the camera, sharp and well lit,** no sunglasses. The face stays the same size as in your photo, so do not use a far shot.
- **Hold something if you like:** a book, a phone or a coffee stays in your hands.
- **Leave space behind you.** A photo from the knees up or a full body photo leaves room for the danger.
- **One person in the photo.** Never use a photo of a stranger or a celebrity.
