## What is the Smalltown Boy trend, and where did it start?

"Smalltown Boy" is a synth-pop single by Bronski Beat, released in May 1984. It reached number three on the UK singles chart. In 2024 it had a TikTok moment, when people filmed their Gen X parents dancing to it like it was the 1980s.

The 2026 trend is a different format. It copies a short duo clip shot with a wide lens in front of a red wooden fence: one man grabs his head and screams into the lens while the other dances behind him, then they swap, dance side by side and rush the camera together. Reposts of the clip spread on TikTok in early October; one posted on 6 October had 484,000 plays four days later.

AI creators recast the duo as cats. On 2 October @fluffyfishlover posted a ginger cat and a tuxedo cat at the red fence. On 8 October @zefosik posted two cats in a white living room, which passed 597,000 plays and 23,800 shares within two days. Tutorials for one-click template apps followed. The Pet version on this page copies the living room reel, and the Human version puts two people into the red fence reel.
