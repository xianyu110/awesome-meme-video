### Which photo works best?

Use **one sharp, full-body photo**, head to shoes, so the model sees your height, build and stance. A costume is mostly the body, so a face-only selfie leaves it guessing.

- Stand straight and face the camera, in good daylight. Any room works: the prompt moves you to a Halloween party.
- Fitted, plain clothes show your shape. A big coat hides it.
- Wear your hair the way you plan to on the night.
- For **Couple costume**, add two photos, one person each. The first photo stands on the left and gets the first part of a pair, such as the angel in "Angel and devil".
- Use your own photo, or the photo of an adult who agreed. Never a celebrity. This template makes AI costumes for adults only.
