## What is the Talk to Me Doja trend, and where did it start?

The meme comes from a scene in the first episode of the anime "Yamada-kun and the Seven Witches". Ryu Yamada, the school delinquent, has just swapped bodies with the top student Urara Shiraishi, and when a boy asks "her" out, he tries to act like Urara: hands to the face, a shy sway back and forth. On 7 November 2026 @lovingbalanci posted that clip with the caption "Speak to me Doja" over a sped-up "Like That" by Doja Cat; Know Your Meme calls it the earliest known version. The tags #speaktomedoja and #talktomedoja appeared on TikTok the same day.

Two days later people started doing the sway themselves next to the anime GIF. One dance take from 9 November had 3.3 million views and 420,500 likes by 13 November, and the mattress store owner behind @twin.cities.mattress posted his take on 10 November (605,900 views). Know Your Meme added the entry on 12 November, and US Google Trends interest in "talk to me doja" rose from 3 to 19 between 8 and 12 November. By 13 November, #talktomedoja had 631 TikTok videos and 12 million views.
