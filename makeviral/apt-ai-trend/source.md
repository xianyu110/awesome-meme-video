## What is the APT Pink Studio Duo trend, and where did it start?

"APT." (ROSÉ & Bruno Mars) came out on 18 October 2024. Its video is one long party in a hot-pink studio: Rosé sings into a microphone on the floor, Bruno Mars plays a red drum kit, they lean on each other, sing into the lens and dance. By 15 October 2026 it had about 2.7 billion YouTube views. Cat versions followed within weeks: a cat dance video to the song, posted with #aptcat on 26 November 2024, had 7.6 million TikTok views by October 2026.

In late September 2026 the AI version took off. AI video tools can now copy a clip shot for shot and swap the faces, so people put two new faces into the studio, first famous ones, then their own. AI video sites turned it into a two-photo template from 29 September: photo 1 plays the drums, photo 2 sings. On 6 October a Peruvian couple posted their own APT AI video, the clip at the top of this page, and AI cat versions of the studio scene, the APT cat trend, run next to the people versions.

### Which photo works best?

You need **two photos**, one person in each.

- **Photo 1 is the drummer** at the red drum kit. **Photo 2 is the singer** with the microphone, who kneels on the floor in the first shot.
- **A sharp face, turned to the camera**, in good light. Chest-up is enough, but a photo that shows your outfit helps: you keep the clothes from your photo, and the clip shows you full body at the end.

- **For the Pet version**, use two pet photos: pet 1 drums, pet 2 sings. A clear photo of the whole animal, its face in view, works best. Cats and dogs both work.
- Use your own photo, or the photo of an adult who said yes. Never a celebrity, a stranger or a child.

## What goes wrong, and how do you fix it?

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The singer and the drummer are the wrong way round | Photo 1 always drums, photo 2 always sings. Swap your photos and run it again. |
| A face looks less like the photo | Use a sharp photo with the face to the camera, no hat brim or hand over it. A far, full-body photo gives the model too few pixels of your face. |
| The two faces blend | Use two photos with one person each. A group photo confuses the model. |
| A white cap or sunglasses appear | The prompt keeps your look from your photo. Run it again; a front-facing photo helps. |
| The mouth close-up is not your mouth | It is half a second of lips only. Every run is a new take, so a second run can come out closer. |
| The model refuses a photo | Some photos get refused. The credits come back; try another photo. |
