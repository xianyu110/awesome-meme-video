## What is the Bicep Heart Trend trend, and where did it start?

The bicep heart started as a gym joke on X: TikTok commenters wrote in late October 2026 that their X (Twitter) timelines were full of bicep hearts. ## How do you do the bicep heart by hand?

If you want to film it yourself first, here is how to do the bicep heart:

1. Make a fist with one arm and bend it up at the elbow, the fist next to your head, so the bicep and the forearm make a curve.
2. Bring the other hand to the inside of that arm, thumb down on the bicep and the fingers curled over the top.
3. The curve of your arm is one half of the heart and your hand is the other half. Turn your arm a little to the camera until the heart on the bicep reads clearly.

It works with any arm size: gym regulars made it big, and people with small arms post it too. Some call it the biceps heart trend, with an s; it is the same move. The AI version does the same moves for you from one photo, so you need no gym pump and no second take.
