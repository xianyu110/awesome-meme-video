## What is the Elevator Outfits trend, and where did it start?

The elevator fit check is older than the AI trend. On 18 December 2023 the fashion house Karl Lagerfeld posted an "elevator fitcheck" on TikTok: a model steps out of a wood-paneled hotel elevator in a new look each time the doors open. Put the phone on a stand outside the elevator, pointing straight in, and stand in the middle of the car. Let the doors close, change outfits, and film again in the same spot for every look.
