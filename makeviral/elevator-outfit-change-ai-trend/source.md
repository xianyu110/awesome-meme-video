## What is the Elevator Outfits trend, and where did it start?

The elevator fit check is older than the AI trend. On 18 December 2023 the fashion house Karl Lagerfeld posted an "elevator fitcheck" on TikTok: a model steps out of a wood-paneled hotel elevator in a new look each time the doors open. It had 369,800 plays by 17 October 2026, and other creators later put their own elevator fits on its original sound. On 25 September 2024 @bossymarie asked "does this transition have a potential?" over her own elevator outfit transition (144,300 plays), and on 12 August 2025 @karolvidaldiaz posted "4 pisos, 4 vestidos" (4 floors, 4 dresses): four evening gowns, one per floor, on the same sound.

In 2026 fashion brands made it an ad format, and two brand elevator reels passed 11 million plays each. Then it went AI. On 8 September 2026 @morgan.pierce.pm posted an AI-labeled "the elevator outfit change that broke my brain", by 11 October template makers were posting CapCut "Elevator Outfit Transition" templates, and on 12 October 2026 @fabianparks posted "AI elevator, real outfits": three looks, the doors closing between them, and a walk out to the camera. A day later the Korean creator @reels_drgn shared the full Seedance 2.5 prompt for the same shot. On 17 October 2026 #elevatorfitcheck had 1,161 TikTok videos and 3.9 million views.

### How do you film an elevator transition for real?

Put the phone on a stand outside the elevator, pointing straight in, and stand in the middle of the car. Let the doors close, change outfits, and film again in the same spot for every look. Then cut the takes together where the doors are shut. Editors like CapCut have elevator door transition templates for the cut, which is why an elevator trend CapCut template is a common search. A real elevator transition video takes a quiet elevator and many rides, so ask the building first.
