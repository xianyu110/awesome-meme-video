## What is the Beni Hatırlayınız trend, and where did it start?

The words are Atatürk's own, but he never said them. In the handwritten draft of the Onuncu Yıl Nutku, the speech he read on 29 October 1933 for the tenth year of the Republic, the last lines were: "Bu söylediklerim hakikat olduğu gün, senden ve bütün medeni beşeriyetten dileğim şudur: Beni hatırlayınız!" ("On the day what I say comes true, my wish from you and from all civilized humanity is this: remember me!"). Then he put a mark beside the last two sentences and crossed them out. Hikmet Bayur wrote in his memoir "Atatürk'ten Anılar" that the words felt very sad to him and that he asked Atatürk to remove them; others who read the draft asked the same. The draft is kept in the presidential archive in Çankaya. In 2010 Can Dündar showed the handwritten speech, with the crossed-out sentence, in Milliyet.

On 7 November 2025 Kiğılı posted a 10 Kasım film that opens with the line in an AI voice. Its caption: "10. Yıl Nutku'nun sonuna yazmıştın 'Beni Hatırlayınız' cümlesini. Sonra üstünü çizip çıkarmıştın konuşmandan... Biz altını çiziyoruz." ("You wrote it at the end of the speech, then crossed it out. We underline it.")

The shots of this video come from a different reel: Donna Kim's (@aiwithdonna) "Your Next Opponent Is You" video of 21 August 2026, where a small figure climbs into a beam of light and then faces a second figure on a higher hill. Here the meaning is not a contest. Atatürk waits in the light, you climb toward it, and the answer to "Beni hatırlayınız" is the one Türkiye gives every 10 Kasım: "Atam, izindeyiz."
