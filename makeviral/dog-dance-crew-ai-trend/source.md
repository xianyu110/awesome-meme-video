## What is the Dog Dance Crew trend, and where did it start?

The trend started with one clip. On 25 October 2026 the TikTok account @world.winnie, named after a golden retriever called Winnie, posted "When you dog assembles his crew": Winnie and three friends, a Dalmatian, an English bulldog and a husky, are four dogs dancing on stage in perfect sync, with a mirror floor under them. The caption tags it #ai. By 27 October it had 1.0 million views, 57,400 likes and 49,200 shares, and 82 videos used its sound. The account had already posted the same "one kick 10,000 times" caption as @winnie.golden.world, the AI golden retriever of the [bottle kick challenge](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/bottle-kick-challenge-ai).

The share count is the story: viewers send it to friends and tag their own dogs, and the comments pick a favorite ("team Husky") or ask for a chihuahua. The sound is the TikTok edit of Beyoncé's "Single Ladies", the song of the earlier [Single Ladies dog dance](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/single-ladies-dog-dance-ai), where one pet dances in front of two people. A puppy line-up came before: a "Dancing Puppy Crew" CapCut post with five puppies in a living room got 263,400 views from 7 October. On 28 October a photo app posted a tutorial of the dog dance crew clip.

The joke is a crew of very different dogs that somehow move as one, so the lead should stand out:

- **Fluffy or long-eared dogs.** The ears and fur fly on the head tosses, like the AI dog dancing at the front of the original, a golden retriever.
