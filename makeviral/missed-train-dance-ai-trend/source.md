## What is the Missed Train Dance trend, and where did it start?

The missed train dance comes from the Sydney creator @zacjeremiah, who tags his clips #trainguy. In them a friend films him as he runs down to the platform, the train leaves without him, he throws his head back in despair, and then he dances on the empty platform in his work clothes. His first post of this kind with over a million views went up on 11 February 2026 (6.2 million views), and dozens more followed.

On 5 May 2026 he posted the take the trend copies today: he runs down the stairs, the yellow doors close right in front of him, he drops to his knees and slides across the floor, then gets up and dances (10.3 million views, 1.5 million likes). His 7 October clip passed 9.6 million views.

On 18 October 2026 a phone-editor AI template started to copy that 5 May take shot for shot, sold as "missed my train AI dance, try it with your photo". UK template posts that put famous faces on it reached 681,000 views on 22 October, and US template accounts followed on 23 October.
