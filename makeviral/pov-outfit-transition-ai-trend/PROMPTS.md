# POV Outfit Transition AI: Six Items, Six Matching Looks

- Source: [makeviral](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/pov-outfit-transition-ai-trend)
- Slug: `pov-outfit-transition-ai-trend`

The POV outfit transition is the fashion reel filmed from your friend's eyes: a hand holds an item out to the camera in an empty spot, a jump cut, and you stand there in an outfit that matches it, again and again across the city.
