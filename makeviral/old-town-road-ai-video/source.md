## What is the Old Town Road trend, and where did it start?

"Old Town Road" is the country-rap song Lil Nas X uploaded on 2 December 2018. It spread on TikTok as the yeehaw challenge, and the remix with Billy Ray Cyrus came out on 4 April 2019. The video followed in May 2019: in its best-known scene, Billy Ray Cyrus leans out of a red Maserati convertible with a white money bag, Lil Nas X sings in a black cowboy hat in a candy and ice cream shop, and the two ride off together.
