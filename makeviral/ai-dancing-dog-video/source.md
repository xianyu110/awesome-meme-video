### Which photo works best?

Use **one sharp photo of your pet with the whole body in view**, ears to tail. The prompt stands the animal on its hind legs, so the model needs to see the legs, the paws and the markings.

- Good light, eye level, in focus. A blurry action shot gives a blurry dancer.
- Pick a photo of the room you want in the video. The prompt keeps your real room and extends the floor.
- One animal per photo. With two, the model may merge them.
- Clear markings help: a white chest, a spot over one eye. The prompt keeps them.
- Use your own pet, or a pet whose owner agreed.

## How do you post an AI dancing pet video?

Start with a real second. Many creators open with a short real clip of the pet sitting still, then cut to the dance: the contrast is the joke. A caption from the pet's point of view ("when the treats come out") works better than "AI dance".

Keep the pet's name the same across posts and post the same animal again and again, so people follow the pet, not a single clip. Tag the video as AI-made where the platform asks for it.
