## What is the Ain't No Mountain trend, and where did it start?

"Ain't No Mountain High Enough" is the soul duet that Nickolas Ashford and Valerie Simpson wrote for Marvin Gaye and Tammi Terrell, released in 1967; Diana Ross recorded her own version in 1970. Its chorus is one of the most sung lines in pop, and the song is busy on TikTok again in October 2026: #aintnomountainhighenough has 18,357 videos and 247.5 million views (20 October).
