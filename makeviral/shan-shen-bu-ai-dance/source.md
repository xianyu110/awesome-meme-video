## What is the Shan Shen Bu Dance trend, and where did it start?

The moves are real dance. Shan shen bu is a basic step of Anhui Huagudeng, the flower drum lantern dance of Anhui province, listed in China's first batch of national intangible cultural heritage in 2006. The viral clip is an old Beijing Dance Academy teaching video in which Professor Zhao Tiechun and a colleague name each move, then show it on a bare blue stage: 闪身步 (the flash step), 浪子踢球, 狗熊哆嗦毛 and 摇头晃脑.

In late September 2026 the clip went viral in China: Taiwan's TVBS counted 17,793 online mentions between 21 and 30 September, and on 1 October CCTV warned about knee injuries from copying the step without training.

Then the flash step dance went worldwide. On 29 September @._su05 turned the moves into an office skit about dodging work (13.7 million views), and the first AI version went viral the same day (6.1 million views). An AI shrimp followed on 3 October (2.1 million views), a French kung fu skit by @abregefrere on 5 October (5.5 million) and the K-pop group NEXZ on 15 October. By 17 October #闪身步 had 169.6 million views on TikTok.

## Shan shen bu meaning: what is the flash step?

What is the shan shen bu meaning? 闪 (shan) means to dodge or flash, 身 (shen) is the body and 步 (bu) is a step: "the body-dodging step". In English people call it the flash step, the dodge step or the feint step, and search "chinese flash step", "chinese flash dance", "chinese dodge step" or "chinese dodging dance"; the name is also spelled sha shen bu and shanshen bu. 閃身步 is the same word in traditional characters.

The Shan Shen Bu original is a teaching video of Huagudeng, the flower drum lantern dance from Anhui, filmed at the Beijing Dance Academy years ago: the teacher says the name, then shows the move. The other moves in the meme have names just as odd: "prodigal son kicks the ball" (浪子踢球) and the "bear shivering fur" dance (狗熊哆嗦毛). The flash step meme works because a serious folk dance lesson looks exactly like someone slipping out of trouble, which is why every Shan Shen Bu meme is a dodge: a broom, a boss, a chore.

## Shan Shen Bu Dance trend questions

### What is Shan Shen Bu?

Shan Shen Bu (闪身步, shanshenbu) is the "flash step", a sudden sideways dodge from Anhui Huagudeng folk dance. An old Beijing Dance Academy teaching video of it went viral in late September 2026, and the Shan Shen Bu dance became a meme: people use it to dodge brooms, bosses and chores, and AI makes animals do it.

### How do you write Shan Shen Bu in Chinese?

In simplified Chinese, used in mainland China, it is 闪身步. Taiwan and Hong Kong write it 閃身步, in traditional characters. The pinyin is shǎn shēn bù, which people also type as shan shenbu. Character by character it reads "dodge, body, step".
