## What is the I Need a Girl Dance trend, and where did it start?

The dance comes from one reel. She says the hold-up intro of "I Need a Girl (Part Two)", a 2002 hip-hop song, straight to the camera; he throws a peace sign high above his head; then the beat drops and both swing their arms, bounce and step to the lens with big grins.
