## What is the BAD Dance Challenge trend, and where did it start?

"BAD" is the title track of GOLDEN HOUR : Part.5, the 14th mini album of the K-pop boy group ATEEZ, out on 26 June 2026 (the music video had 103.4 million YouTube views by 23 October). The move that made it a challenge is the chest pop: a few seconds where the dancer leans the body to one side and pops the chest forward on the beat. Member San's version on the 28 June SBS Inkigayo stage spread across TikTok, Instagram Reels, X and YouTube Shorts within days (Korea JoongAng Daily, 14 July), and stars, comedians and fans from Korea to Thailand copied it.

The main TikTok sound of the song, posted on release day, has about 160,000 videos. In October the BAD challenge came back two ways: AI versions in Korea, where AI pets and AI characters arrive "late to the BAD challenge" (an AI Maltese on 16 October, an AI cat on 20 October), and a Malaysian dance crew's contest cover with 1.9 million views (21 October), while fans wait for the group's next comeback.
