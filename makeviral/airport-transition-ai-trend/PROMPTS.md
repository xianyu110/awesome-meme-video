# Airport Transition AI: From the Gate to a Neon City in One Cut

- Source: [makeviral](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/airport-transition-ai-trend)
- Slug: `airport-transition-ai-trend`

The airport transition is a two-shot reel: you sit at the airport gate, point at the camera, make a finger circle over the lens, and the camera dives through it into a neon city street at night, where you walk out and spin as the beat drops.
