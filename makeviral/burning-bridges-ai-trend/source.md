## What is the Burning Bridges trend, and where did it start?

"Burning Bridges" is a track on Drake's album *Iceman*, released on 15 May 2026. Its music video moves to a crowded dinner party under crystal chandeliers, where friends raise metal cups. The chorus line "Where she at?" comes with a move: one hand flat over the eyes like a visor while you scan the room. That move became the Where She At Drake meme, and it left music behind during the World Cup. On 2 July 2026, Drake was filmed doing it in Toronto after Cristiano Ronaldo scored for Portugal against Croatia, and on 6 July Ronaldo did it himself before Portugal played Spain.

The AI version followed in the autumn. The earliest dated one that the east.im guide found is a Threads post from 19 September 2026 that credits Higgsfield's Genjutsu character swap. People then put themselves, friends, pets and film characters into the party. On 5 October @alex.kuri posted his AI version side by side with the original; by 12 October it had 316,500 views and 34,100 likes on TikTok. The #burningbridgesai tag started on 7 October, passed 2 million views within three days and had 1,802 videos and 6.1 million views by 12 October.
