## What is the Baba Akü Yok trend, and where did it start?

The line comes from **Vizontele**, the 2001 comedy by Yılmaz Erdoğan and BKM, set in a small town in southeastern Turkey in 1974. In the **vizontele akü sahnesi**, Şafak Sezer's character tries to start the orange pickup, opens the hood and calls to his father, played by Cezmi Baskın, who sits on a chair by a blue door: "Baba, akü yok!" The father asks if the battery has just run flat. "Baba, akü komple yok. Çalmışlar." They opened the hood and stole the battery too, and the father has one request for the thieves: if you open the hood, at least do not steal the battery.

The official clip on Yılmaz Erdoğan's YouTube channel (7 November 2014) has 4.25 million views. The line became a stock reaction: a minister quoted it in 2019, a father and son in Adıyaman said it word for word when their pickup's battery was really stolen in 2023, and "Babow akü yok" on an empty supercar front trunk reached 22.5 million TikTok views. In late October 2026 the first AI face swaps of the scene hit TikTok, and from 12 November AI video apps advertised a two-photo **baba akü yok yapay zeka** template in Turkey.
