## What is the Reaching Out Cat trend, and where did it start?

The sound is a short clip of the line "reaching out to touch a stranger" from a Michael Jackson song. On TikTok it is called "original sound", and it was first posted on 23 July 2026 by @his_stories_7277: a hand reaches up, then a man laughs into the selfie camera. That clip has 527,200 views.

In late October cat owners started to use it. The phone lies on the floor and films a hand that reaches for the ceiling, then the video cuts to the family cat sitting calmly in the room, and on "touch a stranger" an arm sweeps in and lifts it out of the frame. @kb_rose24 posted a take called "Touch a stranger" on 4 November (439,700 views).

The reaching out cat trend broke out on 8 November, when @slyerstar_ posted her grey tabby: 4.2 million views and 1.2 million likes, with the caption "she's ok no kitty's were harmed". Within a week the cat posts reached millions each: @thehurtfam's black cat 4.6 million views, @im.mal.1k 3.4 million and @basic_orange_cats' "Simba's turn" by the Christmas tree 3.0 million, the clip this template copies. Many owners say they sped the clip up and never yanked the cat. On 15 November the sound held 30,100 videos.
