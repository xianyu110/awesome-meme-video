Use **one sharp photo of you, standing, with your face clear and your whole body in view.** The picture shows you from your head to your shoes, so a full-body photo gives the best result.

- **Face the camera** or turn a little to it, in good daylight. The same face goes into the real you and the drawing, so a sharp, well-lit face matters most.

## What can go wrong, and how to fix it

- **The drawing does not look like you.** The sketch is a loose self hug drawing in pencil with closed eyes, so it is softer than your photo. A sharp, front-facing photo helps it most. Run it again if the drawn face looks like someone else.
- **A second real person instead of a drawing.** Sometimes the person behind comes out in color or 3D. Run it again; the prompt asks for flat graphite with no color.
- **Extra arms or hands.** The hug is a layered illusion (the drawn arms over your real shoulders), and AI pictures sometimes add a hand. Run it again.
- **A spelling slip on the wall.** The words are asked for letter by letter, but a letter can still change. Check the words before you post.
- **Cut-off shoes.** Use a photo that shows your whole body, or accept the picture from the knees up.
