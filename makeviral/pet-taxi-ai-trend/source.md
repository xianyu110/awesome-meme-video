## What is the Cat Taxi trend, and where did it start?

The cat taxi starts with one AI-made clip from a Japanese city street at night. A man in a black suit with a briefcase stands at the curb and raises his hand. Far down the empty road a calico cat as big as a bus runs toward him, lies down beside him, and lets him stroke its cheek. He climbs onto its back, and the giant cat trots away with him between the street lights. The copies on TikTok carry the watermark of @cartoon_box86, an account that no longer exists.

From 10 November 2026 reposts spread under the plain caption "Cat taxi", one of them claiming "this is not a.i, this is a real life taxi cat famous in Asia". It is AI. It rides a bigger wave of giant cat clips: "When the train is late because the train is a cat" by @thegiantcatworld reached 4.0 million views from 18 October.
