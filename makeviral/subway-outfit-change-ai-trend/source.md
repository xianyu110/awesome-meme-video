## What is the Subway Outfits trend, and where did it start?

Subway fits are an old TikTok genre. In November 2023 Refinery29 called them "the new street style" and counted over 13 billion views in TikTok's search results for "subway fit". Transitions came with them: a passing train or a pillar hides the cut, and the creator comes back somewhere else or in something else. On 1 December 2024 @farbfilms used a subway pillar to jump from the platform to Times Square ("POV: You've finally been to New York, and now it's time for this one transition", 42,500 plays). ### How do you film a train transition for real?

Stand on one platform and put the phone on a stand on the opposite platform, pointing at you, never on the platform edge. Film one take per outfit in the same spot, then cut the takes together in an editor on the frames where a passing train covers you. It takes many takes and many trains, which is why most people use AI for train transition reels.
