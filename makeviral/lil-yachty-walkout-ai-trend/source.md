## What is the Lil Yachty Walkout trend, and where did it start?

The clip comes from the Lyrical Lemonade Summer Smash festival in August 2021: one handheld shot follows the rapper from backstage, past the speakers, out onto the stage and down a long runway into the crowd, with a high, bouncy step on every beat. Reposts called it the hardest walk out ever, and the #lilyachtywalkout hashtag was made on 10 July 2022. A TikTok repost by @musicmediaco from April 2024, "The HARDEST Walk Out of All Time", has 1.1 million views.

The first AI wave came in April 2024: from 8 April, people used Viggle AI to swap other faces into the clip, and the Lil Yachty walking on stage meme spread on X as a face swap format: the Lil Yachty walk out meme became a template for any face.

In 2026 it came back with people's own faces. On 28 September @brendanfallis posted his AI version, his own face doing the whole walkout, with the caption "If Monday was a feeling for me it would be this!". From 30 October US creators posted "Stage Walkout Filter" tutorials for a one-photo template, and AI template sites added the walkout on 4 November.
