## What is the You Talking Bout Me trend, and where did it start?

The You Talking Bout Me meme comes from a stream clip of the rapper DDG and two friends at a restaurant counter. The friend closest to the camera laughs about bills, the one in the middle keeps asking "You talking about me?", and the joke is really about the quiet friend at the end, who looks at his phone and never reacts. Copies were on TikTok by 29 August 2023, when a repost by @splabbin passed 1.1 million views.

In July 2025 the meme got a second life with "accuracy" recreations: friends re-enacted the scene at their own counters, and one posted on 9 July passed 8.2 million views. The reposts never stopped: the full clip got 1.7 million views on 30 October 2025, and @zincozz's "You talking about me?" reached 2.1 million views and 353,000 likes after 20 August 2026. In October 2026 AI video sites turned it into a template with one to three photos, from 13 October. People search it as "I be thinking about my bills", the DDG bills meme or "you talking bout me DDG".
