## What is the Marigold Bridge AI trend, and where did it start?

In Mexico, cempasúchil (marigold) petals are said to guide the dead home on the Day of the Dead, 1 and 2 November. The 2017 Pixar film Coco made that path famous as a glowing bridge of petals to the afterlife: the marigold bridge, or puente de cempasúchil. People still search for the Coco bridge, the Coco marigold bridge and the Coco bridge scene, the crossing to the land of the dead, every October: "coco bridge" had about 1,600 US searches in October 2025.

In October 2025, Mexican TikTok filled with photos of relatives who had died that year, under the line "Es su primer Día de Muertos, no lo dejen cruzar solo".

The AI version came on 20 October 2026. The CapCut creator @ia_chiomora posted a template that turns one photo into a 3D animated clip of the person stepping onto the glowing bridge and gathering the petals, then their real photo on an altar. Her caption, "No lo dejen solito, es su primera vez cruzando el puente de cempasúchil" ("Don't leave him alone, it's his first time crossing the marigold bridge"), reached 3.5 million views in under three days, and the template passed 9,900 uses by 21 October.

## No lo dejen solito: what the words mean

"No lo dejen solito" means "don't leave him alone". The full line, "Es su primera vez cruzando el puente de cempasúchil", means "It's his first time crossing the marigold bridge": the person died in the last year, and this is su primer Día de Muertos, their first Day of the Dead. For a woman, posts write "no la dejen solita"; others write "no me lo dejen solito" or "no lo dejen solo", and the 2025 posts said "es su primer Día de Muertos". Some write to the person instead: "primer Día de Muertos sin ti", the first Day of the Dead without you.

**The marigold bridge meaning.** In Mexican belief, the bright color and scent of the cempasúchil guide the souls from the land of the dead, the Mictlán, back to their families for one night. Families lay petals in a path up to the ofrenda. That is what the puente de flores de cempasúchil, or puente de pétalos de cempasúchil, stands for: the way home. Que significa el puente de cempasúchil? It is a symbol of that path, not an old legend with a fixed text: the leyenda del puente de cempasúchil that people search for is this belief, told in many ways. Some posts call it the puente de cempasúchil en el cielo, the bridge in the sky.

The comments under the trend show what it means to people: thousands name a father, a grandmother or a friend who will cross for the first time this year, and ask the others to walk with them.
