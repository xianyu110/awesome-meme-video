## What is the Nestle Crunch Trend trend, and where did it start?

The Nestle Crunch ad is a 1980s TV commercial: a sunny backyard set, a big close-up bite of the bar, wide-eyed faces at the camera and the jingle line "That's why I love Nestle Crunch". On 14 September 2009 the YouTuber AnnainCA uploaded it as "1980s Nestle Crunch Commercial", and on 27 September 2016 a Vine edit by Wille J swapped the bite for a slap and passed 9.3 million loops. That made it a Nestle Crunch meme: years of edits with a surprise ending, and a TikTok sound from 2019 (Know Your Meme). #thatswhyilovenestlecrunch holds 2,238 videos with 85 million views.

The AI wave started on 23 October 2026. At 11:18 UTC a UK account posted the first clip of a new CapCut AI template that swaps your face into the ad, with the caption "pov: you always wanted to know how you'd look in this ad". Two posts from that first day had 503,000 and 343,000 views by 27 October. By 27 October the template sound had 365 videos and gained 139 a day, and 105 of 158 posts came from UK accounts, with 88% of the views. Tutorials and copies followed in Poland, Italy, Germany and France.
