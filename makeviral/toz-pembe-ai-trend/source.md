## What is the Toz Pembe Dance trend, and where did it start?

"Toz Pembe" is a song by manifest, a six-member Turkish girl group that came out of the YouTube talent show Big5 Türkiye. It came out on 3 July 2026 on Hypers Music, with a music video by Mali Ergin in a 2000s pop look: the group dances in front of a giant tape-deck boombox, a set that took about ten days of handwork to build. The video had 100.9 million YouTube views by 12 October 2026, and Spotify put the song third on its list of the most-streamed songs in Turkey in summer 2026. Google searches for "toz pembe" in Turkey went from 33,100 in June to 246,000 in July.

On TikTok the routine spread through dance covers and tutorials, and edits of dancing kittens set to the song passed half a million views. In October 2026, AI video apps began to advertise a Toz Pembe music video template in Turkey. On 4 October the AI creator @caotic.avocado posted three AI-made cats in wigs and Y2K outfits dancing the routine in front of a giant boombox. Commenters named the cat group "Kedifest".
