## What is the Wassup in a Minute trend, and where did it start?

The sound came first. On 14 January 2026, producer zbee posted a jersey club remix of "From Time", the 2013 Drake song with Jhené Aiko. Its line "wassup, been a minute" is what most people hear as "wassup in a minute", and some hear "wasala in a minute" or "wassup wait a minute".

The look is older. For years, fans of the football anime Blue Lock have drawn its players as Atlanta rappers, in puffer jackets, chains and baggy jeans, holding stacks of cash. They call it Atlanta Lock. On 22 October 2026 the US animator @ristyisvalid made four of those drawings sway on a white background to the remix, one after another: 1.2 million views. The next day's post, captioned "Wassup in a minute", reached about 1.45 million views and gave the trend its name.

Within two days, fans redrew it with characters from other anime. On 24 and 25 October the AI wave followed: tutorials for the "character shake effect" with an AI video app template, an AI image prompt, and "upload one photo" templates that put you in the wassup in a minute anime edit.
