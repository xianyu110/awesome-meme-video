## What is the Lego Car Wash AI trend, and where did it start?

The earliest AI version we found is "Ai lego washing car" by the AI creator @ai_forever2, posted on 12 September 2026: a hand sets a muddy yellow classic car on a toy conveyor, a toy worker sprays it, foam pours over it, and it ends clean on a spotlit stand. A CapCut template that does the same from one photo followed. On 26 September @elrambo229 in Mexico washed his white work truck with it, and in early October posts came from Mexico, Colombia and the UK.

The trend took off on 10 October, when truck drivers in El Salvador and Guatemala posted their own rigs: @adilson789's white truck reached 179,100 views, and on 11 October @lpez55124's dump truck reached 354,800. "Esos lavados están mejor que los que le hacemos nosotros" ("those washes are better than the ones we do"), one driver wrote. From 13 October, tutorial accounts named it the Lego Car Spa, and @isolde3292's Spanish tutorials passed 2.2 million views. The Lego car wash TikTok trend now lives under #lavadodeautolego, with 440 videos and 7 million views by 15 October.
