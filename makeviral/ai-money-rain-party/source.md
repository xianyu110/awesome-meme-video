## What is the Money Rain Party trend, and where did it start?

Showering the groom and the dancers with notes is an old custom at weddings in Pakistan and India. In Urdu and Hindi it is called paise ki barish, "money rain" (پیسوں کی بارش in Urdu, पैसों की बारिश in Hindi), also written paison ki barish or paiso ki barish, and in Punjabi captions noto ki barish or notan di barish. Wedding videographers and families post these moments, and they are some of the biggest wedding clips on TikTok: a barat day clip by @khawajauzair0 (18 December 2022) has 2.5 million views under #moneyrain, and a money rain clip from Islamabad by @safivines43 (20 November 2024) has 26.2 million. On 22 October 2026, #moneyrain held 23,405 videos with 509 million views.

In October 2026 the custom became an AI trend. On 16 October the Pakistani AI template promoter @lulu.ai04 posted a tutorial that opens with AI-made results: men in festive shalwar kameez dancing down a wedding hall under chandeliers, and a crowd on a red carpet in a night garden, while notes rain on them. The Urdu voice-over says it shows how to make this video from your own photo. It reached 607,700 views and 21,300 likes. A second tutorial on 18 October, with a party on a lit terrace, reached 405,100 views. Both carry TikTok's AI-generated label.
