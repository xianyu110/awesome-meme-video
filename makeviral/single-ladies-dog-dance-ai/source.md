## What is the Single Ladies Dog trend, and where did it start?

"Single Ladies (Put a Ring on It)" is Beyoncé's hit from October 2008. Its music video dance was choreographed by Frank Gatson Jr. and JaQuel Knight after a 1969 Bob Fosse routine. On TikTok the dance came back in late 2023 on an edit of the song posted on 5 November 2023; a version by three members of TWICE from 26 December 2023 has 43.6 million views.

On 15 September 2026 @beridzelenee posted the routine with two other dancers in a dark studio. It had 5.8 million views and 978,300 likes by 12 October. Two weeks later, AI versions put a pet in the middle dancer's place: the first ones we found are from 29 September, and a CapCut template ("Swap your pet in for this Beyoncé dance trend") spread the idea.

The biggest one came on 4 October: the dachshund account @king_odiee put its long-haired dog in the middle spot, "I learned some new dance moves from my girl @Beyoncé". It reached 40.7 million views, 5.1 million likes and 1.2 million shares by 12 October. A how-to by @miso_ara from the same day had 12.7 million views.

The joke is the contrast: two dancers give the routine everything, and your pet in front of them does a few serious head moves like the star of the show. So the best casts are:
