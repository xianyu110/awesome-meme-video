## What is the Christian Dior Dance trend, and where did it start?

The sound went up on TikTok in late June 2026: a sped-up house edit of the line "you know my style and I love Christian Dior" from "Dior", the 2025 UK number one by MK featuring Chrystal. On 2 July the South African creator @eishhdawgg, who posted the sound, did the first dance to it with two friends and asked "Do we rate this dc?". The clip had 251,600 views by 12 November.

The Christian Dior dance trend grew in the US in the autumn. The twins @kymaxwell31 posted the first big duo on 13 September (595,000 views). On 6 October two soccer players filmed it on a floodlit field (4.6 million views), and the same day two college friends, @3thngreene, did it in their dorm room: 4.0 million views and 292,600 likes. That dorm reel is the example on this page.

Solo takes followed, from a patio (4.1 million views) to a TV weatherman in front of his weather map. By early November the sound held about 149,000 videos, with 3,600 to 9,500 new ones a day, and US Google searches for "christian dior dance" went from 10 in September to 210 in October.
