## What is the Hotline Bling Dance trend, and where did it start?

"Hotline Bling" is a 2015 single by the rapper Drake. Its music video premiered on 19 October 2015 and went up on YouTube on 26 October 2015, where it had 2.16 billion views by 10 November 2026. In it, Drake dances alone in a red puffer jacket and a turtleneck inside empty boxes of light that slowly change color. Critics compared the set to the light art of James Turrell, who answered in a statement that he was flattered but had nothing to do with the video.

The Drake Hotline Bling dance became a meme within days. Creators filmed their own versions: the YouTuber Just Sul posted a parody on 24 October 2015. On TikTok, #hotlinebling has 90,912 videos and 1.19 billion views (10 November 2026).

The Hotline Bling trend came back eleven years later as an AI wave. AI video template sites added a Hotline Bling dance template from 9 October 2026, two more sites added one on 9 November, and from 5 November AI clips on TikTok put cartoon characters into the same glowing rooms.
