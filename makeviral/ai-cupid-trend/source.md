## What is the AI Cupid Trend trend, and where did it start?

It started as a sketch meme. In late August 2026 a short black-and-white cartoon went around TikTok: a woman walks along, a little Cupid aims his bow at her, and she swats him away with her handbag without even stopping. One English repost carried the line "Aim somewhere else!" and the caption "Not today Cupid", and Spanish-speaking women posted it with captions like "I hit Cupid with my bag so many times he never came back".

Then AI creators made it photoreal. On 2 September 2026 an Argentine account posted a cinematic AI version, a winged Cupid diving from a palace roof at a woman in a white coat, with the caption "le dimos una paliza a cupido" (we gave Cupid a beating); it passed 298,000 views. In October two Instagram reels took the joke further. On 9 October @anamadeai posted an AI fashion film where a cherub's heart arrow misses the man and hits a coat in a shop window, so the woman falls for the coat (103,398 likes by 21 October). On 11 October @emily.skye.official posted the handbag version at a Gothic cathedral, which reached 496,546 likes and 11,133 comments by 21 October.
