## What is the Anıtkabir AI trend, and where did it start?

Built between 1944 and 1953, Anıtkabir received Atatürk's remains on 10 November 1953, and its main hall is ringed by a colonnade of 44 square stone columns. A photo in front of those columns is a classic family picture.

AI made the same picture possible without the trip to Ankara. On 7 August 2025 the TikTok couple @osman_merve_ozer posted an AI image of a couple in a white trouser suit and a black suit walking hand in hand across the stone square, with the colonnade and the crowd behind them, and put their full prompt in the caption, calling it possibly the best ChatGPT prompt. The post had 124.2K views by 30 October 2026, and several comments asked for the prompt as text to copy.

In October 2026 the look turned into a video template. From 27 October an AI photo app ran Turkish ads in which one person, a couple or a family stands full body in front of the colonnade in bright sun and moves a little. Our ad watch counted 31 new ads on 30 October alone. With 29 Ekim over, the same scene now fits 10 Kasım visits and every national day.

### Which photo works best?

You need **one photo** for Single, **two photos** for Couple / Family (one person per photo), or **one photo of your pet** for Pet.

- **Full body is best**: you stand in the result from head to shoes, so a photo that shows you at least from the knees up gives the best fit.
- **A sharp face**, turned to the camera, in good light, with no sunglasses and nothing in front of it.
- **Your clothes change**: the result dresses you in an elegant white trouser suit or a black suit with a white shirt, the look of the trend. Your face, hair and body stay yours.
- **For 10 Kasım, pick a calm photo.** The model keeps your expression, and a big laugh does not fit a day of remembrance.
- Use your own photos, or photos of adults who said yes. Never a stranger or a celebrity.

## How we keep it respectful

Anıtkabir is Atatürk's resting place, so a few things never happen in your photo or video:

- **No Atatürk face.** No portrait, statue face or picture of him is added to the scene; it is you and the building.
- **Calm poses only.** You stand, walk a few steps or smile. No dancing, jumping, silly poses or gestures.
- **No flag misuse.** No flag is worn, dropped or placed on the ground, and no party logo or slogan appears.
- **No text on the picture.** Add your own caption when you post.

When you share it, say it is an AI picture. Real photos of the place stay clear from the made ones that way.

Our prompts start from the prompt @osman_merve_ozer shared in the caption of their AI couple post on 7 August 2025: a wide shot in daylight, the couple walking in the front, the colonnade and the crowd behind, a white trouser suit and a black suit, a patterned stone floor and a lightly cloudy sky. We keep its words and order, remove the sunglasses so your face shows, and add a version for one person and one for a pet. The video part is ours, because the original is a still image.

### Can I make it with my partner or my family?

Yes. Pick Couple / Family and upload two photos, one person in each: a partner, a parent, a sibling or a friend. The two of you walk hand in hand toward the camera and stop side by side. Use photos of adults who said yes.

### Can I make it with my dog or cat?

Yes. Pick Pet and upload a clear photo of your pet's face and body. Your pet sits calmly on the stone square in front of the colonnade, looks around and then at the camera.

### Is this a real photo taken at Anıtkabir?

No. It is an AI picture: the building, the sun and the visitors are made by the model, and only your face and body come from your photo. Say that it is AI when you post it.

### Is Atatürk in the picture?

No. This page never adds his face, portrait or statue: it shows you in front of the building. If you want a picture next to his framed portrait, use [Atatürk ile Fotoğraf](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/ataturk-ile-fotograf-ai-trend); for a walk beside his silhouette, use [Atam İzindeyiz](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/atam-izindeyiz-ai-trend).

5 seconds, vertical 9:16, with soft wind, a distant crowd and a calm piano melody, and no speech. Photo only gives a vertical 2K photo. Both fit Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp status.
