## What is the All I Want Xmas Dance trend, and where did it start?

The dance started on 3 November 2026, when the creator Ayiah Soufi (@ayiahsoufi_) posted it with a friend under the caption "NEW CHRISTMAS DANCE ALERT! DC: US!!" (DC means dance credit: they made it up). The clip had 4.6 million views and 600,500 likes by 12 November, and its top comment, "Ok now this dance is actually good", had 115,900 likes. The sound is a sped-up cut of Mariah Carey's 1994 hit "All I Want for Christmas Is You", a TikTok sound first posted in November 2024 that had 112,700 videos by 12 November 2026.

Two days later she posted "GUYS KEEP DOING OUR DANCE!" (2.3 million views), then a trio and a 4-person version.
