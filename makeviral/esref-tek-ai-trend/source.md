### Which photo works best?

Use **one sharp photo of your face**, from the chest up, looking at the camera.

- Daylight or a bright room, eyes open, nothing covering the face. The close-up stare is the big moment, so a sharp face matters most.
- A calm, serious face works best: the lead never smiles.
- Any clothes are fine. The video dresses you in a black shirt and a long black coat.
- Your beard or moustache stays as it is in your photo; the video never adds one.
- One person per photo. With two faces, the model may mix them.
- Use a photo of yourself or of an adult who agreed to it, never a photo of an actor.

For the **Pet** version, use one photo of your dog or cat with the whole body and the face in view.
