## What is the Snow Leopard Meme trend, and where did it start?

The meme starts with a real photo. A snow leopard named Himmel sits behind the glass at Ishikawa Zoo in Nomi, Japan, with its front paws up, and an X user posted the photo on 25 May 2024. In October 2026 someone edited it with AI and gave the snow leopard the huge, round, glossy eyes of **Mei Mei**, also called Tole Tole Cat, a cat that went viral in 2023. That is why fans call it the **snow leopard Mei Mei**, **Mei Mei snow leopard** or **Snowmei**.

Know Your Meme dates the first meme post to 23 October 2026 on X. The caption "*paws at you*" is an older roleplay line, written like an action between asterisks, and people follow it with a punchline: a game invite like "hop online" or "deadlock?", "True friendship", or "am i annoying". On 28 October the Epic Games Store posted "paws at you hop online" on X.

On TikTok the hashtag #snowleopardmeimei started on 24 October and had 894 videos and 16.1 million views a week later. The most watched edits passed 1.7 million plays within days. On Google Trends in the US, "paws at you" rose from 7 on 24 October to 32 on 30 October, and "snow leopard meme" went from too few searches to show to 24 on 1 November.
