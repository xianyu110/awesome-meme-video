## What is the Flying Roadster AI trend, and where did it start?

On 15 October 2026 Tesla showed its new Roadster at SpaceX's test site in McGregor, Texas. This Tesla Roadster flying car drove upside down on a curved ramp, lifted on cold-gas thrusters, flipped and landed. Many viewers took the footage for AI, but Snopes rated it real. Tesla plans production for 2028.

The memes came that night. At 05:51 UTC on 16 October, X user **@pingu4ty** replied to Elon Musk's post with an AI clip, "Just remember which button is which": a man stuck between yellow cabs in a white sports car presses a button by the cup holder, the car blasts straight up between the towers and his coffee flies. By 17 October it had 225,900 views on X. From 08:00 UTC the same scene ran on TikTok with captions like "Day 1 of owning a Tesla Roadster", and creators remade it with their own faces. The first AI clip past a million views followed at 11:31 UTC (@xander.sgc, a sky full of flying cars, 1.9 million), and a Kling 4.0 clip from that afternoon has 2.7 million. By the end of the day, AI video sites sold the traffic jam scene as a ready-made template.
