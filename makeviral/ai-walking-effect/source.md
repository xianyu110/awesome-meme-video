## What is the AI Walking Effect trend, and where did it start?

The AI walking effect started in 2025 as a photo-to-video trick: creators fed one photo to an AI app and got back a slow, confident walk toward the camera. The #aiwalkingeffect hashtag was created on 2 June 2025. On 11 September 2025 the Indonesian creator @arsya.rich posted "Efek foto bisa berjalan" ("a photo effect that can walk"): a woman in a white headscarf walking down a flower path. It reached 1.8 million views.

The ai walking trend took off in South Asia that autumn. A post from 12 October 2025 called a "trending Ai walking effect editing video" reached 5.3 million views, and a how-to from 8 November 2025 shared the Gemini prompt behind a walk (1.5 million views). The AI walking effect TikTok wave has not stopped: in 2026 creators turned it into in-app effects, such as an AI model walk effect, and a post tagged #aiwalkingeffect on 1 November 2026 has 315,500 views.

On 4 November 2026 #aiwalkingeffect held 7,299 videos and 107.6 million views, #aiwalkeffect 2,200 videos and 7.8 million views. AI video apps now offer street-style walk presets built on fashion creators' real reels: the City walk and Catwalk versions here copy two of those reels.
