## Why the flip looks like a swap

The trick of the shoulder carry trend is a camera angle, not strength. The lifter turns their back to the camera while the rider sits up on their shoulders. Now the rider's upper body hides the lifter's head, so the rider's face sits on top of the lifter's jacket and legs. That is why the comments say "he's facing the wrong way": it looks like a girl lifting her boyfriend with no effort. The AI version keeps this angle, so your result shows the same shoulder carry swap.
