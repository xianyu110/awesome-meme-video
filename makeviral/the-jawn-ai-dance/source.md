## What is the The Jawn Dance trend, and where did it start?

"The JAWN" is a 2026 dance track by Jerri; posts asking fans to pre-save it went up on 26 May 2026. "Jawn" is Philadelphia slang for almost anything, so the trend's name, "that's the jawn", means "that's the one".

The routine on this page comes from a comedy skit. On 16 July, Québec comedian Monika Pilon posted "On the dancefloor!": she stands in her backyard with a glass of orange juice, scratches her neck and waits, straight-faced, for the drop, then breaks into a grin and bounces. It has 18.7 million views and 1.0 million likes by 16 October, the funny lady original that the trend copies.

Then AI took over. From 10 August, CapCut AI templates turned the skit into a one-photo effect ("Join the viral 'That's the Jawn' trend! Upload your photo to make your funny AI dance clip"), and the template posts reached 7.6 million, 8.9 million and, on 30 September, 6.8 million views. People put in their grandparents, their partners and their pets; on 17 August an AI-made tabby cat stood on the same lawn with the glass of orange juice and did the whole routine (@scottbradley71), the reel this page's Pet copies. By 16 October 2026, #thatsthejawn had 8,079 TikTok videos and 26.9 million views, and #thejawndance 956 videos.
