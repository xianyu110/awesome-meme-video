## What is the 3D Month Letters trend, and where did it start?

The 3D month letters started in Indonesia as a ChatGPT photo trend. On 8 October 2026, @hnfnswnqa3 posted "Tren foto AI huruf 3D Oktober pakai ChatGPT" (the 3D October letters AI photo trend with ChatGPT): women in casual outfits lean on stacked pastel letters that spell OKTOBER. The post had 3.9 million views and 36,500 likes by 1 November. Its caption holds the prompt, and the later posts repeat it word for word.

In the comments, people asked for the next month ("bikin versi November dong", make a November version) and said they made it for their birth month. On 28 and 29 October, @lovinglylov3ly0 posted the November letters with the same caption: 687,400 and 727,800 views by 1 November. The posts promote a CapCut template, and the caption tells people to paste the prompt into ChatGPT.

The idea comes back every month, and the posts for a month start a few days before it begins. The month hashtags are big already: #hellodecember has 84,828 TikTok videos and #hellonovember 76,598 (1 November 2026).

### Which photo works best?

Use **one full-body photo of you alone**, from the head to the shoes, with your face clear and turned to the camera. The result is a full-body photo too, so a full-body picture keeps your face, your build and your outfit the most.

- **Wear what you want in the photo.** The prompt keeps your own casual outfit, shoes and bag when the photo shows them. The viral photos show jeans, cardigans, kimonos and hijabs.
- **Stand in good light.** Daylight from a window or outside, no strong shadow on the face. No sunglasses if you want your eyes to show.
- **A plain background helps,** but any background works: the template moves you to the studio.
- **One person per photo.** For the **Duo** version, add two photos: photo 1 stands on the left of the letters, photo 2 on the right.
- **Pet version:** one photo of your dog or cat with the whole body and the face in view.
- Use your own photo, or one of an adult friend who said yes.

## What goes wrong, and how to fix it

- **A letter is missing or doubled.** AI image models still misspell long words now and then. Look at the letters before you post, and run it again if one is wrong: each run is a little different.
- **The face looks a little off.** Use a sharper photo with the face turned to the camera and more light. A tiny face in a far-away photo is the most common cause.
- **The outfit changed.** When the photo does not show your whole outfit, the template dresses you in a relaxed casual outfit. Use a full-body photo to keep yours.
- **You want another month, or your name.** Only December, November and January are here for now. For other words, copy the prompt into AI Photo and change the letters.
