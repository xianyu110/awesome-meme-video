## What is the Pick It Up Dance trend, and where did it start?

The Pick It Up Make It Jump dance runs on a beat that DJ Zackkk, a Dade County, Florida DJ, posted on TikTok on 23 April 2026 as "original sound - djzackkk" with the title "PICK IT UP TECO" on screen. The routine came months later: on 19 September 2026 a dancer who goes by lilcoco posted it with the caption "dc: me", dance credit: me, and other dancers soon credited "dc: @Lilcoco".

In October it became one of the big school and hallway dances on TikTok. @overlyyocean's solo take outdoors at night on 3 October passed 716,000 views, a duo take on 7 October reached 2.7 million, and a solo take filmed in an office reached 3.9 million views on 26 October. Captions quote the line "pick it up, make it jump, put yo hands up". By 30 October the sound was in about 14,000 videos.
