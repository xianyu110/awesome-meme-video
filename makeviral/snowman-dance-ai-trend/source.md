## What is the Snowman Dance trend, and where did it start?

Couples started to dance to the chorus of Sia's "Snowman" on snowy squares in Russia in the winter of 2020 to 2021. One of the earliest clips, by @prostokom1 on 21 January 2021, calls it a waltz, and some people still call it the snowman vals. The song was already all over TikTok because of the singing Snowman challenge of December 2020, where you sing the chorus in one breath.

The dance went big two years later. On 3 November 2022 Sia's own account posted a couple dancing it in the snow, filmed by @alexander.hill: 25.8 million views. On 16 March 2023 the Norwegian creator @mariajuterud danced it in a green gown in the falling snow, and the clip reached 27.4 million views and 4.1 million likes.

In December 2025 the snowman dance trend moved indoors: the couple @realjayllnn did it by the Christmas tree in matching red pants and finished with a jump onto the partner's back. On 8 November 2026 @nataliireynoldss posted her "mine forever" pajama version, which had 38.8 million views and 2.9 million likes by 13 November and opened this season's wave.
