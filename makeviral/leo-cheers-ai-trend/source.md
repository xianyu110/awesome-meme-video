## What is the Leo Cheers trend, and where did it start?

The toast comes from Baz Luhrmann's film **The Great Gatsby**. Leonardo DiCaprio plays Jay Gatsby, the host of huge parties nobody has met. When he finally appears, he turns to the camera, smiles and lifts a champagne coupe while fireworks burst and the music swells. Know Your Meme says the second trailer showed the shot on 20 December 2012, 43 seconds in, and the film opened in May 2013.
