## What is the Laughing Fairy AI trend, and where did it start?

The laughing fairies come from the TikTok account **@fae.eyes** (Fae Nest), which posts AI clips made to look like found footage: tiny, wrinkly fairies with long ears and see-through wings, caught at night in a phone's flash. Its earliest post we found, from 1 October 2026, shows one fairy on a leaf and has 1.2 million views.

On 5 October two gossip clips went viral. "I wonder who theyre talking about" came first, at 02:35 UTC, and has 5.1 million views. "I really wish i knew what they were saying" followed that evening: a whisper in the ear, a gasp, then both fairies giggle. It has 8.5 million views and 1.1 million likes. Fans wrote the babble down as "ñilili, prilili, ñaca" and posted the clips as "me and my bestie" memes, so the fairy meme spread in Spanish and Portuguese too. From 9 October the creator calls the main fairy **Prilili** and tells a fairy soap opera. A Brazilian reupload has about 13 million views, and people keep asking for a fairy version of themselves and their friends. That is what this template makes.
