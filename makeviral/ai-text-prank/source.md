The format grew out of AI photo pranks on parents. In December 2025, @joshaipranks texted his dad a photo of a cooking pot edited to hold his koi fish in boiling water (2.9M views). In 2026 the jokes moved to harmless home "upgrades" texted as a text prank. @_lillyverysilly's fake rain on fresh concrete, sent to her boss on 17 August, reached 23.6M views. ### Which photo works best?

Use **one real photo you took yourself**, so your dad knows the place at once. The edit keeps the house, the yard, the angle and the light, and adds only the joke.

- **Christmas house:** the front of the house from the street or the driveway, the whole roof in view.
- **Waterslide:** the backyard from a window or the patio, with the pool if you have one. No pool works too: you get one.
- **Storm:** one adult outside the house, the whole body in view, on the path, the lawn or the street. Only someone who is in on the joke or will laugh about it.
- **Doghouse:** your pet in the yard, near its doghouse, kennel or bed.

A normal phone photo in daylight looks most real. Avoid screenshots, filters and very dark photos.
