### Which photo works best?

Use **one clear photo of you** from the chest up or the waist up, with your face easy to see. The AI reads everything in it to pick the answers: your face and expression, your hair, your clothes, the colors and the place.

- **Pick a photo with a mood.** A golden-hour selfie, a rainy window or a night out gives clearer answers than a plain passport-style shot.
- **Keep it sharp and well lit.** Your photo sits in the middle of the board, so a blurry photo stays blurry.
- **Just you.** One person per photo; the answers are about that one person.
- **Vertical or square works.** The photo is cropped to fit a large frame in the middle, with your face kept in view.
- **For the pet version,** use one photo of your pet with its head and body in view.
