## What is the Popstar AI trend, and where did it start?

The trend copies one shot of Popstar, a 2020 rap music video in which a pop star lip-syncs a rapper's verse while he lives the star life in a mansion: a champagne bottle in his hand and a bald, bearded bodyguard at his shoulder. The video won Best Video at the 2020 MTV Europe Music Awards.

Six years later, Popstar music video AI remakes opened the role to everyone. The earliest AI version we found on TikTok went up on 30 September 2026. On 2 October a Brazilian football star posted an AI version with himself in the lead, made by the AI creator @downey.ai with Higgsfield Genjutsu. Sports accounts reposted it the same evening, and one repost had 688,300 views by 13 October.

Copies made with Higgsfield Genjutsu, Genematic, CapCut and Dreamina followed from 4 October. On 10 October a big rap news account on TikTok shared a version that puts the rapper of the verse in the role, with 324,600 views and 25,600 likes by 13 October. That day, US Google searches for "popstar video" hit their highest point of the past 30 days.
