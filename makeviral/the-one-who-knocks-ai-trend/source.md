## What is the The One Who Knocks trend, and where did it start?

The line comes from "Cornered", season 4, episode 6 of the TV drama Breaking Bad, which aired on AMC on 21 August 2011. In a dim bedroom, a quiet man tells the person on the bed that he is not in danger, he is the danger, and ends with "I am the one who knocks". A YouTube user posted the clip the next day, and the speech became a meme. Know Your Meme added its entry "I Am the One Who Knocks" in 2015.

On TikTok the scene never went away. On 3 November 2026, #iamtheonewhoknocks had 3,930 videos and 119.8 million views, #iamthedanger 100.7 million and #theonewhoknocks 71.6 million. Edits of the scene still pass a million views: one posted on 28 June 2026 has 1 million.

The AI wave is new. On 31 October 2026 an AI video site published a guide to a one who knocks AI trend, and by 3 November three AI video sites had a template for it. No AI version has gone viral yet. Ours is a new scene with your own face: it never uses the TV clip, its sound or its actor.
