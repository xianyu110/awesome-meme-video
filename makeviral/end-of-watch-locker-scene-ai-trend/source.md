## What is the End of Watch Locker trend, and where did it start?

*End of Watch* (2012) is a found-footage police film. In its locker room scene, Jake Gyllenhaal's officer films himself with a small camera and shows his gear to the lens, one piece at a time, while his partner sits beside him.

The meme took off on TikTok in August 2023 with the sound "original sound - xylo", a clip of the rap song "Twirlanta" ("my stepper gon' brush him, if I press the button"). People made their own edition at work: a gym edition by @wardsimons on 16 August 2023 (811,300 views), a fast-food edition on 18 August (633,800) and a Walmart edition on 20 August (724,200). The sound is in 66,100 videos.

The AI wave started in August 2026. A Kling AI template puts your face on the film shot, and tutorial accounts posted the Jake Gyllenhaal police locker scene from 7 August; the most viewed, on 9 August, has 132,700 views. Jake Gyllenhaal locker scene TikTok posts got their own tag, #lockerscenetrend, the same day and had 226 videos and 966,401 views on 2 November 2026. An End of Watch film edit passed 1.1 million views in October, and new AI versions of the locker scene kept coming.
