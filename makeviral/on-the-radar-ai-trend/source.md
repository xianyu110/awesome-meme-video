## What is the On The Radar Booth trend, and where did it start?

On The Radar is a hip-hop radio show whose booth clips are a TikTok staple: a small studio lit bright green, a glowing neon ring, headphones and standing mics. On 17 April 2026 the show posted a classic take of Fetty Wap and Monty rapping "679", the 2015 song, side by side in that booth. It had 19.5 million views by 18 October 2026.

The first AI wave of the On The Radar trend came on 28 April 2026: an AI cartoon character freestyling in a copy of the booth got 1.9 million views, and #ontheradarai started two days later (3,736 videos and 14.5 million views by 18 October). From 29 August the 679 clip became the template: app how-to videos showed how to swap two new faces into it, and the biggest one, posted on 5 September, has 2.0 million views. AI video sites made it a two-photo template from mid-September. On 16 September an AI influencer account posted its two AI-made idols doing the 679 routine, the clip at the top of this page.

### Which photo works best?

For **Duo**, upload two photos, one person in each: **photo 1 is you, on the left, and you rap first**; photo 2 is your friend on the right. For **Solo**, upload one photo.

- **From the waist or chest up, face sharp and well lit**, looking at the camera or a little to the side. The clip frames you from the waist up, so your face is large.
- **You keep your clothes from the photo.** A hoodie, a jacket or a chain fits the booth look.
- **No sunglasses, no hand over the face.** The video adds black studio headphones over your ears.
- **One person per photo.** In a group photo the AI can pick the wrong face.
- Use your own photo, or a photo of an adult friend who said yes. Never a celebrity, a stranger or a child.
