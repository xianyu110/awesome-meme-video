## What is the Oouuh Kitchen Duet trend, and where did it start?

"Oouuh" is a song by Fredo Bang, a rapper from Baton Rouge, released as a single in July 2018; people also write the title as Ouu, Ouuu or Ooouuu. The trend starts from the Fredo Bang ukulele clip of the same year: in a small kitchen, Fredo Bang sings the song a cappella while Einer Bankz, a ukulele player and producer from San Francisco known for playing behind rappers, strums along. Einer Bankz posted it on TikTok on 27 April 2022 and again on 21 February 2024 as "a timeless classic". By 11 October 2026 the two posts had 6.8 million and 20.1 million views.

The Fredo Bang AI trend took off at the start of October 2026. The earliest AI copies we found are from 30 September, and from 1 October CapCut template accounts posted versions with new faces swapped in, under the template label "Pioneer". The most viewed of them had 6.2 million views by 11 October, and the #ouuuhtrend tag, started on 1 October, had 1,357 videos. In most copies of the Oouuh meme, streamers and TV characters took the two places. On 6 October @trevorlarcom put himself and his friend Dillon into the kitchen instead.
