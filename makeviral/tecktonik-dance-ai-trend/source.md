## What is the Tecktonik Dance trend, and where did it start?

Tecktonik, also called electro dance, took shape at the Metropolis club in Rungis, south of Paris, where Cyril Blanc and Alexandre Barouzdin ran "Tecktonik Killer" nights from 2002. The arm-heavy style spread online and at the Paris Techno Parade in 2007, then faded after Metropolis ended those nights in late 2008.

In February 2026 the Russian dancer Sasha Cheba, @cheba_man on TikTok, began posting the old moves to 2000s electro hits. On 24 May 2026 he posted "я чеба мэн" ("I'm Cheba Man"): he dances on a small stage under string lights, in red light, to Benny Benassi's 2003 track "Love Is Gonna Save Us", while the crowd films him. By 12 October it had 17 million views and 1.9 million likes. Spinnin' Records shared him on 30 May, and US Google searches for "tecktonik dance" reached 2,400 a month in May and June, up from 320 in September 2025.

AI versions followed: on 14 June @ashanolas made her Abyssinian cat do the dance on the same kind of stage, and in October AI templates of the stage dance spread on CapCut and Higgsfield. Then Cheba Man took the dance on tour: on 13 October he posted it at night in front of the Eiffel Tower, "Cheba in Paris", with 1.1 million views two days later.
