## What is the Gang Gang Dance trend, and where did it start?

The moves come from a hip-hop dance battle. The Belgian freestyle dancer Doyah (@doyahtreize) dances a slow, confident round to a West Coast rap song with a "gang gang" hook: a low crouch with the arms reaching down, swings and leans, a long look up at the lights, and a finish with one hand on the hip and the other at the chin. Reposts of his battle rounds, like @equente.oficial's on 2 September 2026 with 1.3 million views, carried it around TikTok.

On 23 September @unsvng posted an AI version in which a Jean Phil-style character, the bob-haired AI Frenchman of that month, dances the round on a battle stage, with Doyah credited in the caption. By 14 October it had 43.8 million views and 3.9 million likes, its most liked comments said the AI had fooled them, and its sound was in 374 videos.

CapCut templates followed within days. On 27 September @thespazg7 put his own face into the battle with AI and started the #ganggangdancebattle tag, the take this page copies.
