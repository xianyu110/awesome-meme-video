## What is the Thanksgiving Bend trend, and where did it start?

The words are old. "Beans, greens, potatoes, tomatoes, lamb, rams, hogs, dogs ... you name it" comes from a live recording of the gospel singer Shirley Caesar, the spoken part of her song "Hold My Mule". In November 2016 a remix of that chant started the #UNameItChallenge, also called the U Name It Challenge, and people danced to it in their kitchens before Thanksgiving.

Ten years later it came back. On 5 November 2026 @djsaucedup, a DJ from Austin, Texas, posted a new dance edit of the chant with the line "Is that Thanksgiving I hear". His post had 351,000 views and 49,000 likes four days later. Dancers across the US picked it up at once and posted their own routines as a "Thanksgiving bend", most with "dc: me" (their own dance credit), so there is no single set routine. By 9 November the sound was in 641 TikTok videos, with about 32 new ones a day.
