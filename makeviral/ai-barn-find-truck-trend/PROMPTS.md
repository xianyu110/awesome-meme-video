### 1. Prompt 1

*Kind: prompt*

```text
Use the uploaded image as the base. Put my truck in an old barn with spiderwebs: my truck is the vehicle in the uploaded image, whatever kind it is (a pickup, a car, an SUV, a van or a tractor). Restage the exact vehicle from the uploaded image as a barn find, 20 years from now: parked in a dusty wooden barn under a thick layer of dust, warm sunbeams cutting through gaps in the wooden slats, straw on the floor, abandoned-Americana aesthetic, fine film grain. The vehicle's body, wheels and paint remain recognizable beneath the dust. Documentary photography. It has sat untouched for 20 years: thick grey-brown dust and dried dirt on the hood, the roof and the windows, so the glass is cloudy; dusty spiderwebs hanging from the mirrors, the wheels, the bumpers and the roof beams; dry leaves and loose straw on the hood and the roof; faded paint with a few small rust spots; cloudy headlights; tires a little flat, sunk into the dirt floor. It is still clearly the same vehicle as in the photo, so the owner knows it at once: the same body shape and size, cab, bed or trunk, doors, paint color under the dust, grille shape, headlights, wheels and tires, ride height or lift, bumpers, racks, light bars and every other part it has. Never a different or older model, never restored or shiny. The barn: weathered vertical wooden boards with thin gaps of daylight, heavy roof beams with a few broken boards and holes in the roof, a hay bale, an old rusty barrel and an old wagon wheel at the sides, dust floating in the beams of light, warm brown tones and deep shadows. The vehicle stands in the middle of the barn in a front three-quarter view, the whole vehicle in frame from the roof to the tires, in the lower half of the picture, with the camera at headlight height and the roof beams and the light above it. Photorealistic, a real photo, not CGI, not a painting. Vertical 9:16. No people and no human faces anywhere in the picture. No badges, emblems or lettering on the vehicle and no readable number plate, even if the photo shows them. No text, no captions, no logos, no watermark.
```
