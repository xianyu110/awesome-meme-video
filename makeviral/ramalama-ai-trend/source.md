## What is the Ramalama trend, and where did it start?

"Ramalama (Bang Bang)" is a track on *Ruby Blue*, the first solo album by Róisín Murphy, released on 13 June 2005 and produced by Murphy and Matthew Herbert. In 2006 it soundtracked a group routine on *So You Think You Can Dance*.

The Ramalama TikTok trend started in the summer of 2025. By October 2025 the Ramalama Bang Bang meme had become a costume reveal: the phone sits low and sees only legs and shoes while you stalk toward it, then the camera tilts up on the beat and shows the whole costume. Murphy posted her own video on 20 October 2025, and the costumes peaked around Halloween. US Google searches for "ramalama" reached 18,100 in November 2025.

The walk came back at the end of September 2026. New Engen listed the Ramalama Costume Walk among the October 2026 TikTok trends, and by 11 October the sound had 446,100 videos. ## How do people film the Ramalama Bang Bang walk themselves?

The human version of the Ramalama walking trend needs no AI, only a costume and a phone. The steps most creators follow:

1. Prop the phone low, so it sees only your legs and shoes.
2. Walk slowly toward it in time with the song. Drag your feet, or a broom or a bat, for a creepy look.
3. On the beat, tilt the camera up, or step back, and show the whole costume.
4. Film somewhere spooky: a garage, a stairwell, a dark hallway or a street at night.
5. Skip text and talking. The reveal is the joke.

For more than one costume, like the AI kitten reel, film the same walk in each costume from the same spot, then cut the clips together on the beats.
