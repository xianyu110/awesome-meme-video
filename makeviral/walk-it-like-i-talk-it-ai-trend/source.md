## What is the Walk It Like I Talk It trend, and where did it start?

The clip behind the trend is Migos' ride on Carpool Karaoke, the car segment of The Late Late Show on CBS, which aired on 13 November 2018. The group opened the segment rapping the "walk it like I talk it" hook with the host at the wheel, and the four seats, the head bops and the quick cuts between them became the format.

In September 2026, creators started to swap other faces into the same 20 seconds with AI video tools such as Higgsfield Genjutsu, keeping every move, cut and mouth shape. Early versions put famous athletes in the seats: a UFC version on TikTok from 20 September had 70,300 views by 14 October. On 24 September, Lewis Hamilton shared an AI version with four Formula 1 drivers, made by Instagram creator @dan.directed, on his Instagram story, and the motorsport press picked it up.

By October the trend had moved on to sports teams, friend groups, AI-made characters and pets. The biggest TikTok version we found, from 11 October, had 545,500 views and 83,700 likes three days later.
