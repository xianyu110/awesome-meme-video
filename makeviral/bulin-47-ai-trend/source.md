## What is the Bulin 47 Freestyle trend, and where did it start?

On 2 October 2026 the freestyler Kevin Wai, known as Kevincito (@kevincitooficial11 on TikTok), walked up to the Dominican dembow artist Bulin 47 outside a restaurant in Spain with a portable speaker playing a dembow beat. Bulin 47 answered with an improvised freestyle while people around them filmed on their phones. Many TikTok captions place the clip in Madrid. Kevin Wai's TikTok had 4.1 million views and 506,500 likes by 10 October. Listín Diario counted more than 50 million plays across platforms on 7 October, and El Nuevo Diario reported more than 65 million views on Instagram a day later.

The AI version followed within days. From 6 October, people used AI video tools to swap the rapper for someone else, while the speaker, the crowd, the red light and the camera moves stayed. The best-known swaps used football stars and politicians; others used the creators' own faces, cartoon characters and animals. TikTok's #bulin47freestyle tag, started on 7 October, had 1,821 videos and 24.2 million views by 10 October. The two versions on this page copy two of those AI reels: a creator's own-face version and the Bulin 47 cat version.

The clip now has a second name. "Dame hombro" ("give me shoulder") is the line people took from the freestyle, and from mid-October more and more people search for a Dame Hombro AI video. Spanish posts write "Bulin 47 IA" or "Bulin 47 improvisado IA", and searches also spell the name Bullin 47, Bulim 47 or Bulin47.
