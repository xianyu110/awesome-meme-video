## What is the Baby With Mustache trend, and where did it start?

The clip shows a swaddled newborn on a blue hospital pad, blinking up at the camera with a thick, dark mustache and a little goatee, so some posts call it a baby born with beard. It is also spelled moustache: baby born with moustache, newborn baby with moustache. It was on TikTok by 23 October 2026, and one repost from that day had 2.7 million views a week later. On 24 October Complex shared it as a baby in Ecuador apparently born with a full, bushy mustache: 5.1 million plays and 360,000 likes. By 26 October, Karmactive counted about 50 million views in total.

The question everyone asked was real or fake. On 24 October the Brazilian fact-check site Boatos.org said the facial hair was added with an AI filter, because the mustache shifts and vanishes in some frames. Then the jokes turned into a trend. AI accounts made their own mustache baby videos the same day, and a "Part 2" with another mustached newborn reached 1.44 million views by 30 October. ## Is the baby born with mustache real or fake?

The viral clip is very likely fake. On 24 October 2026 Boatos.org reported that the facial hair was added with an AI filter: the mustache shifts on the face and vanishes in some frames. Karmactive wrote on 26 October that fact-checkers found signs of digital alteration, and that a newborn cannot grow a thick adult mustache. Nobody has confirmed who the baby is or where the video was filmed, and nobody behind the clip has said which tool made it.

Newborns can have fine, soft body hair, called lanugo, and some have a little dark fuzz above the lip. It usually falls out in the first weeks. A thick, dark, grown-up mustache like the one in the meme is not something babies are born with. For a question about your own baby's hair, ask your pediatrician.
