### 1. Prompt 1

*Kind: prompt*

```text
Using the uploaded reference photo, create an ultra-realistic, high-fashion editorial beauty portrait that preserves the person's authentic facial features, natural hairstyle, age, skin tone, and eye color. THE CONCEPT: Create a stunning black-and-white portrait with two pops of color: the person's own eyes, and an oversized, luxurious chunky-knit sweater in the same color as the irises in the uploaded photo: a rich, saturated version of that real eye color. THE EYES: The irises keep the exact eye color of the uploaded photo, the same hue and the same darkness: dark brown stays dark brown, light brown stays light brown, hazel stays hazel, green stays green, blue stays blue. They are in natural color, clearly visible, with catchlights, and they are the only color in the face. Never gray, white, silver or washed-out irises, and never a lighter, brighter or new eye color. The sweater should have extra-long, thick, cozy sleeves. Pose the person with both hands raised toward the face, the hands tucked inside the long cuffs, allowing the oversized sleeves to cover the mouth and lower face, leaving the eyes beautifully visible. The top edge of the cuffs rests just below the nose, and only the mouth and chin are behind the knit. The cuffs never touch the nose; the nostrils and the nose tip are always in view. THE EXPRESSION: The person's eyes are the STAR of this portrait! The person is smiling warmly behind the sleeves, and the smile shows in the eyes: the cheeks lift, the lower eyelids rise a little and the outer corners of the eyes crinkle softly. Relaxed eyebrows, playful, flirty, innocent or mischievous, always warm and happy. Never a wide-eyed, startled, scared, sad, tense or blank stare. THE PHOTOGRAPHY: Elegant black-and-white editorial photography. ONLY the sweater and the irises remain in color. Soft, flattering beauty lighting from the front with gentle shadows. A simple, softly blurred grayscale background. Close-up composition emphasizing the eyes and the sweater's rich knitted texture. IMPORTANT DETAILS: Maintain the person's exact facial identity and natural hairstyle. Do not change the hair length, eye color, facial structure, or age. Glasses stay on when the photo shows them. No hat or cap. No exaggerated features, distorted hands, or unnecessary accessories. The finished portrait should feel cozy, warm and effortlessly beautiful. Vertical 9:16 close-up portrait. Exactly one person. No text, no letters, no logo, no watermark.
```
