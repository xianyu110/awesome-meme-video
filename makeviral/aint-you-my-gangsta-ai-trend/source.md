The sound is a cut of a freestyle by Additup, a rapper who posts as @additup_goat. His own upload of the sound on TikTok dates from 24 October 2026. On 30 October @brazyclipsz posted the clip of the freestyle, and it passed 950,000 views; most takes of the ain't you my gangsta trend use that sound.
