## What is the I'm Not Yo B No More trend, and where did it start?

The song is "Not Yours" by Lady Binladen. Its hook is a swear word, so people write it "I'm not yo b no more" or "I'm not his b no more". The Drill Hype account posted it on TikTok on 30 May 2026, and two June posts with the sound now have 18.1 million and 11.9 million views.

The dance came in the fall. On 26 September the Milwaukee creator Steve Glover (@thastona on TikTok) danced to his own cut of the song at a house party, and on 4 October "Going crazy in the club" reached 13.5 million views by 26 October. That club clip is the Steve Glover dance people copy: both fists up beside the head, stomping knee lifts in a wide stance, low lunges like a fencer, the arms flung wide.

On 13 October the AI account @dalefromaccounting23 posted an AI-made office worker doing the club dance move for move in front of his coworkers: 2.2 million views, and the I'm Not Yo B No More AI trend began. Phone-editor AI templates and US tutorials followed. Almost every post of the I'm Not Yo B No More trend is from the US.
