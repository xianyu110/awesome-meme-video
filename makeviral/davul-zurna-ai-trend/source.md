## What is the Davul Zurna Dance trend, and where did it start?

The clip is real. Muharrem Usta, a stove maker who films davul and zurna sessions in his workshop in Ankara, posted it on 29 October 2026 as "#köprününaltı" ("under the bridge", a line of the folk song he sings). He dances in the middle, the zurna on his left and the big davul drum on his right, walks up to the phone, sings straight into it, raises an arm and walks back with both arms up. That is the **köprünün altı davul zurna** clip.

The AI wave came a week later. On 5 November @sinandoqu put the faces of world leaders on the three men and posted it as "Liderler tek bir konuda anlaştı: Davul Zurna" ("The leaders agreed on one thing: davul zurna"), with Kim Jong Un as the dancer. It passed 763,000 views on TikTok and 2.3 million plays on Instagram, where the caption credits Muharrem Usta as the source. People started calling it the **davul zurna akımı**, and from 10 November AI video apps advertised the same scene in Turkey with users' own faces.
