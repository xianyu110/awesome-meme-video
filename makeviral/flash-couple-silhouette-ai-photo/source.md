The look took off in Turkey in November 2026 as TikTok photo posts: one picture, no caption on the image, and the same sound, "orijinal ses" by the account SONYAZ, a romantic edit sound from October 2025. ### Which photo works best?

**Pick the version, then the photos.**

- **Couple:** photo 1 is you, photo 2 is your partner. You end up on the left, your partner on the right, close together.
- **Couple photo:** one photo of you two together. The pose and the framing of your photo stay; only the background and the light change. This is how the original template works.
- **You + pet:** photo 1 is you, photo 2 is your cat or dog.
- Use sharp photos with the **face in full view**, from the chest up or closer. Daylight or a bright room works best: the result dims the faces, so they need detail to start from.
- Hair matters: the rim light draws it. Loose hair and curls look best.
- No sunglasses, no heavy filter, no text or stickers on the photo.
- Only adults who said yes, or your own pet.
