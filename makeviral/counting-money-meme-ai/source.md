## What is the Counting Money Meme trend, and where did it start?

Counting money has been a reaction meme for years: a boss counting money, a rich man counting money, the man counting money meme with a thick stack, or a cartoon cat counting cash, posted as a counting money GIF whenever someone gets paid, wins a bet or acts rich. In the US, "counting money meme" gets about 590 Google searches a month and "counting money GIF" about 1,900 (Google Ads averages to September 2026).

In November 2026 it became the counting money trend on TikTok, made with AI. On 1 November a Mexican TikTok account posted a CapCut template under the caption "¡Cuenta billetes como un gran jefe!" ("count bills like a big boss!"): you upload one photo, and the template turns you into someone counting stacks of cash. The post passed 946,000 views, 29,300 likes and 11,600 shares, and its comments filled with people saying the money is fake, which is the joke. An English copy followed on 4 November ("count stacks of money like a real boss") and had 207,000 views two days later. Spanish posts call it contando billetes or contando dinero; English ones call it the boss counting money template.
