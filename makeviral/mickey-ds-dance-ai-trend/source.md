## What is the Mickey D's Dance trend, and where did it start?

The chant is older than the dance wave. Its TikTok sound, an "original sound" by the account takii, was created on 25 April 2018, and YouTube compilations of a "Mickey Mickey D challenge" (#mickeylikejhacari) went up on 12 March 2018. "Mickey D's" is American slang for McDonald's, and the words are a drive-through order: large number 3, don't forget the fries.

In October 2026 the sound came back as a two-person dance, the Mickey D's dance trend. A duo clip filmed in a US school hallway on 14 October reached 4.5 million views, and students, friends, teachers and families copied it in hallways, kitchens and parking lots. On 22 October the TikTok account @obengaffect posted two soldiers in uniform doing the routine in their barracks room; it had 160,700 views by 31 October.

By 31 October the sound was in 168,500 TikTok videos, and #mickeyds held 7,158 videos and 130.5 million views. From 19 October, CapCut posts promoted an AI template that makes a baby do the dance; one of them had 176,900 views by 31 October.
