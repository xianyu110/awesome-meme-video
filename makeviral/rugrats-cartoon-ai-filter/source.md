## What is the Rugrats Filter trend, and where did it start?

The Rugrats cartoon trend runs on a CapCut AI template, the kind people call the Rugrats AI Cartoon Transformation. You add a photo, the photo wipes away, and a cartoon version of the same picture appears in a pastel 90s playroom with alphabet blocks and toys on the rug. The template's sound, an "original sound" by Xochitl Ramire, was first posted on 2 October 2026 and was in 1,488 TikTok videos by 1 November. One of the first posts with real reach was @yoyo.559's group of friends in Fresno on 9 October (2,008 views), the clip at the top of this page.

Through October, people used it on group photos, couples and family pictures, and the most viewed post on the sound reached 17,400 views on 29 October. Rugrats was everywhere that Halloween too: a salon's Rugrats costume post had 329,000 views on 30 October. On 31 October a wave of US tutorial accounts started posting "how to do the rugrats cartoon trend", and Spanish posts call it the tendencia de caricatura IA Rugrats. The name is older: in 2024 a TikTok baby-face effect also went around as the Rugrats filter.

### Which photo works best?

Upload **one sharp photo**: for **Group**, one group or family photo with 2 to 6 people; for **Just you**, a photo of you alone, waist up or full body; for **Pet**, your pet with its whole body in view.

- **Everyone's face in view, in good light.** The cartoon keeps what it can see: hairstyles, beards, glasses, hats, tattoos and jewelry.
- **Pose like you want the cartoon to pose.** The filter keeps each pose and gesture, so a piggyback, a peace sign or a drink in the hand comes through.
- **Wear what you want your cartoon to wear.** Clothes keep their colors and prints.
- **Family photos:** your own kids are fine in your own photo. Nobody's face should be cut off at the edge.
- Use your own photos, or photos of friends who said yes. Never a celebrity or a stranger.
