## What is the Hole in Shirt trend, and where did it start?

The hole in shirt effect started as a CapCut template, not an AI tool. The template, made by the CapCut user BABLO, takes one photo twice: it keeps your photo as it is, cuts a round window with a white rim into the chest of your shirt, puts the same photo inside the window, smaller, and lets it spin slowly. A tilted light-blue neon ring circles the window and small sparkles fall. Its sample clip, a smiling man in a white sleeveless top, went up on TikTok from Burkina Faso in mid-October 2026.

On 18 October 2026 the TikTok account @yr5sdowfg6 posted a CapCut how-to for it, with captions in English, French and Amharic. It reached about 363,000 views and 66,000 likes, and on screen the template had about 35,700 uses. From 19 October, hundreds of users, most of them in Burkina Faso, posted their own photo with the hole in their shirt on the how-to's sound. By 21 October that sound had about 1,000 videos and gained about 600 a day.
