## What is the Mom Into Me trend, and where did it start?

The trend started on TikTok in December 2022. As Know Your Meme puts it, a mom catwalks into the room in her normal clothes, walks out, and walks back in wearing the kind of clothes her daughter wears. The sound is a snippet of Skee-Lo's 1995 song "I Wish". On 14 December 2022 @jasminfogeddd dressed her dad in her brother's clothes to the same sound, and on 15 December @malindahlgren0 posted the first known "turning my mom into me" video from Norway. It passed 4 million views in a week.

The next day @pedersenmalene's version took off (15.6 million views), and in the weeks after it the reels with the most views came from the US and Australia. @nadinebloch1's reel of 22 December has 42.4 million views, and @mdogzzz's of 2 January 2023 has 66.4 million. The same sound now sits under 123,600 TikTok videos. Dads, grandmas and boyfriends got their own takes, also called "transforming my mom into me" and, in the UK and Australia, "turning my mum into me".

It never quite stopped: on 10 January 2026 @domciagg's mom reached 11.8 million views, and in October 2026 the Spanish site Qué! wrote that the trend was sweeping TikTok again.
