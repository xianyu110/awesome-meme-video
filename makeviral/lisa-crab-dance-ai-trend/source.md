## What is the LISA Crab Dance trend, and where did it start?

The crab dance comes from Thailand. It goes with "ปูหนีบอีปิ" ("Poo Neep E-Pi", roughly "the crab pinched E-Pi"), a Thai country song by พร จันทพร, spelled Pon Jantapon by fans, released in 2017. LISA of BLACKPINK, who is Thai, taught it on the Korean variety show "Knowing Brothers" in the episode that aired on 17 October 2020. Her straight face while her hands snap like claws made the Lisa crab dance Knowing Brothers clip a hit: it passed 100 million YouTube views in February 2022.

On 11 November 2024 LISA did the crab dance again for fans at her Singapore fan meetup. A fan clip posted by @haickz the next day has 15.4 million views and 1.2 million likes. Dancers kept the Lisa crab dance challenge going on TikTok, and AI versions followed: an AI dog in a pink skirt by @mynamepopcorn (10 December 2025, 810,200 views) and three AI cats in tutus by @peta.house (25 February 2026, 1.4 million views). On 12 November 2026 two AI video sites added a crab dance AI template. By 13 November 2026, #lisacrabdance had 4,029 TikTok videos and 61.8 million views, and the Thai song tag #ปูหนีบอีปิ 37,781 videos and 267.7 million views.
