## What is the Crochet Filter trend, and where did it start?

The crochet filter is not a camera filter but an AI prompt. You give an AI image tool one photo, and it gives back the same picture as if you were an amigurumi doll, the small stuffed crochet doll made by hand from wool and yarn. Your face becomes a round yarn face with big doll eyes, your hair becomes thick strands of yarn, and your clothes, the floor, the plants and even your phone come out stitched.

The wave started in Colombia. On 14 October 2026 @adriana_official86 posted 15 crochet pictures of herself and her partner, made with ChatGPT. By 17 October the post had 244,400 views and 10,200 likes, and under it she pasted the exact text she used, again and again, for everyone who asked. From 15 October, Spanish how-to videos called the look fotos tejidas (knitted photos), filtro tejido or muñeco tejido, and on 16 October English how-to videos named it the crochet filter and the amigurumi filter. The trend is still small and early: its biggest posts have tens of thousands of views. A first knitted-photo wave, a wool texture photo filter, went around in Spanish in December 2025.

The trend did not start as an app effect. @adriana_official86 made her crochet doll pictures in ChatGPT with one short Spanish prompt, and pasted it under her post for everyone who asked "pasa el texto" (send the text). In English it says:

> Turn this photo into an image in a knitted crochet style, as if the person were a doll made completely by hand with wool and yarn. Keep the facial features, the skin tone, the hair, the clothes, the pose and the background of the original photo, but make everything have a knitted texture, with visible stitches and realistic amigurumi details. Make it look sweet, pretty and very detailed, without losing the essence of the original photo.

MakeViral uses that prompt with its words and order kept. We only add what the model needs: a vertical picture, one doll for each person, logos turned into plain fabric, and a description of the doll face that the trend's pictures show, so that the doll still looks like you.
