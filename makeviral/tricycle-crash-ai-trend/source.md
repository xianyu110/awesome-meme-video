## What is the Tricycle Crash trend, and where did it start?

The trend is an AI template, not a real stunt. Each clip is about 4 to 5 seconds, and most posts string three or four of them together, then show how to make one. The captions run in Spanish, English and French: "efecto divertido triciclo mini choca contra árbol en pendiente", "Funny effect: a mini tricycle crashes into a tree on a slope".
