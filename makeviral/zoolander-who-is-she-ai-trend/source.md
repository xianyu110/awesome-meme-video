## What is the Zoolander Stare trend, and where did it start?

The stare comes from the red carpet scene of *Zoolander* (2001), in which two rival male models spot each other at a fashion awards night. On TikTok it became the **who is she meme** in December 2022, set to the song creators name as "Who Is She?" by I Monster: a post by @volcogot on 18 December 2022 has 9.7 million views and 1.1 million likes. The #zoolandermeme tag has 15,932 videos and 1.3 billion views.

The AI version started in 2026. On 28 February the Turkish account @yapayzekaserisi posted the scene with two football stars in it (3.4 million views), and on 10 March a Coca-Cola bottle and a Pepsi can as the two models (18.9 million views, 1.5 million likes, 531,400 shares). Tutorials on how to make the Zoolander AI meme followed from 13 March. The meme is still alive: a version with two video game characters passed 679,000 views in September 2026, and an AI video app added the red carpet duel as a preset on 11 November 2026.
