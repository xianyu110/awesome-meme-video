## What is the Flower in Nose trend, and where did it start?

The trick is simple: a short flower stem hidden in one nostril, a puzzled close-up, then a little daisy pulled out and offered to the camera, set to the line "I hope he buys you flowers" from Bruno Mars' 2012 song "When I Was Your Man". The joke is that you do not wait for him: you grow the flowers yourself.

The earliest popular reel we found is by the Irish creator @darraghomurchu, posted on 5 May 2026 with the caption "wtf is that in my nose". It had 806.1K views on 29 October, and it is the reel the Human version copies. The flower out of nose joke became the flower nose trend of May: a Turkish version from 27 May, captioned "burundan çiçek akımı" (the flower from the nose trend), passed 4.9 million views, and a couple's version from 31 May passed 2.1 million.

In September a CapCut AI effect made the nose flower trend an AI trend in Asia (in Vietnam it is the "trend hoa trong mũi"), and on 26 October the first AI pet version appeared: a white Pomeranian that pulls a daisy out of her nose. The pet wave is still small.
