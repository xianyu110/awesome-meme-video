## Is it legal to dar grau? A word on the stunt

Grau is a real stunt, and on public roads it is against the law in Brazil. Article 244 of the Brazilian traffic code (Código de Trânsito Brasileiro), which riders call the artigo do grau, treats riding on one wheel as a very serious offense: Motor Show listed a fine of R$ 293.47 and a suspended license in 2022. That is why grau de moto 244 and "grau de moto é crime" are such common searches. Cities like Belo Horizonte set aside closed spaces for it.
