## What is the Devil Wears Prada AI trend, and where did it start?

The Devil Wears Prada trend grew out of one sound. On 22 September 2026, KANA DJ (@kaannnaa) posted "Prada Prada Prada", a club mashup of Kai Angel's "andy warhol" and Jakarta's "One Desire" that repeats the line "Prada, Prada, Prada". The editor @nopowerbaank turned it into the transition on 1 October: a lip-sync at home, the background drops away on the beat, then a cut to a second look. That clip passed 2.2 million views, her 3 October and 8 October clips passed 4.8 and 4.7 million, and template sellers began to call it the "nopowerbaank trend".

The name comes from the sound and the 2006 film The Devil Wears Prada (its sequel came out in 2026), but the clips use no film footage. From 8 October, AI versions made from one photo appeared, and on 11 October a version with white doors and a black leather jacket on a night street passed 3 million views. By 13 October a trend site counted 24,900 videos on the sound. The trend lives mostly on Russian-speaking TikTok as «Дьявол носит Прада»; the English Devil Wears Prada AI trend and the AI tutorials followed from 12 October.
