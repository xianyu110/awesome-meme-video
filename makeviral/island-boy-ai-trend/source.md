## What is the Island Boy trend, and where did it start?

The original is a freestyle by the Florida twins Flyysoulja and Kodiyakredd, who call themselves the Island Boys. In October 2021 a clip of them rapping "I'm an island boy" went viral, first on Twitter and then on TikTok, and it reached 9 million views before the post was deleted. A produced version with a music video followed that November. People still search for the island boy pool freestyle, the island boy song and the "im an island boy" line, and #islandboys holds almost 187,000 TikTok videos.

That walking dead island boy clip is the one this template copies. The same day the account posted four more casts of the same pool, and by 9 November oakgen, Dreamina and media.io had island boy AI video templates of it. We saw no repost with millions of views yet, so treat it as an early trend.
