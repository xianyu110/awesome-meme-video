## What is the Single Camel trend, and where did it start?

It started with a song. On 12 October 2026 a Sindhi singer posted a sad, funny song about still being unmarried ("kunwaro", also spelled kuwaro or kunwara), and people in Sindh, Pakistan, began to lip-sync it two days later.

Then AI creators gave it a face, and the camel AI video was born. On 16 October @aiduniya97 posted an AI camel wrapped in a shawl on a rope cot, "koaro aa" (260,800 views by 7 November), and Sindhi AI pages followed with camel comedy clips on the same sound: the AI camel video became its own Sindhi AI video genre, with names like the kunwaro camel and Oont ki shadi (the camel's wedding). The one that broke out came on 31 October: @aicarton9876's AI camel in a torn gray coat walks alone through a foggy village while couples sit close by the fires. It passed 1.5 million views and 158,000 likes, and the #singlecamel hashtag was created the day before.

By 7 November the sound held 11,900 videos. Other creators made the camel a man with a camel head in a shalwar kameez, alone on his rope cot at night while camel couples sleep, with titles like Single Oont and Kunwara Oont (oont is the Urdu word for camel). The comments are full of people tagging their single friends.
