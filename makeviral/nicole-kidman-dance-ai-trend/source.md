Its hook, "baby girl in control, I feel like Nicole Kidman", took off on TikTok right away. By 24 September Billboard counted more than 1.5 million TikTok uses and a viral dance trend, and the song jumped from No. 45 to No. 22 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated 26 September.

The dance is a short routine to the hook. TikTok tutorials credit the choreography to the dancer Kyler Irby; one slowed tutorial from 21 September had 944,900 views by 11 October. On 6 October Nicole Kidman posted her own TikTok to the song from Chanel's show in Paris, with Dua Lipa, Pedro Pascal and Kristen Wiig. It had 32.6 million views five days later.
