### Which photo works best?

Upload **one sharp photo**: for **Human**, a photo of you alone, waist up or full body if you have one; for **Friends**, one group photo with 2 to 4 people; for **Pet**, your pet with its whole body in view.

- **Face the camera in good light.** The cartoon keeps what it can see: your hairstyle, beard, glasses, piercings and tattoos.
- **Wear what you want your cartoon to wear.** Your clothes, their colors and their prints carry over. A close selfie works too; the AI then adds simple trousers and shoes.
- **Friends:** everyone's face in view, nobody cut off at the edge, at most four people.
- **Pets:** the ears, the fur markings and any collar or bandana in view.
- Use your own photo, or a photo of friends who said yes. Never a celebrity or a stranger.
