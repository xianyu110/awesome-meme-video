## Real glitch makeup vs the AI version

The real glitch makeup trend is a green screen makeup trick. Makeup artists paint chroma-green face paint on their skin, film it, and key the green out in an editor so glitch colors show through. That is why people also call it green screen glitch makeup. Then they spend hours on the results look: a wig, liner and paint lines, rhinestones, sometimes white contact lenses. A green screen makeup transition like that needs paint, a wig, a steady camera and an editing app.

The AI makeup transition skips all of it. Like the glitch transition CapCut templates, it starts from one photo, but it also makes the paint part, so the whole makeup transition is one video. It is an AI makeup transformation, not a glitch filter. TikTok filters only add glitch bars on top of your face, while this changes the makeup and the hair after the cut.
