Avril Lavigne's 2002 ballad I'm With You became his signature: he sang it in the rain in April 2025, on park rides on 1 September 2026 (3.9 million views) and in a roller coaster selfie at Hopi Hari park on 24 September (4.2 million views). That roller coaster reel is the one this template copies.

On 4 October 2026 he posted the video that went around the world and became the pirate ship meme: he sits in a packed crowd on the swinging pirate ship ride at Hopi Hari, the Vulaviking in Vinhedo, Sao Paulo state, in slow motion, perfectly at peace while everyone around him screams. By 15 October it had 26.4 million views and 5.3 million likes on TikTok, and the top comment, "this level of dissociation should be studied", has 332,900 likes.

The next day, worldwide Google searches for I'm With You by Avril Lavigne hit their high of the month, and from 13 October AI apps advertised it as an I'm With You AI trend.
