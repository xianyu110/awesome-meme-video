```text
Create an exactly 15-second, 9:16 ultra-photorealistic Hollywood-level cinematic romantic thriller using only [Image 1] and [Image 2] as character references. The one in [Image 1] always pushes, and the one in [Image 2] always falls. Strictly preserve both characters' recognizable facial identities, skin tones, hairstyles, body proportions and original clothing throughout every shot. Never swap identities, blend faces, or reverse their roles.

In every frame, copy each exact face from its own photo (the face shape, eye color and eye shape, eyebrows, nose, mouth, skin tone, age, hair color, hair length and hairstyle, and any beard, moustache, glasses or freckles). Each face stays the same from the first frame to the last, so their friends recognize them at once. Both stay fully clothed.

Set the entire story on a dramatic coastal cliff overlooking a violently turbulent ocean during an approaching storm: dark rocks, enormous crashing waves, white sea foam, thick overcast skies, powerful coastal winds. Live-action 35mm film look, subtle film grain, organic skin textures, cold desaturated blue-gray color grading, soft overcast daylight. No glossy CGI.

0-2 s: a wide shot of enormous dark waves crashing against towering cliffs; a massive wave explodes into white spray, with roaring wind.
2-3.5 s: hard cut to a stable eye-level medium-wide side shot of the couple on the rocky coastline, holding hands, exchanging affectionate smiles, the wind in their hair.
3.5-5 s: the one who falls wraps their arms around the other's waist and lifts them into a passionate embrace, turning gently.
5-7 s: hard cut to an intimate side-profile close-up of a passionate kiss, eyes closed, both faces large, the gray-blue ocean blurred behind.
7-9.5 s: hard cut to an extremely tight profile close-up as the kiss ends: the one who pushes opens their eyes, and the affectionate expression turns into a cold, emotionless stare; the other shifts from affection to confusion. The romantic music fades into an ominous low drone.
9.5-11.5 s: hard cut to a stable medium-wide side shot near the cliff edge: without warning, the one who pushes thrusts both hands against the other's chest in one clearly visible push; the one who falls staggers back, throws the arms out and falls beyond the edge, while the one who pushes stays standing, cold and detached. A sharp impact sound and a whoosh.
11.5-13.5 s: hard cut to a vertical top-down aerial shot of the one who falls, face up, eyes wide in shock, arms out, falling in slow motion toward deep open water far from the rocks, as the camera rises away.
13.5-15 s: hard cut to a medium shot at the foot of the cliff: the one who falls pops up out of the foam, unhurt and soaking wet, spits out sea water and glares up with a deadpan "seriously?" look. A big comic splash and a short playful music sting.

No blood, no injury, never a body on the rocks: the fall ends in deep water and the one who falls is fine. Exactly two people. No text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark.
```
