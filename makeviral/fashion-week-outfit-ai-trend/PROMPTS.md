# Outfits of the Week AI: Seven Days, One Door, a New Look Every Day

- Source: [makeviral](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/fashion-week-outfit-ai-trend)
- Slug: `fashion-week-outfit-ai-trend`

Outfits of the week is the reel where one spot stays the same and the outfit changes every day: the camera films the same door, hallway or elevator, one short clip per day, with the day's name on screen.
