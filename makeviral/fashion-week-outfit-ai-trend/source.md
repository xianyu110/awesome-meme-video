## What is the Outfits of the Week trend, and where did it start?

The week of outfits is one of the oldest fashion formats on TikTok. #outfitsoftheweek had 205,118 videos and 3.4 billion views on 29 October 2026, and #ootw 218,024. Creators film one spot and cut the days together: @aloha.vero's "7 outfits of the week" (2 October 2023) has 208,000 plays and @izzydilg's "a week of outfits in paris" (17 November 2024) 220,800. Fashion week diaries took the same shape: @wisdm8's "Every single thing I wore during Paris fashion week" (14 October 2025) has 760,600 plays.

In 2026 it got day labels and went AI. On 21 July 2026 the AI-made influencer @zuri_ladiva posted "What I wore to work this week… Monday to Friday", five office looks in one elevator, the day on top (722,300 plays). On 5 August 2026 @lizy.p posted "Outfits of the week, city edition": she steps out of the same front door from Sunday to Saturday, with a handwritten day word beside the door (386,300 plays). On 3 October 2026 the AI influencer @mimi_rae0 reached 714,900 plays with five work outfits at one street corner, Monday to Friday. On 28 October 2026 AI video apps began running ads for a fashion week version: one selfie, the same Paris door every morning, seven couture looks.
