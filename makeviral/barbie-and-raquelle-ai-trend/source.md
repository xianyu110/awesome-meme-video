## What is the Barbie and Raquelle trend, and where did it start?

The trend started on TikTok in early October 2026 as photo slideshows. Each slide puts a still of Raquelle, the glamorous brunette rival from the animated web series Barbie: Life in the Dreamhouse (2012 to 2015), above a photo of the poster copying her pose, outfit and face. Best friends do it as a pair: the blonde one is Barbie, the brunette one is Raquelle. @67mrvee's slideshow of 6 October had 75,600 views by 16 October, and the biggest post we found, a bestie pair by @kenz_delong from 13 October, had 1.3 million views and 324,600 likes.

The AI version came next. On 11 October @xgk5o9 posted a how-to: keep your pose, hand gestures, camera angle, background and clothes, and let an AI image model turn your own selfie into the show's 3D cartoon style, so the doll on top is you. CapCut "Barbie slides" templates followed within a day. US Google searches for "barbie and raquelle" and "raquelle barbie" peaked on 12 October.

### Which photo works best?

Upload **one sharp, posed photo**: for **Solo**, you alone, from the waist up; for **Besties**, one photo of you and your best friend together.

- **Strike a pose.** The doll copies it exactly: a hand in your hair, a pout, a wink, a phone mirror selfie or a hand on your chin. The pose is the joke of the trend.

- **Wear the outfit you want on your doll.** Your clothes, jewelry and their colors carry over, and so does your background.
- **Besties:** both faces in view, close together, nobody cut off at the edge.
- Use your own photo, or a photo of a friend who said yes. Never a celebrity or a stranger.
