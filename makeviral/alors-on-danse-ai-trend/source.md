### Which photo works best?

You need **two photos, the DJ first**. Photo 1 is the DJ who stands at the table and taps the mini keyboard. Photo 2 is the MC who claps, points at the screen and laughs.

- One person or pet per photo, with the face clear and in good light.

- Any background works: the reel sets the white studio.

- Use photos of yourself, of adults who agreed, or of your own pets. Never a celebrity or a stranger.
