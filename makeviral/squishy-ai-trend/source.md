## What is the Squishy Face trend, and where did it start?

Squishy faces started as an AI ASMR niche in March 2026. An Instagram account called Oddly Pressed, whose bio said "All content is artificial and non-human", posted top-down clips of glossy silicone faces on wooden tables being kneaded by two hands, and had 55,300 followers by mid-March. On 12 March a YouTube creator shared a ChatGPT "master prompt" for the look, and the tutorials that followed passed the same text on word for word. AI versions were on TikTok by 19 March, and in April a squishy version of a famous footballer's face got 10.4 million views.

CapCut then made it a one-photo template: a 6-second AI template by @Louise9125 turns a selfie into the same clip. Tutorials for it got 624,000 views in May and 1.3 million in July, and a new wave of tutorials started in October 2026. Search grew with it: in the US, "squishy face" went from about 1,300 Google searches a month in February to 2,400 a month from May.

Use **your own photo**, or a photo of a friend who said yes.
