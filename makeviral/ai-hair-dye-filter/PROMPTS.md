### 1. 1. The video prompt is filled in. Settings: Seedance 2.5, 10 s, 720p, 9:16, sound on. The video prompt

*Kind: video_prompt*

```text
A viral AI hair dye video: the person from [Image 1] dyes their own hair burgundy from a bowl, then shows it off sleek and straight, then curled, filmed like a real phone video. Photorealistic, natural daylight, fine phone-camera grain, vertical 9:16.

The person is the one in [Image 1], in every frame: copy the exact face from [Image 1] (the face shape, eye color and eye shape, eyebrows, nose, mouth, skin tone, age, and any beard, moustache, glasses, piercings, freckles or face marks), and the clothes and any jewelry from [Image 1]. The same face from the first frame to the last, so their friends recognize them at once, also when the head tilts down and the hair falls around the face. Never morph, never beautify, never change the age or the skin tone.

THE HAIR. Before the dye: exactly the hair from [Image 1], with the same color, texture, length and style (braids, a bun, curls or short hair stay as they are). After the dye: long, straight, sleek, glossy burgundy hair: a deep wine red with a brighter cherry-red shine at the roots and along a neat middle part, falling well past the shoulders. Later: big, bouncy, glossy burgundy curls from the cheeks down, full and soft, in the same deep wine red. The new color is always this burgundy: never bright red, pink or orange.

The place is the place behind the person in [Image 1], with the same light; if [Image 1] shows little of it, a bright yard outdoors in daylight. The camera is a phone held at arm's length, framed from the waist up, the face large and sharp.

0-3 s: the person looks into the camera with a cool, serious face, a round bowl full of thick, dark burgundy hair dye in one hand and a flat dye brush in the other. They dip the brush and paint the dye onto the hair from the parting down; every stroke turns the strands deep burgundy.
3-4.5 s: a quick cut: the dye is done and the hair is now the long, straight, sleek burgundy hair described above. The bowl and the brush are gone; they run both hands through it.
4.5-6.5 s: a flat iron glides slowly down the long straight hair from the roots to the ends, with a thin wisp of steam, and the hair shines.
6.5-8 s: a quick cut: the hair is now in big, bouncy burgundy curls; they lift the curls with both hands and let them drop.
8-10 s: they flip the hair back over one shoulder, lift the chin and smile proudly into the camera, the glossy curls framing the face.

Audio: soft brush strokes, the hiss of the flat iron, a small happy laugh and a light, upbeat original instrumental beat. No lyrics, no talking.

The dye stays on the hair and in the bowl: never on the face or the skin. Anatomy rules: one head, two hands, five fingers on each hand. Exactly one person: no hairdresser, no other hands. No text, no captions, no logos, no watermark, no brand names on the bowl, the brush or the flat iron.
```
