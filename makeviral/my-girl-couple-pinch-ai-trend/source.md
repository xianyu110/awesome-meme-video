## What is the My Girl Couple Trend trend, and where did it start?

It started in Brazil. On 21 October 2026 at 16:31 UTC, an AI creator account posted a 10-second clip captioned "My girl" with a heart: a huge hand covers a couple's eyes, flies away after 7 seconds, and the two smile into the camera. TikTok marks it as AI-generated. By 23 October it had 3.6 million views and 290,500 likes, and the top comment, with 6,360 likes, just asks "AI?". Most comments are in Spanish.

The clip's sound became the trend. Two hours after the post, people began copying it for real, covering the lens with their own hand and then showing their face; 37 hours later the sound had 947 videos. ## Giant hand, big hand, hand pinch: how the trick works

The joke is forced perspective. A hand close to the lens looks as big as the two people behind it, so it seems to pinch their heads between the thumb and the fingers. That is why people also call it the giant hand trend, the big hand trend or a couple pinch. On TikTok, many people film it for real: a friend holds a hand over the camera, then pulls it away, like a hand over camera transition. The AI version needs nobody to hold the hand: it builds the whole shot from your photos.

It is a cousin of the [hand pinch filter](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/hand-pinch-filter-ai), the hand pinch trend where you close your eyes and a realistic hand pinches your cheek. That one is for one person and the fun is your reaction; this one is for two, and the fun is the reveal.
