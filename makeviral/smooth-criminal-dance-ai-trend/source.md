## What is the Smooth Criminal Dance trend, and where did it start?

"Smooth Criminal" is Michael Jackson's 1988 single from *Bad*. Its film in *Moonwalker* made the fedora, the white suit and the famous forward lean, the **smooth criminal lean**, part of pop culture.

The TikTok routine is not the Michael Jackson Smooth Criminal dance of that film, and it came much later. The dancer @charliewakey_ posted his own "Smooth Criminal" choreography on 12 July 2025. In June 2026 the Canadian dancer @carlowrush, who also started the Billie Jean dance trend that summer, posted his takes on 3 and 10 June; they had 5.1 and 4.5 million views by 31 October. Dancers copied it with "dc: @carlowrush" in the caption, and tutorials slowed it down and mirrored it.

In August the **smooth criminal trend** turned into a duo and friends challenge: @larosi.222 posted a duo take on 16 August (5.2 million views), @isabel_jasmine one on 18 August (2.9 million), and a friend group, @thesixpack.sl, one on 6 September (2.2 million). @carlowrush and Ryan Hoffmann followed on 13 September (4.2 million). AI accounts made pet versions, and on 31 October takes were still going up every day.
