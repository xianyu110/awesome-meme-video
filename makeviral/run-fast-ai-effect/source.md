### Which photo works best?

**Things version:** use **one photo of a place with clear, separate things in it**: chairs and a table on a porch, a sofa in a living room, a parked car, a shed, a small house, potted plants.

- Shoot it like the trend: a phone held upright at eye height, the things in the middle of the frame, some empty ground around them so they have room to run.
- Two to eight things work best. One tiny thing in a big room is hard to see; a hundred things turn into a blur.
- Daylight and a sharp photo. The video starts from your exact photo, so what you shoot is what you get.
- People in the photo stay where they are: only the things run.

**You or your pet version:** one sharp photo of one adult or one pet, the whole body in view, with open space in front of them.
