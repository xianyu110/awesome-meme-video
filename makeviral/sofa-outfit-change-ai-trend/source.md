## How does the sofa outfit change work?

Every look follows the same camera move, four times in 12 seconds:

| Time | What you see |
|---|---|
| 0-3 s | The camera starts low at the foot of the sofa, rises to straight above you and turns slowly. Look 1. |
| 3-6 s | A fast spin with motion blur, and you come out of it in look 2. |
| 6-9 s | Another spin, look 3. |
| 9-12 s | The last spin, look 4, and the camera settles above you. |

The spin is the trick: the outfit changes in the blur, so it looks like one take.
