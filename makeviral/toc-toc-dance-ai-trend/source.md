## What is the Toc Toc Dance trend, and where did it start?

The dance spread at the end of September 2026 with a wave of AI-made clips of deadpan characters in big wigs and vintage suits. The best known of them is Jean Phil, or Jean Philanthrope, an AI character whose first video went up on Instagram on 17 September, which is why many people call it the Jean Phil dance.

On 28 September the French account @castinggastro posted the clip most versions copy: one dancer in a white suit hops, knocks and kicks across a bare room. It had 3.4 million views by 14 October, and its sound was in 2,378 videos. A group version by @jeancultouw passed 9.5 million views.

Real people and pets joined in. An AI tabby cat by @shalunts_art did the routine on 4 October and reached 1.6 million views. On 6 October the college group @4thfloorboys danced down their dorm hallway, and the clip had 24.1 million views and 3.1 million likes by 14 October. BBC News covered the character on 7 October. By 14 October #toctoc held 36,674 TikTok videos and 510 million views.
