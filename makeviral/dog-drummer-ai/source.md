## What is the Dog Drummer trend, and where did it start?

The water trick comes from the drummer Emil Vergo. On 16 September 2024 he posted a drum cover of "Beggin'" (the Måneskin version) on TikTok, played outdoors: he pours water over the drums before the first hit, so every hit throws up a spray. That clip has 37 million TikTok views, and the same clip on YouTube Shorts, "BEGGIN + WATER", has more than 14 million.

On 6 July 2026 he posted a studio version: a dark room with warm string lights, a red kit and neon yellow sticks. It passed 12.1 million views and 750,000 likes on TikTok.

In November 2026 AI creators began to remake that studio clip shot for shot as a dog playing drums video, with a dog behind the kit: a deadpan bully dog that pours the water, tosses the bottle and plays like a touring pro. The first AI dog drummer templates went online on 6 November, built on an AI clip shared on X that month. It is still an early trend: #dogdrummer held 1,649 TikTok videos on 8 November 2026.
