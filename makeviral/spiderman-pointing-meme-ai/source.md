## What is the Pointing Meme trend, and where did it start?

The spider man pointing meme comes from a frame of a 1960s cartoon, the episode "Double Identity", in which a villain dresses as the hero and the two point at each other. The first known image macro was posted on 5 February 2011, and the picture became the internet's way of saying "two people accuse each other of the same thing" (Daily Dot). The film No Way Home used it in its promotion in 2022, with three heroes.

Every Halloween, fans in web suits act it out in the street. A New York group got more than 2 million TikTok views in 2021 (Comicbook.com), and on Halloween 2025 @laixtao filmed a crowd of them pointing on Halsted Street in Chicago (34,400 views). On 31 October and 1 November 2026 the pose went round again: a famous streamer in a web-suit costume walked into a New York restaurant full of fans in web suits, and they all did the meme. On 7 November 2026, #spidermanpointingmeme had 319 TikTok videos and 3.2 million views, and #spidermanpointing 1.6 million.
