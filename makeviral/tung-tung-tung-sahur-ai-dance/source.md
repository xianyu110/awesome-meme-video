## What is the Tung Tung Sahur Dance trend, and where did it start?

Tung Tung Tung Sahur is an AI-made meme character: a wooden log with a face, stick arms and a bat. Its name copies the drum beats that wake people for sahur, the meal before dawn in Ramadan. It was first posted as an AI video in February 2025, and the #tungtungtungsahur hashtag on TikTok dates from 28 February 2025.

The dance came a year later. On 30 March 2026, @memescreens posted a green-screen template of the log dancing to "Dumb wit it (get in dea)" by Geaux Teezy, with AI animation by @sloptokk. People put it next to themselves and danced along, and two of those duets from 2 and 3 April passed 4 million and 2 million views. Know Your Meme added it as the **Triple T Dance** on 15 April.

The biggest clip followed on 27 April: an animation by @chickennoodle266 of the log doing the "it's hard to breathe" hush routine to "Cradles" by Sub Urban, which has 22 million views. Its moves are the ones this template follows.
