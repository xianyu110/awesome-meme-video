## What is the Fuzzy Dream Dance trend, and where did it start?

The dance is **skeur**, also called skeur hom: a low, wide squat-and-bounce style from South African TikTok. #skeur held 27,164 videos and 439 million views on 29 October 2026, and #skeurhom, used since January 2026, 12,999 videos.

The sound is a sped-up dance remix of the first line of Madonna's 2008 song "Miles Away": "I just woke up from a fuzzy dream". The remix sound most clips use went up on TikTok in January 2025 and was in 13,900 videos by 29 October 2026; two newer sounds with the same line add about 13,000 more.

In early October South African dancers started to caption their skeur clips with the line, and the Fuzzy Dream trend took off. On 16 October the singer and dancer @dowaynesmiler5 posted the solo take on this page. It had 1.6 million views and 250,200 likes by 29 October. The dance then crossed to the US, and on 23 October the first AI versions from a phone-editor template appeared; the #fuzzydreamdance tag started on 28 October.
