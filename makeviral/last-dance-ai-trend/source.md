## What is the The Last Dance trend, and where did it start?

The song is "fpd ff7 botsu 8" by the Vocaloid producer Maretu, first uploaded to SoundCloud on 19 September 2014. Fans nicknamed it "The Last Dance", and you will also see it searched as botsu 8 the last dance. Know Your Meme dates the earliest known animation with it to 14 August 2026, by @fugashizzz._. On 29 August @amy_yayxd shared a stage background that made the format easy to copy; it had 6.8 million views by 3 November.

Artists filled that stage with pairs of their favorite characters dancing one final duet, and a stick-figure dance base by @1sampokoskifan (21 September, 880,500 views) showed the moves step by step. Then the last dance trend went IRL: duos, couples, siblings and cosplayers filmed the spin in hallways, parks and parking lots, and some of those clips passed 7 million views. An English lyric cover by @vinnmp4s followed on 6 October.

On 2 November Know Your Meme made the trend its lead story. By then the sound was in about 81,000 videos, and Google counted 1,000 US searches for "the last dance trend" in September, up from none in August.
