## What is the Face Bouquet trend, and where did it start?

The face bouquet took off on TikTok in the first week of November 2026. On 3 November @jalisamyana asked "yall want a flower bouquet of me?" with a bouquet of her own faces, and a few hours later, on 4 November, @xo.lenoraaa posted the clean version that everyone copied: nine cut-outs of her head in a folded kraft paper cone with a white polka dot bow, on pink. Her post started the tag #flowerbouquetofme and had 6,030 views and 395 likes by 6 November.

The look comes from a CapCut template that cuts your face out of your photos and stacks the copies as flowers. On 4 November tutorial accounts began to post "how to make the flower bouquet with face on it filter" videos (@zmomotyy875 had 6,145 views by 6 November), and by 5 November dozens of tutorials carried the caption "flower bouquet with face on it". Posters call theirs "a bouquet of me", "a bouquet full of me" or "best gift ever". The idea is older: a photo bouquet of printed pictures on sticks is a handmade gift with 10.9 million views on #photobouquet. This template makes the bouquet with AI instead of scissors.

### Which photo works best?

Use **a sharp selfie from the chest up**, face to the camera. Each flower is a cut-out of your head and shoulders, so your face, hair and outfit in the photo are what you get, nine times.

- **Face the camera** in soft daylight. A clear, bright face stays recognizable even when the heads are small.
- **No hat, no sunglasses, no hand over the face.** Clear glasses stay on.
- **3 selfies:** three different photos of you, with different outfits or smiles. The bouquet mixes them.
- **Couple:** your photo first, then your partner's or friend's. One person per photo.
- **Pet:** your dog or cat with its face to the camera and both ears in view, not blurry.

## What can go wrong, and how to fix it

- **Some heads look a little less like you.** Nine faces in one picture are small, and a small face is harder to keep than one big portrait. Run it again, or use a sharper, brighter selfie taken straight on.
- **The number of heads changes.** The prompt asks for 7 to 9, and a run can come back with one more or one less. It still reads as the trend.
- **A real flower shows up.** A few green leaves at the rim are part of the look; if a real flower takes the place of a face, run it again.
- **Couple mixed you up.** Check the order: photo 1 is you, photo 2 the other person. One person per photo, both faces clear.
- **A pet head looks wrong.** Use a photo with both ears and both eyes in view; a side view gives side views.
