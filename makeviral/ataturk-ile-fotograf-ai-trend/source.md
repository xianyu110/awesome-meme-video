## What is the Atatürk ile Fotoğraf trend, and where did it start?

Turks have posed next to portraits of Atatürk in schools, offices and homes for generations. In 2023, the 100th year of the Republic, AI made it a trend. On 28 October 2023 Webtekno wrote about a site that put people's photos next to an Atatürk image, and on 29 October 2023 Cumhuriyet called "Atatürk ile yapay zeka fotoğrafı nasıl yapılır?" one of the most searched questions of the day. TikTok's #atatürkilefotoğraf tag was created that same day.

The search comes back every autumn. On 29 October 2025 Türkgün published Gemini prompts for an atatürk ile yan yana fotoğraf, and in October 2026 creators shared new prompts in their captions: on 25 October a TikTok creator posted a full 29 Ekim photo prompt with "Atatürk'ün portresi arka planda saygılı ve estetik bir şekilde yer alsın" ("Atatürk's portrait appears in the background, respectfully"). From 27 October 2026 an AI photo app ran Turkish ads for the look this page makes: an adult in a suit, standing next to a large framed black-and-white portrait in a sunlit marble hall.

Not everyone likes AI pictures of Atatürk. In a Posta column on 17 November 2024, Işınsu Kestelli warned that made-up AI images of him are getting hard to tell from real photos. That is why this page never makes a new picture of Atatürk himself: he appears only as a framed portrait on the wall, and only you are in the room.

## How we keep it respectful

This is a tribute, so a few things never happen in your photo or video:

- **Atatürk is never changed or animated.** He appears only as a framed portrait on the wall, in the style of his well-known official photographs. In the clip only you move: the face in the portrait never moves, blinks or talks.
- **No hug, no touch, no joke.** You stand next to the portrait; you never touch it, and nobody acts as him.
- **The flag is treated with care.** In the Celebration version you hold a small flag upright; it is never worn, folded or on the floor.
- **No politics.** No party logo, no slogan, no text on the wall.
- **Adults only.** Many trend posts use photos of children; this page makes photos of adults.

When you share it, say it is an AI photo. That is the honest way, and it keeps the real photographs of Atatürk clear from the made ones.

## 29 Ekim or 10 Kasım: which one to post?

**29 Ekim Cumhuriyet Bayramı** is a celebration: the **29 Ekim portrait** (bright sunlight, an elegant suit) or the **Celebration** version (flags and balloons) fit it. **10 Kasım** is different. On 10 November 1938, at 09:05, Atatürk died at Dolmabahçe Palace, and every year Turkey stops for a minute of silence at that time. It is a day of remembrance, not a party. The **10 Kasım remembrance** version is made for it: soft light, dark clothes, carnations below the portrait, nothing festive. If you search for a 10 kasım yapay zeka fotoğraf or a 10 kasım yapay zeka atatürk image, this is the calm one to share, with a simple caption such as "Saygı ve özlemle".
