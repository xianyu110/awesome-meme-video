## What is the La Mujer del Pelotero trend, and where did it start?

"La Mujer del Pelotero" ("the baseball player's wife") is a Cuban party song. Deezer lists a recording by Baby Lores on the compilation Cubaton 2010, and the group Merenglass recorded it around the same time. The sound behind the TikTok trend is a newer version by Ángel DR, released on 6 February 2026, which he calls his cover.

The dance is mostly hands: you stand in a row with friends or family and do the same moves on the beat. It is Spanish for "the baseball player's wife". The song jokes about the partners of men with different jobs, and the line "le gusta el bate a la mujer del pelotero" ("the baseball player's wife likes the bat") is a playful double meaning.
