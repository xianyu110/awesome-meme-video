## What is the ATM Glitch trend, and where did it start?

The clip that started it went up on Instagram on 6 October 2026 (5 October in the US), posted by @ehustlr with @aicreatedthis_. It looks like grainy black-and-white footage from the camera in the corner of an ATM booth: a woman in a fur coat and sunglasses takes her cash, the machine suddenly shoots out a nonstop stream of bills, the blast knocks her down, and she ends sitting in a carpet of money. The text above it read "The only glitch I'm praying for this year", and the 5,436 comments are mostly one word: "Mine".

For ten days it spread as reposts with that line and its twins, "the only glitch I want" and "the only glitch we need", from the US to Nigeria, South Africa, Pakistan and India. On 17 October Russian-speaking AI creators turned it into a one-photo template they call "Банкомат" ("ATM"), and @prostoanastasiya_ posted her remake the same day: the same booth and the same burst with a new woman in glasses. It reached 813,546 likes in three days, with comments like "The problem I want" and "Where is this ATM?" in English, Portuguese, Spanish and Hindi. By 19 October a cat stood at the ATM too.

The idea is older than the trend. AI clips of ATMs that "glitch" and spray money have gone around since 2025, and some were passed off as real: in late 2025, fact-checkers in South Africa had to debunk fake ATM glitch CCTV clips. This one won because it does not pretend. Everybody knows it is a wish, and the comments play along.
