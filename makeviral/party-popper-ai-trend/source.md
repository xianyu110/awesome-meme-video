## What is the Baby Boom Trend trend, and where did it start?

The sound is the hook of "sH1pu4Ka!" (Shipuchka, Russian slang for a fizzy sweet), a song by the Belarusian-born singer МЭЙБИ БЭЙБИ, or Maybe Baby, released on 11 February 2022. Its first line, "бейби бум, за щекой шипучка" ("baby boom, a fizzy sweet in my cheek"), became a TikTok lip-sync sound in autumn 2026: a hashtag that spells out the whole line was created on 29 September.

The party popper came on 4 November 2026. Two Russian-speaking bloggers posted the version with a pop on "бейби бум", confetti rain and playful hand moves, and both passed 3 million views in ten days. By 14 November the main sound held 94,700 videos, and #бейбибум grew by about 520 videos a day. Adults joined in: @vladragovsky's "manly lip sync" from 11 November, the reel this page copies, has 1.1 million views.

On 13 November a Belarusian AI tutorial by @minnelemlen showed how to make the тренд с хлопушкой (the party popper trend) from one photo, and English captions started to call it the baby boom trend.
