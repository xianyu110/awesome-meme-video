## What is the Abra Kadabra trend, and where did it start?

The Abra Kadabra song on TikTok is a Brazilian funk "som original" whose hook chants "abra kadabra". Its sound page lists a first video on 19 May 2026, and by 18 October 2026 it had 469,000 videos; a second upload of the same hook by the account DUDU MUSIC had 1.3 million. It is not Lady Gaga's 2025 single "Abracadabra", and people who search for the Abra Kadabra Brazilian song often mean this funk hook.

The Abra Kadabra dance took off in late August 2026. A group reel on 21 August credited its own steps ("Dc: nosotras"), and duo reels from 26 to 31 August got 3 to 7 million views each. The idea is always the same: one person dances alone, the second walks in on the beat as if by magic, and the two finish in sync.

The cleanest take came on 1 September 2026 from Kelly (@kellyisdancing), a 22-year-old dancer in Germany: she dances alone in a black top and white sweatpants, and her "evil twin", Kelly again in the opposite colors, steps in at the 3-second mark. By 17 October it had 3.8 million views and 508,800 likes. In October the Abra Kadabra trend TikTok wave moved to pets, magic-trick edits and AI: on 17 October 2026 an AI video site added a two-photo version where you snap and a friend or a pet appears.
