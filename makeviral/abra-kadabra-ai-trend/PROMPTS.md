# Abra Kadabra Trend AI: Snap, and Your Friend Appears

- Source: [makeviral](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/abra-kadabra-ai-trend)

The Abra Kadabra trend is the TikTok duo dance to a Brazilian funk track with a chanted "abra kadabra" hook: one dancer starts alone, and on the beat a second dancer appears next to them, then both dance the same routine in sync.
