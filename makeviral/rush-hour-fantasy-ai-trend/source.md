## What is the Rush Hour Fantasy trend, and where did it start?

The trend comes from the back-seat sing-along in the 1998 action comedy *Rush Hour*. In the scene, the passenger in the middle of the back seat of a black sedan sings Mariah Carey's 1995 hit "Fantasy" along with the radio, while the two men in the front, the driver and a bodyguard, side-eye the show and then turn round. People search it as the rush hour fantasy scene, the rush hour singing scene or just "rush hour sweet fantasy", after the words of the song's chorus.

A TikTok post of the scene by @grimlock9412 on 7 July 2024 has 956,300 views, and one by @retro.flashback0.02 on 13 October 2025 has 1.1 million. In August 2025 Very Asian reported that Mariah Carey still loves the moment ("That's still a moment for me. I love it"). The biggest post is from the streaming service Tubi on 10 August 2026: 3.8 million views and 314,100 likes by 10 November.

On 10 November 2026 two AI video sites turned the scene into a template, with a pet or a friend as the singer. The #fantasyrushhour tag had only 7 videos that day, so the fantasy rush hour AI version is brand new.
