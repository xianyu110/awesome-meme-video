## What is the Lonyo Dance trend, and where did it start?

"Lonyo!" is a 2025 song by the London artist Bakar. It samples "Summer of Love", the 2000 UK garage hit by Lonyo, and its Spanish hook "mi único anhelo es no volver a pensar en ti" (my only wish is to stop thinking about you). The video, directed by Tom Emmerson with choreography by Joe Grey Adams, Jordan J Funk and Aisha Amuah, came out in August 2025: a drone films a huge after-school dance in a London playground. It had about 559,000 YouTube views by November 2026.

In late October 2026 the **Lonyo AI trend** began on Instagram: creators ran the video through an AI motion swap and replaced the dancers with film heroes, superheroes and cats, each post captioned "Original MV: Bakar, Lonyo!" with #Lonyo. The cat versions, the **Lonyo dancing cats**, need no people at all. MakeViral copies two AI-made versions, one with an adult street dance crew and one with cats. The music video itself stars real schoolchildren, so we never use it.

- **2000**: The UK garage artist Lonyo releases "Summer of Love"; Bakar makes its Spanish hook "mi único anhelo" the leitmotif of "Lonyo!" 25 years later. Source: [jenesaispop](https://jenesaispop.com/2025/08/18/505431/bakar-summer-love-lonyo/).
- **2025-08**: Bakar releases "Lonyo!" with a video by director Tom Emmerson: dozens of London schoolkids dance a drilled routine in a playground, filmed by drone. Source: [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/tom-emmerson-bakar-lonyo-video-film-project-010925).
- **2025-08-13**: Promonews features the video and credits the choreographers Joe Grey Adams, Jordan J Funk and Aisha Amuah. Source: [Promonews](https://www.promonews.tv/videos/2025/08/13/bakar-lonyo-tom-emmerson/92191).
- **2026-10-26**: The first AI swaps of the video appear on Instagram, captioned "Original MV: Bakar, Lonyo!": a film heroine in a yellow tracksuit leads a crew in black suits. Source: [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/De-YjtLNCka/).
