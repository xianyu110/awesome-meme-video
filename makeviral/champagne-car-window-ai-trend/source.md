## What is the Champagne Car Window trend, and where did it start?

The trend comes from Russian-speaking Instagram. On 1 October 2026, @marina_yaptunay posted a 9-second reel with the file name "в трендах" ("trending"): on a wet highway at night she sits in the open rear window of a dark burgundy SUV, raises a bottle of champagne with both arms, pops it, sprays a tall jet into the rain, drinks until it runs down her chin and throws the bottle away while a man drives. Instagram marks the reel as age-restricted because of the alcohol. It had 135,303 likes by 20 October.

The AI remakes started the next day. @ai.creatorledii posted an AI version with a man who sits on the outside of a black SUV, pops and drinks, with the caption line "Только так и не иначе" ("only like this and no other way"). Commenters asked for the prompt in English, Spanish and Russian. Within days, Russian AI preset sites sold the scene as a one-photo preset called "Шампанское на ходу", champagne on the go, and AI creators posted their own champagne car trend clips on TikTok, some with a Happy Birthday title at the end.
