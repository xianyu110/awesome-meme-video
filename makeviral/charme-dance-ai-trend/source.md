## What is the Charme Dance trend, and where did it start?

Charme is Rio de Janeiro's soul and R&B party dance: small gliding side steps, rolling shoulders and cool turns, danced by whole crowds at the same time. The best-known baile charme meets under the Viaduto de Madureira, and the dance also fills the street parties at Pedra do Sal, a historic square in the old port.

The trend starts with a real clip. On 7 August 2026, the charme dancer @zenegaooficial posted himself dancing in the middle of the Pedra do Sal crowd at night, under bright floodlights: 1.6 million views and 275,100 likes by 18 October. In October, people began to put other faces into that clip with AI. On 16 October, the TikTok account @seuzerj put its white-haired AI grandpa, Seu Zé de Madureira, into it ("nobody believed in grandpa at the baile charme"): the first AI version without a celebrity to take off, with 96,400 views.

On 18 October at 12:12 UTC the first CapCut template tutorials for the charme dance trend came out, and the top one passed 605,000 views in less than a day. Most makers are in Brazil, where creators call it the dança AI retrô of the Festa Charme.

## What is charme, and what is a baile charme?

Dança charme, o que é? Charme is the dance of Rio's black music parties since the 1980s: soul, R&B and slow funk, danced with smooth, coordinated steps instead of jumps. A baile charme is the party itself; baile charme, o que é in one line: a night where the whole floor does the same gliding moves to old-school R&B. People who learn it look for dança charme passo a passo videos, and the classic tracks are called charme das antigas.

The most famous baile charme, the Baile Charme do Viaduto de Madureira, takes place under a road bridge in Madureira, which is why searches for baile charme Madureira and the Viaduto de Madureira come with this trend. The clip the trend copies was filmed at the Pedra do Sal street party in central Rio. That is why people also call it the Rio street party AI trend, or the charme dance Brazil trend.
