## What is the Thriller Dance AI trend, and where did it start?

The Thriller dance is the zombie routine from Michael Jackson's music video "Thriller", directed by John Landis and choreographed by Michael Peters. The video premiered on MTV on 2 December 1983, and it is closely tied to Halloween: fans around the world re-create its zombie dance.

The search data shows the same rhythm. US Google searches for "thriller dance" reached 27,100 in October 2025, against 2,400 a month in early 2026. A clip of the dance at the 2025 New York Halloween Parade had 10.1 million TikTok views by October 2026. After the biopic *Michael* opened on 24 April 2026, searches rose again, to 8,100 in May. On 1 June the LA dancers behind @totummare posted the dance break as a duo; it passed 1 million views.

The AI versions came in summer 2026.
