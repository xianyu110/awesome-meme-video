## What is the La Calabaza Dance trend, and where did it start?

"La calabaza" is Spanish for "the pumpkin". The song is "LA CALABAZA (TINIEBLA Edit)", a fast guaracha-style track by the independent producer Pumpky; its hook shouts "la calabaza" again and again. Pumpky's own TikTok sound of the track dates from 29 December 2025.

The dance comes from AI cats. On 28 June 2026, the AI cat animation account @meowtakeover posted three AI kittens dancing in sync by a road (107.4 million views). On 27 August it posted the same trio again, now with a black cowboy hat on the white kitten and small round glasses on the black-and-white one, dancing on a roadside at night to "La Calabaza". That reel has 28.9 million views and 4.1 million likes by 16 October, and it is the first La Calabaza reel that went big: reposts and edits followed within hours, and a green-screen copy of 28 August gave TikTok the sound most people use (253,400 videos by 16 October).

Then everyone joined. People pasted the kittens over their own dance videos, did the dance themselves, and turned the trio into their own pets with AI: on 9 September an AI version with three dachshunds in hats reached 54.2 million views, the most viewed AI take of the trend. US Google searches for "la calabaza" went from 480 in July to 18,100 in September 2026, and by 16 October #lacalabaza had 57,428 TikTok videos and 517 million views. With Halloween on 31 October, the pumpkin song is now everywhere.
