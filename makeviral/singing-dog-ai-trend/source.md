## What is the Singing Dog trend, and where did it start?

The singing dog trend started on 14 October 2026 with a phone app's AI template called "Singing Pets" (a CapCut template by @Shjn). The first post came from the UK: @tinaverrico's Rottweiler Charlie, with a hair dryer held high and its mouth wide open, reached 51,700 views and 2,500 shares. On 15 October @kblackwell1975's grey dog Oakley got 38,100 views, and on 16 October a boxer and a goldendoodle followed with 34,100 and 10,900. Every post uses the same sound, a big operatic singing voice, and commenters joke about famous opera tenors.

By 17 October the sound had 121 videos from dog and cat owners, and the first tutorials explained how to make your pet sing from one photo. People tag it #dogblowdry, #singingdogtrend and #petsingingtrend.

The idea is older than the template: AI "shower concert" clips of a dog singing in the shower went viral from December 2025, such as a chihuahua clip with 4.2 million views. The new trend adds the towel, the hair dryer and the brush, so the dog with hair dryer looks like it is getting ready for a big night.

### Which photo works best?

Upload **one photo of your pet**: your dog in the Dog version, your cat in the Cat version (any pet works in either).

- **Face first.** The clip is a close shot from the chest up, so the face matters most: sharp, in good light, with both eyes and the ears in view.
- **Front of the body.** A photo where your pet sits or stands facing the camera gives the right fur colors and markings on the chest and the front legs.
- **One pet only.** With two pets in the photo, the AI picks one or mixes them.
- **Real colors.** Skip filters, costumes and heavy shadows: the clip copies the fur colors from your photo.

A wet dog straight out of the bath is fine, but a dry, fluffy photo shows the real coat best.
