### Which photo works best?

Upload **one photo of your pet**: your dog in the Dog version, your cat in the Cat version (any pet works in either).

- **Face first.** The clip is a close shot from the chest up, so the face matters most: sharp, in good light, with both eyes and the ears in view.
- **Front of the body.** A photo where your pet sits or stands facing the camera gives the right fur colors and markings on the chest and the front legs.
- **One pet only.** With two pets in the photo, the AI picks one or mixes them.
- **Real colors.** Skip filters, costumes and heavy shadows: the clip copies the fur colors from your photo.

A wet dog straight out of the bath is fine, but a dry, fluffy photo shows the real coat best.
