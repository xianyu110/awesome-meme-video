## What is the Tying My Shoes Meme trend, and where did it start?

The clip comes from the New York creator @jaywiccwya, who tags his videos #jaywicwya. Filmed with the phone low on a park path, it shows him put his drink cup down and crouch to tie his shoe. People walking past take the cup for a tip cup and drop bills in it, and he switches to a huge grin and sits down behind it. The post is no longer on his profile, but copies went around from 14 July 2026: one titled "Tie my shoes gone wrong", another with his watermark and the line "When you're just trying to tie your shoes".

On 20 July 2026 a compilation opened with it under a French caption that means "the fastest career change in history". It passed 1.6 million views, the first post of the trend with more than a million.

In November 2026 the clip turned into an AI trend: creators kept the park, the camera and the passers-by and swapped only the man. Two remixes came out on 7 November; on 11 November @merkurianin posted an AI black cat in his place. It had 1.2 million views two days later, and it is the clip this template copies.
