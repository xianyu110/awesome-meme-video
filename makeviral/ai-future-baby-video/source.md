### Which photo works best?

Upload **two photos, one adult per photo**, the first partner first. Clear, front-facing faces in daylight give the model the most to mix.

- Head-and-shoulders photos work best. The model needs the faces, not the outfits.
- Natural hair color and no heavy filters. A beauty filter changes skin tone and eye shape, and the baby inherits the filter.
- Similar light in both photos helps. One dark photo and one bright one can make the model favor the brighter face.
- Both people must be adults who agreed to it. Never use a photo of an ex, a crush, a celebrity or a stranger, and never a photo of a real child.

## Can AI show what your baby will actually look like?

No. The model does not read genes and does not know which traits are dominant. It looks at two faces and draws a baby that borrows visible features from both: eye shape, hair color, skin tone. Run it twice and you get two different babies. Real children often look nothing like either parent at one year old.

Treat it the way people treat a horoscope: a sweet "what if" to share, not an answer. That is how the best posts frame it too, with captions like "the AI says our kid would have my eyebrows" rather than claims about the future.

## What goes wrong with an AI future baby video, and how do you fix it?

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| One parent looks like a stranger | Use a sharper photo of that person, and check the still before you animate it. |
