## What is the Need for Speed AI trend, and where did it start?

The trend started with one post. On 11 November 2026, the truck owner @lakenyoung shared an AI video of his red pickup dropped into a movie-style street scene, with the line "What did Ai do to my truck" and #needforspeed. The joke: the AI kept the red paint but turned his pickup into a low two-door sports car halfway between a truck and a muscle car. It reached 70,500 views and 5,334 likes in three days.

On 12 November, tutorial accounts reposted his clip as a Need for Speed AI video tutorial (161,500 views for the first one; @lakenyoung answered "Bro this is my video") and pointed people to a Kling app template: paste the video link, replace one photo with your car, generate. By 14 November #needforspeedaitrend and #needforspeedaicar had about 80 videos each and more than 330,000 views, and more than ten new tutorial copies came out on 13 November. Posters call it the Need for Speed scene, after the street-racing films and games, and the most common complaint under the tutorials is that the app template gives everyone the movie car instead of their own.
