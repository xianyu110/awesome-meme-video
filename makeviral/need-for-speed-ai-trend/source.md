### Which photo works best?

Upload **one sharp photo of your whole vehicle**: a car, pickup truck, SUV or van.

- **Front corner view.** Stand at a front corner so the photo shows the front and one side, from the roof to the tires. The scene shows your vehicle from the front, the side and behind, so the shape and the paint matter most.
- **Daylight, no crowd.** Park it alone in good light. People in the photo are left out. A photo at night or under colored lights can change how the paint looks in the video.
- **Special backs.** A tow boom, a dump bed or a utility body is drawn from what your photo shows. Pick a front-corner photo that also shows part of the back.
- **Mods count.** The lift, the wheels, the bumpers, racks and light bars carry over, so keep them in the picture.
- **Badges and plates:** the video leaves out badges, emblems and the number plate, so you can post it without showing your plate.
- Use a photo of your own vehicle, or one whose owner said yes.
