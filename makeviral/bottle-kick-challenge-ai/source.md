## What is the Bottle Kick Challenge trend, and where did it start?

The **bottle kick challenge** is older than the AI clip. Martial arts gyms, families and comedians stack water bottles and try to kick the top one off without touching the rest; one 2019 try by @sebastianmoy has 5.2 million views. On TikTok the #bottlekickchallenge tag has 1,550 videos and #waterbottlekick 1,750 (24 October 2026).

In October 2026 it came back. On 6 October the Japanese comedian Wes-P (@uespiiiii.1115) posted his own stacked-bottle kick skit, which reached 1.8 million views and 189,300 likes. Then one AI clip took over: a thin man with a hair bun and a big moustache poses like a kung fu master next to two yellow bottles on a mosaic-tiled box in a sunlit riad, kicks, knocks everything over and limps off. A repost with the caption "Pravo musko" ("a real man" in Serbian and Bosnian) reached 2.97 million views and 90,800 shares on 13 October. Template tutorials soon called it the Bravo Musko bottle kick trend, and AI template sites added a bottle kick effect from 19 October. A pet version, where a cat or a dog stands up and kicks the bottle, runs alongside it.
