## What is the Steer Wrestling trend, and where did it start?

Steer wrestling, also called **bulldogging**, is a timed rodeo event: the rider gallops next to a running steer, slides off the horse, grabs the horns and brings the steer down onto its side.

The trend comes from a rodeo videographer in Canada who posts as **@reelwesternmedia**. On 27 August 2026 the account posted one slow-motion take of a steer wrestling run at an outdoor rodeo, filmed with a long lens from the arena dirt, captioned "Going down" and set to a hard rap track. By 10 November 2026 it had 4.2 million views, 346,900 likes and 14,800 shares. The same account had posted an earlier run, "Beast mode", on 18 July (649,600 views), and other rodeo videographers followed with their own slow-motion steer wrestling videos, such as @hanginhorses on 18 September (2.9 million views).

The AI side started in November: on 9 November 2026 a photo app began to run ads that put a user's face into the scene, with 11 ads by the next day. We found no AI creator version on TikTok yet, so this steer wrestling AI trend is still early. The hashtag #steerwrestling had 13,004 videos and 436.7 million views on 10 November.
