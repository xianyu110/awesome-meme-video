## What is the Tamagawa Koi Dance trend, and where did it start?

"多摩川で、恋…したい♡" is the 12th single of 一条ゆう (Yuu Ichijo), a Japanese singer who calls himself a "super idol of the music world" and writes his own songs. It came out on 30 September 2026 (FOMO). Yahoo! News describes a bittersweet love across the Tama River, between Tokyo and Kanagawa, set to a nostalgic melody like an old kayokyoku pop song.

The dance starts with his own post. On 4 October he filmed himself on a stony riverbank: he walks toward the camera, points left and right, turns his palms up, then crouches on the river stones and makes small hand hearts. That clip passed 14 million views. Covers came the next day (@masamidrums, "this song is way too addictive", 1.3 million views), and on 6 October the YouTuber duo パパラピーズ made the duo version with hand hearts and a twin pose (2.7 million). Many duo covers use its sound.

Members of the idol groups CUTIE STREET and M!LK posted the dance from 10 October (Yahoo! News). Most posts use #多摩川で恋したい or #多摩川で恋してみたい. AI versions followed: an AI poodle and cat in pink tutus on 8 October, a CapCut AI template on 14 October, AI cats on 18 October.

## What does 多摩川で恋したい mean, and who is 一条ゆう?

多摩川 (Tamagawa) is the Tama River, which runs between Tokyo and Kanagawa. "恋…したい" means "I want to fall in love", so the title reads "At the Tama River, I want to fall in love". That is the tamagawa de koi shitai meaning in one line. Most people remember it as 多摩川で恋してみたい ("I want to try falling in love at the Tama River"), which is why that name and its romaji, Tamagawa de koi shite mitai, are searched more than the real title. People also misspell it 多摩川に恋してみたい, and fans shorten it to 多摩恋.

一条ゆう (Yuu Ichijo) is the singer. He debuted in spring 2022 and writes, composes and programs his own songs. Searches like 一条ゆう とは, 一条ゆう 曲 and 一条ゆう 多摩川 jumped in October 2026 because of this one song.
