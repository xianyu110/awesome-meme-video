## C-Walk dance steps: what the AI copies

If you have looked for a C-Walk tutorial and given up, this is the short version. The C-Walk dance moves are footwork first: the upper body stays calm while the feet draw quick shapes on the floor.

- **Heel-toe:** one foot taps the heel, then the toe, as the other foot slides. This C-Walk heel toe move is the base of every combo.
- **V-step:** the feet step out wide and back in, so they draw a V on the floor.
- **Shuffle:** small, fast slides from side to side; the C-Walk shuffle keeps the rhythm between the bigger steps.
- **Crossover and turn:** one foot crosses in front of the other, and the dancer spins out of it.

How to C-Walk for real? Start slow with the heel-toe, add the V-step, then link them; any C-Walk for beginners video on YouTube or TikTok shows the same C-Walk steps.
