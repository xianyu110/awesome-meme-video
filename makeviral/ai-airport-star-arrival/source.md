### Which photo works best?

You need **one sharp photo**: of yourself for Human, of your pet for Pet.

- **Human:** waist up or full body, face turned to the camera, in good light. The still gives you the trend's star look: a white shirt, loose jeans, white sneakers, a chain and black sunglasses, so the clothes in your photo do not matter.
- **Hotel arrival:** a full-body photo works best, because you step out of the car in **the clothes from your photo**. Wear what you would arrive in: a suit, a dress, your best outfit. No sunglasses here, so your face stays clear.

- **Pet:** the whole animal in view, ears to paws, in daylight. Your pet gets small black sunglasses and walks between the bodyguards on all four paws, at the airport or at the hotel.

- Use your own photo, a photo of an adult who agreed, or your own pet. Never a celebrity or a stranger.

It is an AI video of a normal person walking out of airport arrivals like a celebrity: bodyguards clear the way, fans film with their phones, cameras flash and a fan begs for a photo. It started with a Seedance 2.0 prompt in April 2026 and came back in October 2026 as a CapCut AI template called "Celebrity airport arrival".

### Is the airport celebrity video real?

No, it is AI. Creators upload a photo of themselves, and the AI makes the airport, the crowd, the bodyguards and the cameras. The first clips were made with Seedance 2.0, and the October clips with a CapCut AI template.

### Which prompt do people use for the AI airport celebrity video?

A timed Seedance 2.0 prompt that splits the 15 seconds into five beats, from the crowd behind the barriers to the star getting into a car. Creators in Turkey, France and India shared it in April and May 2026. This template uses the same prompt, a little shorter and with the creators' names and the car make taken out, so you write no prompt.

### Is this the CapCut "Celebrity airport arrival" template?

No. The CapCut template is closed and shows no prompt. This is MakeViral's own template for the same scene, built on the prompt that the trend's creators shared in April and May 2026.

### How long is the video, and does it have sound?

15 seconds, vertical 9:16, as an MP4 file: 1080p with Standard, 720p with Best. It has its own sound: cheering, a fan shouting "Please, one photo!!", camera clicks, airport announcements and an engine. There is no music.

### Can I use a friend's photo or a celebrity's photo?

Use your own photo, or a photo of an adult who said yes. Never a celebrity or a stranger. The clip looks like real fan footage, so keep the AI label on when you post it and never present it as a real event.

### Why does my face look different in the video?

The model rebuilds you from one photo, and the camera moves through a crowd, so a dark, blurry or side-on photo leaves it guessing. A sharp, front-facing photo in daylight helps most. Every run is different, so a second run can look more like you.
