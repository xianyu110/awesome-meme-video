## What is the Love Options Dance trend, and where did it start?

"Love Options" (연애의 조건, "the conditions of love") came out in 2013, by the four-member girl group BESTie. In 2026 short-form video brought it back. The first TikTok post on its sound to pass a million views went up on 6 July: a "how I look vs what I'm listening to" meme over the group's old dance practice (3.6 million views). In July the dance spread in Chinese-speaking regions, and a late-July edit that put the song on another girl group's performance went viral in Korea (The Fact).

The Love Options trend became a hand-gesture challenge: fans copy the chorus moves to the "dudududu" hook and the line 저 정말 바라는 거 없어요 ("I really don't want much"). The Pyo brothers' duo cover on a park deck (13 September, 951,200 views) is the reel this page's Duo copies, and five days later an AI version took off: two AI-made cats doing the same routine (@kungseol, 551,300 views), the reel this page's Pet copies. By 28 September about 570,000 TikToks used the song (The Fact). On 19 October 2026 it stood at No. 47 on Melon, and the four members said they will reunite (NewsQ).
