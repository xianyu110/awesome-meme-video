### Which photo works best?

**Two close face photos, one of each of you.** Only one eye of each person shows in the result, so the eyes matter most.

- **Couple:** photo 1 is you, photo 2 is your partner. Your eye goes on the left, theirs on the right.
- **One photo:** one photo of you two together, faces close and both faces in full view.
- **With your pet:** photo 1 is you, photo 2 is a close photo of your pet's face.
- Shoot in daylight, face to the camera, **both eyes wide open** and sharp. A selfie at arm's length works well.
- No sunglasses, no colored contact lenses, no heavy filter: the eye color and the lashes from your photo stay.
- Only adults who said yes, or your own pet.

Then type **two letters**. The trend puts your partner's initial in your eye and your initial in theirs. One capital letter per eye; letters such as Ö, Ç or Ş work too.
