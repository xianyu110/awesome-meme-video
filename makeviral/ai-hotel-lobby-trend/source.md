The AI version started in September 2026. XXL traced it to a clip of two cats in the booth on TikTok in early September; Know Your Meme names a TikTok swap from 16 September as the first big one. Within a week, people had put friends, pets, film characters and tech founders into the booth, and one-click template apps followed. The most viral AI version we found is two AI-made cats by @therealmeowfia, posted on 23 September: 6 million views by 12 October. Takeoff died in 2022, and some fans asked people to keep the swaps respectful.
