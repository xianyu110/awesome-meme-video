## What is the GTA 6 Loading Screen trend, and where did it start?

The series has used painted character art for its loading screens for over 20 years. In 2025, people started to ask ChatGPT for a realistic portrait "in the art style of" a GTA loading screen, and prompt posts spread on the web.

The TikTok trend came from a CapCut AI template titled "Your GTA 6 loading screen". It shows your real photo first, then wipes to the painted poster. Its sound appeared on 27 August 2026. The first post with it that took off came from @m0rgan20 on 28 September 2026: "Life as a GTA character", a photo of him in a grey suit next to his car, then the poster version, with 30,900 views and 3,369 likes.

The big wave came in November, when the game's 19 November release date was set. @shadow_monarch_d posted "My loading screen" on 8 November (117,300 views), and another post on 10 November reached 198,100 views. By 15 November the template's sound had 8,931 videos, and tutorials (12 November) paired it with Morgan Wallen's "Last Thing You Need" from the game's album. On TikTok the GTA filter hashtag has 28,669 videos.

### Which photo works best?

Use **one sharp photo of you, from the waist or the knees up**, facing the camera or turned a little to it.

- **One person per photo.** For **Couple**, upload two photos: the first person stands on the left, the second on the right.
- **Pets:** for **Pet**, use a clear photo of your dog or cat with the whole face in view.
