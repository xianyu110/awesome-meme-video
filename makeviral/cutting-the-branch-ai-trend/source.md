## What is the Cutting the Branch trend, and where did it start?

"Don't cut the branch you're sitting on" is a proverb in many languages: do not destroy the thing that holds you up. Real fail clips of people doing exactly that have done the rounds for years. A YouTube Short titled "Man cuts the branch he sits on" went up on 9 May 2021, and on 25 December 2023 the X account @allreactionvids reposted a clip of a man chopping the tree he stands on above the water as a reaction meme. By 5 November 2026 that post had more than 760,000 views.

The 2026 wave runs on a new clip: one continuous phone shot of an older man who squats on a thin, slanted trunk over a calm green river and chops at the wood between his feet and the bank. Every chop makes the wood thinner, and then it cracks and he drops into the river with a big splash. There is no cut and no music, only the chopping, the crack, the splash and laughter. One AI video site says the clip took off as an Instagram Reel in late October 2026; we could not find that post. On 4 November 2026 three AI video sites added the cutting the branch AI trend as a one-photo template.
