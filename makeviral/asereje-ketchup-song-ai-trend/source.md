## What is the Asereje Lip Sync trend, and where did it start?

The Ketchup Song (Aserejé) by the Spanish group Las Ketchup came out on 10 June 2002 and reached number one in at least 20 European countries. Its story: Diego walks into a club, the DJ plays his favorite song, "Rapper's Delight", and Diego sings the chorus in made-up words. That gibberish is "Aserejé, ja, deje".

On TikTok the verse found a second life. On 9 May 2021 @_brenda.castillo_ posted the verse that starts "Mira lo que se avecina a la vuelta de la esquina, viene Diego rumbeando" with the words on screen, asking "¿Las recuerdas?". Her "sonido original" became the trend sound: on 4 November 2026 it is in 51,200 videos.

The lip-sync took off in waves. In January 2025 selfie lip-syncs and street dances on the sound passed 2 to 4 million views each, and in 2026 it came back again: a lyrics post by @th1ngs.celeb (1.2 million views, 24 June) and the car selfie of @widaineryss (2.5 million views, 23 August), the clip this template copies. The #asereje hashtag now holds 102,555 TikTok videos and 1.44 billion views.
