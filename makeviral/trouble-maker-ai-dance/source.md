## What is the Trouble Maker Dance trend, and where did it start?

"Trouble Maker" came out on 1 December 2011 as the debut of the K-pop duo Trouble Maker: HyunA of 4Minute and Hyunseung of BEAST. Its stage routine, a close and teasing duet, became one of the best known K-pop couple dances (many people search for it as the trouble maker hyuna dance), and the pair even toned down part of the choreography after criticism of the close moves.

The Trouble Maker dance is not a new trend: it is an evergreen dance cover. On TikTok, a friends duo by @sunlight_mk reached 2.0 million views in November 2023, a studio cover by @yiienittko.o 1.9 million views in January 2024, and a man and woman cover by @a_kim.i 1.9 million views and 276,200 likes in February 2024.
