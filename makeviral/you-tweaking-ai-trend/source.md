## What is the You Tweaking trend, and where did it start?

The clip comes from a "20 vs 1" dating show on YouTube, filmed in a plain white studio in July 2024. A man asks a woman to do a 360. She turns, shows off her red handbag and smiles, and he bites his fist and can't stop laughing. She says "Oh, he tweaking", he asks "I'm tweaking, how?", and she answers "You tweaking." Reposts spread it on TikTok within days: one posted on 5 July 2024 passed 3.3 million views, one on 19 July passed 7.6 million, and another on 25 July, captioned "what is that laugh", passed 6.8 million views and 1 million likes. People still search it as the oh he tweakin meme, the he tweaking meme or the you tweaking meme.

In October 2026 it came back as an AI trend. Three AI video sites added a two-photo template of the scene between 20 and 22 October, and an AI app's own version with two TV-drama characters passed 200,000 likes on Instagram on 21 October. From 22 October, phone-template videos call it the "oh you can make it cap" trend, after a misheard line from the start of the clip. Ours is a new scene made from your photos: never the show clip, and never the two people from the show.
