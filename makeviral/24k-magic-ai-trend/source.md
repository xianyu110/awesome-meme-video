## What is the 24K Magic AI trend, and where did it start?

In Hotel Transylvania 3: Summer Vacation (2018), Dracula grins, tugs his collar and dances alone on the deck of a cruise ship to "24K Magic", the 2016 Bruno Mars song, while his friend watches from a corridor. A YouTube upload of that Hotel Transylvania 24K Magic scene, "Looking Good Dracula", has 818,396 views.

The scene came back on TikTok on 11 October 2026: a phone clip of it by @jst.daf has 6.7 million views. A day later, on 12 October, the AI creator @theraguler posted a live-action AI remake, shot for shot, with two real-looking young men on a real cruise deck. It has 5.1 million views and 957,200 likes by 2 November, and its comments are a long fight over whether it is AI or CGI. Google searches for "hotel transylvania dance" hit their 12-month high that same week.

On 31 October an Argentine AI remake reached 858,200 views, a CapCut template for the 24K Magic AI dance trend went out, and "how I made the 24K Magic AI dance" tutorials started the same day. Spanish speakers call it Dracula bailando en el crucero, the Dracula cruise ship dance; others call it the 24K Magic deck dance.
