## What is the Olly Olly Dance trend, and where did it start?

The sound came first. On 20 October 2026 the producer OLLY (@prodbyolly) posted his beat with the caption "really?", and the post had 470,900 views by 15 November. On 26 October the Baltimore creator @j.xrdan1 posted a dance to it with "DC: me", the dance credit, and other dancers started tagging him. Captions shout the producer's name, "OLLY!" or "OLLY OLLY", which named the Olly Olly trend and its dance (also spelled Ollie Ollie).

The trick is the exit: between the moves the dancer sprints out of the frame and back, so the clip loops. That made it a meme. On 8 November @minicaseoh6741 captioned his take "How fast I leave when she starts talking about her ex" (455,000 views), and the line stuck to the trend. The AI side started on 2 November, when @reqdeca posted an AI cartoon version with the cast of the 2000s cartoon Danny Phantom doing the dance, credited to @j.xrdan1: 938,700 views and 162,400 likes by 15 November. By then 12,700 TikTok videos used the sound.
