## What is the Toosie Slide trend, and where did it start?

Drake released "Toosie Slide" on 3 April 2020, at the start of the lockdowns, with a dance built for TikTok. The song is named after the Atlanta dancer Toosie, who made the routine with Hiii Key and Ayo & Teo. The lyrics are the dance steps ("right foot up, left foot slide"), and the challenge was already on TikTok on 31 March 2020, before the release. In the music video, Drake does the slide alone in his Toronto mansion, in dark clothes, black gloves and a face covering, with a grand piano in the living room.

The toosie slide challenge became one of the biggest TikTok dances of 2020. On 14 April 2020, the singer and actor NDJ (@nathandavisjrndj) posted his take in a hotel hallway: 4.25 million views. The hashtag #toosieslide holds 619,882 videos and 6.93 billion views (24 October 2026), and the slide is also a Fortnite emote.

The AI wave is new. On 23 October 2026, an AI clip of a TV character doing the Toosie Slide in a mansion went around Instagram, and two AI video sites added a Toosie Slide template the same day. The demand is still small: TikTok shows no AI tutorial yet.
