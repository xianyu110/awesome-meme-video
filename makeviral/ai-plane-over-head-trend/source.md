## What is the Plane Over Your Head trend, and where did it start?

Clips of planes flying very low went viral on TikTok in late summer 2026. On 27 August a hairdresser posted "I still can't believe how low this plane was", a joke that ends with a paper plane advertising her salon; it reached 26.9 million views. On 10 September **@livelyiqra**, who posts aviation videos from London, filmed a selfie as a double-decker airliner passed low over her head. It got 4.9 million views and 529,000 likes, and its sound later played under many of the AI tutorials.

The AI version took off in late September as a one-photo CapCut AI template: you upload a picture and a jet flies right over your head. The first tutorials we found are from 25 September, in Vietnamese and English, then came a Filipino one with 838,300 views and a Burmese one with 796,800, whose screen showed the template at 301,900 uses. On 28 September ByteDance's Dreamina added a "Sunset-Aviation" skill with the same look: you on the guardrail of an airport viewing deck at dusk, waving at a low airliner. A second wave of Russian and Spanish tutorials passed 1.1 million and 947,800 views on 10 October.

- One person or one pet per photo.
- Use your own photo, a photo of an adult who agreed, or your own pet. Never a celebrity or a stranger.
