## What is the Closed on Sunday trend, and where did it start?

"Closed on Sunday" is a track from Kanye West's gospel album *Jesus Is King* (25 October 2019); he now goes by Ye. Its official video, directed by Jake Schreier and released on 28 November 2019, ends with the Sunday Service Choir of his Kanye Sunday Service gatherings around him on a hill while he stands still. That scene is the trend. The Closed on Sunday music video has 54.7 million views on YouTube.

The AI trend started on 14 October 2026, when TikTok creators used Higgsfield Genjutsu, a motion transfer tool, to put someone new in the center of that scene. The first AI version we found, by @bellvtrix_ai, turned the choir into fluffy cats around a kitten knight with angel wings. By 15 October an AI template site had a Closed on Sunday template, and one AI video app was advertising it.

People search for the trend by the song: "closed on sundays kanye" gets about 260 US searches a month, and many type "close on sunday kanye". In Google Trends, "closed on sunday kanye" jumped on 15 October and peaked on 17 October, and "closed on sunday ai" is now the second Google suggestion for "closed on sunday".

**Human:** use a **sharp, front-facing photo** from the waist up, with your face in good light. The video starts close on your face and then pulls back over the crowd, so a clear face matters most.

## Why the Closed on Sunday crowd works

It is all contrast, and that is why the Closed on Sunday meme travels. Hundreds of people move at once, bobbing, raising their hands and singing, and the one person in the middle does nothing. In the original video for the song, that still figure is Kanye himself, surrounded by the Sunday Service Choir in plain robes on open land.
