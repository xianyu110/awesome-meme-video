## What is the TOESA Quit Job trend, and where did it start?

It came out on 7 September 2026. Toesa (퇴사) is the Korean word for leaving your company, and the song is a cheerful goodbye to the office: "모두 안녕히 계세요" ("goodbye, everyone"), then the chorus that ends on "It was a romantic day". On 18 September it topped Melon, Genie, Vibe, FLO and Bugs at the same time, an all-kill (Sports Kyunghyang), and on 11 October it took its fourth music show trophy on Inkigayo (Soompi). By 17 October it had been the No. 1 song on Korean YouTube Shorts every day for 40 days.

The I'm gonna TOESA challenge (퇴사할게여 챌린지) started with the release: office workers dancing the point move at their desks, "last day at work" clips, and covers outdoors, like @jini_0511's sunset take in a long white dress (25 September, 107,800 views), the example on this page. ### What does toesa mean?
