## What is the Beer Jacket trend, and where did it start?

The beer jacket started as AI art. In early November 2025, short AI clips of a see-through puffer jacket filled with golden beer and foam spread on Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest, with captions like "The future of beer is wearable". People asked where to buy one, and fact-checkers answered that it does not exist: a blog post of 6 November 2025 called it digital art with millions of views and no real sightings, and a Lead Stories fact check of 11 November 2025 called a beer-filled puffer parka photo a hoax. The beer puffer jacket video became a meme, and from 7 November 2025 tutorials (one by @l3qeq4rjn8 had 83,700 views) showed how to put your own photo in it.

Football fans took the joke next. During the 2026 World Cup, an AI clip of an England fan with a St George flag who tries to walk into a World Cup game in a beer jacket went around, and on 28 June 2026 a sports channel in Costa Rica even showed it as its image of the day. A reaction post of it, "England fan tries to sneak into World Cup game with a beer jacket", reached 470,300 views on 21 August 2026.

Then it became a phone editor AI template. On 20 July 2026 the Filipino account @aprilynn posted the template post "Trending beer suit AI effect": one photo in, and a woman walks through a cafe in a giant beer mug suit with a foam collar and a glass handle. In October 2026 Spanish-language promoters pushed it as the traje de cerveza IA, the chaqueta de cerveza.
