## What is the Gas Station Dance trend, and where did it start?

The clip is real footage. On 1 July 2024, the blogger Adel Veigel (@adelveigel) posted it on Instagram with the caption "tag the one you want to fly off into the sunset with". On a long drive, she and her friends, the Russian blogger Mikhail Litvin and the streamer Ravshan Dzhulpaev, stop on the parking lot of a roadside gas station. Ravshan dances to the car stereo, Litvin films himself, and Adel climbs out of the car. The song is "Stay with Me" by the Romanian group Akcent, from 2009. By 12 October 2026 the reel had 1.65 million likes.

In late September 2026 the clip came back on TikTok, first as reposts (a Turkish repost of the longer original passed 1.4 million views), then as AI versions. From 24 September, CapCut templates, and later Dreamina ones, let people put themselves and their friends into the three parts, Akcent's own account posted about the trend, and on 2 October the Italian news site Sky TG24 called the AI versions "the trend of the moment".
