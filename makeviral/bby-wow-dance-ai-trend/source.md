## What is the BbY WOW Dance trend, and where did it start?

The dance started on TikTok on 24 August 2026, when @bigbowee posted two friends doing it with a red cup in hand and the caption "Dime dime baby", the words of the song's hook. That clip had 15.3 million views by 14 October. In the same days, clips of the creator known as Maestro Shifu doing the moves spread fast, and many tutorials credit the dance to him, so people also call it the Shifu dance. Friends, coworkers and dance schools copied it through September: a trio by @khristelaldama passed 14 million views, and a slowed, mirrored tutorial by @singsingaduck reached 4.5 million. KAROL G posted the dance with her crew on 9 October.

AI versions came early. On 15 September @weendycruzmx posted two AI-made cats doing the routine in a shopping mall (391,500 views by 15 October). In October an older party clip came back with the song: a young man at a quinceañera bites a sweet bread, stares at the camera with full cheeks and dances off. Green screen templates of this "bby wow guy" meme went up on 12 and 13 October. By 14 October, #bbywow had 109,241 TikTok videos and 848.5 million views.
