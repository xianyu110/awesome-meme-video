### Which photo works best?

Use **one sharp photo of yourself from the waist up**, face to the camera. The prompts keep your face and hair from the photo and change the outfit, the set and the light, so a clear face is what matters most.

- **Daylight from the front,** near a window. A dark or backlit face comes out flat.
- **No sunglasses, no hat, no hood.** Clear glasses stay on.
- **Look into the camera** with a relaxed smile. Every version is a camera-facing portrait.
- **Turn off beauty filters.** A smoothed photo comes back smoothed.
- **Couple or friends:** one photo of each person, you first. The first photo sits on the left.
- **Pet:** your dog or cat whole, face to the camera, ears in view, not blurry.
- **Adults only,** and only photos of you or of people who said yes. Never a stranger or a celebrity.

## Does the AI change my face?

It must not, and that is the main risk with AI Christmas pictures from any tool: a face that only looks a little like you, smoother skin, or a new hairstyle. Two of the viral prompts even asked for a new hairstyle ("long balayage wavy hair" in the first one), which changes how you look.
