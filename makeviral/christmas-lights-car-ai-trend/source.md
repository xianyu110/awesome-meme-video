## What is the Christmas Lights Car trend, and where did it start?

Putting Christmas lights on a car is a real TikTok trend first. The #christmaslightsoncar tag dates from December 2021, and the big wave came in November and December 2024: @m4dave's "Christmas Came Early" on 13 November reached 512,000 views, and @jpflect's car wrapped in Christmas lights on 4 December reached 4.8 million views and 528,300 likes, the most viewed post we found. In December 2024 police in Wyoming and in York County, Pennsylvania, told drivers to keep the lights on their houses.

The AI version followed. On 20 December 2024 @intiszene posted an AI-made sports car covered in glowing lights, "AI generated" (27,700 views), and in December 2025 CapCut effects that "turn your car into a christmas light show" and ChatGPT posts like @billyboyks21's "Make my car look like it parked in the snow with Christmas lights" spread. The Christmas lights car trend came back early in 2026: "Christmas cars are back", @distortionstreet posted on 1 November (531,200 views), and the next day the first AI clip of the new season showed cars on snow with every line traced in lights.
