## What can go wrong, and how to fix it

- **The face looks a little different.** Run it again: each run is a little different. A sharp, well-lit photo facing the camera helps most.
- **The flash look is strong on Retro 2000s.** That is the style of an early-2000s compact camera. If your skin looks too bright, try Cozy autumn, which uses soft light.
- **A leaf covers part of your face.** The prompt keeps the leaves at the edges, but it can still happen: run it again.
- **The turkey hides your chin.** Use a photo from the chest up, not a close-up of the face, so there is room for the table.
- **Matching or Couple mixed you up.** Check the photo order: photo 1 goes on top (Matching) or on the left (Couple). One person per photo.
- **A pet sweater looks odd.** Use a photo where the pet sits or stands, with its chest in view.
