## What is the Mi Chico Dance trend, and where did it start?

"Mi Chico" is a song by the Romanian DJ and producer DJ Goja. It took off on TikTok in spring 2026, and a remix with Jason Derulo and Melody came out on 26 June 2026, with a music video shot in Paris (Wikipedia, Songfacts).

The dance spread long before the remix. On 27 April 2026 a Vietnamese dance studio duo posted it (@bobo_le, 644,000 views by 17 October), and dancers in Korea, the Philippines and the UK followed. Jason Derulo's "easiest way to learn the #MiChico dance" post of 23 June has 7.9 million views, and slowed, mirrored tutorials taught the steps.

AI versions started almost at once: the earliest we found is an AI cat in glasses on 13 April, and on 2 May an AI pink bunny in rhinestone sunglasses danced it to 70,700 views (@highonbubbles), the reel this page's Pet copies. An AI crew of kittens copying a boy band's rooftop routine, posted on 30 September, has 1.2 million views by 24 October (@humantix), and AI crocodiles and dogs followed. By 17 October 2026, #michico had 81,708 TikTok videos and 948.0 million views, and AI video sites added Mi Chico templates on 15 and 16 October.

The crew format leads this second wave. Friends lean in over a phone that lies on the ground, one grabs it for a selfie, then the whole crew dances the chorus on a rooftop parking lot at sunset. The AI kittens were the first AI crew of it; the same creator's crocodiles and puppies followed (852,800 and 471,900 views by 24 October), then crews of AI-made idols (@caisarttfelix, 112,600 views since 11 October), and on 22 and 23 October AI video sites added Mi Chico crew templates for groups of friends. By 24 October 2026, #michico had 82,391 TikTok videos and 962.3 million views.

## Mi Chico meaning, and who sings the Mi Chico song

What is the Mi Chico meaning? "Mi chico" is Spanish for "my boy" or "my guy", the way you would say "my boyfriend" in a song; that is the whole Mi Chico meaning in English. Some AI sites even list the trend as the "My Boy" dance, and the TikTok tags call it the Michico challenge too.

The Mi Chico song is by DJ Goja, a DJ and producer from Romania; the TikTok sound "Mi Chico" by DJ Goja is the one most early dance reels use.
