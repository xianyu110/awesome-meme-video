## What is the AI Hug trend, and where did it start?

The AI hug is older than most AI video trends. In the fall of 2024, TikTok users put two photos side by side, asked an image-to-video site to "make them hug", and posted the result; Dexerto wrote a how-to on 1 October 2024. In September 2025 a new version spread on Instagram: **"hug my younger self"**, where people used an AI image model to make a photo of their adult self hugging the child they used to be. Storyboard18 covered it on 16 September 2025.

### Which photo works best?

Use **two photos, one person in each**, from the waist or knees up. The prompt places both people in one park photo from the knees up, so a tight face crop leaves the model guessing about the body and the clothes.

- Sharp, front-facing faces in good light. Both faces must stay recognizable after the hug turns them.
- Similar light in both photos helps the two people look like they stand in the same place.
- Plain clothes work best. The prompt keeps each outfit, and busy patterns can blend when the two bodies touch.
- Use only photos of yourself and of adults who agreed to the video.

## How do you make the "hug my younger self" video?

Use the same prompts with a different pair of photos: an **old photo of yourself as a child** first, and a **recent photo of yourself** second. The photo step places both of you in the park, and the video step makes you walk toward each other and hug. Upload order does not change the hug, so either order works.

Tips for this version:

- Scan or photograph the old print flat, with no glare. A sharp old photo keeps your child face.
- If the child comes back as tall as the adult, add "the first person is a small child about 7 years old" to the photo prompt and generate the still again.
- In the video prompt, change "a warm, tight, friendly hug" to "the adult kneels down and hugs the child" for a more natural height.

It is the same idea as the 2025 Instagram trend, but as a moving video with sound, not only a still.

## Why do AI hug videos get so many shares?

A hug is short, clear and emotional. Viewers understand it in one second, and the payoff comes fast. The format also has a built-in reason to share: the other person in the video wants to repost it.

The most shared versions give the hug a story. Best friends who live in different countries. A parent and a grown child. Your adult self and your seven-year-old self. Put the story in the caption, for example "we haven't seen each other in four years", and let the video carry the feeling.

## What goes wrong, and how do you fix it?

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The two faces blend into one | Use sharper photos with one person each, then generate the still again. |
| One face changes when it turns during the hug | Common in hug clips. A second run often fixes it; so does a still where both faces are clear. |
| Arms or hands merge | Plain clothes help. Generate the video again, or try Veo 3.1 Fast. |
| The people look stiff | Add "relaxed" and "natural" to the video prompt. |
| The video model refuses the photo | Some models refuse some real faces or some photos. The credits come back; try another model. |

## Do you need permission to make an AI hug video of someone?

Yes. **Both people in the video must agree.** A hug video of a real person who did not say yes can break platform rules and that person's rights, and it can hurt. Use yourself, adults who agreed, or your own childhood photos.

Do not use photos of celebrities, strangers, or someone you are not in contact with. For photos of children, use only your own childhood photos or photos you have the parent's permission for, and keep the prompt wholesome, as ours is.
