## What is the Pumpkin Head trend, and where did it start?

Know Your Meme and the Daily Dot trace the pumpkin head photoshoot (also spelled pumpkin head photo shoot) back to at least the fall of 2020: friends, couples and families hollowed out big pumpkins, wore them over their heads and posed outdoors, often to the song "Oh Klahoma" by Jack Stauber. It exploded on TikTok in October 2021, where it also became known as the pumpkin head challenge. By 27 October that year, #pumpkinheadphotoshoot had more than 52.4 million views, and US Google searches for "pumpkin head photoshoot" reached their highest point of the past five years.

The pumpkin head photo trend has come back every October since. The 2026 wave started early: a family version posted on 25 September passed 7.4 million plays, and a couple walking hand in hand down a tree-lined road passed 6.1 million after 30 September. On 8 October 2026 the TikTok tag had 33,361 videos and 328.2 million views. AI tools began to offer pumpkin head templates in 2025, so now you can do it without a real pumpkin: an AI photo generator like this one makes the pumpkin head picture from one photo.

## Real pumpkin or AI: which is easier?

A real pumpkin head shoot looks simple, but the guides warn about the same few problems:

- **Weight.** "Make sure you pick the lightest one", says Kate Backdrop's guide, which also tells you to measure your head and add about 12 inches. Cienna So's guide: "Go tall and light so your shoulders carry the weight, not your neck", and "If it feels heavy, stop." One creator's verdict in October 2025: "That head is heavvvvy".
- **Carving.** You cut off the bottom, scoop out as much of the inside as you can (the Daily Dot also suggests a shower cap), then cut eye holes wide enough to see and breathe.
- **Fire.** No real candle near your face: guides suggest fake candle lights for the glow, and Shutter Bombs says never to put smoke in a pumpkin someone is wearing.
- **Light and place.** You need a field, a patch or a road, and good light at the right hour.

The AI way is one photo of you in the outfit, one tap, and the pumpkin, the field and the dusk light come from the prompt. Real shoots also hide the skin inside the eye holes with a black mask; the AI version shows only the glow.

## What goes wrong with an AI pumpkin head photo, and how do you fix it?

These are the usual weak points of AI pumpkin heads, and what our prompt does about each one.

| What goes wrong | What the prompt does about it |
|---|---|
| The pumpkin floats above the shoulders or looks pasted on | Its bottom sits right on the shoulders and the collar, with a soft shadow, "so it clearly looks worn and not floating" |
| The pumpkin is head-sized and looks like a mask | It asks for a big pumpkin, "clearly bigger than a human head", like a real one you wear |
| Skin or a face shows through the eye holes | "Inside the holes there is only that glow, never skin or a face" |
| Your outfit changes color or shape | It keeps the clothes, colors, fabrics, shoes and accessories exactly as in your photo |
| Extra or melted fingers | It asks for five fingers on each hand and keeps the hands simple, on the knee |
| It looks like CGI | 35mm film, natural grain, muted colors: "a real photo, not CGI" |
