## What is the NEON Duo Dance trend, and where did it start?

"NEON" is the debut single of A2O LITE, a new K-pop girl group from A2O Entertainment, the company of SM founder Lee Soo-man. It came out on 13 October 2026 and reached No. 1 on the Melon TOP100 29 hours later (SBS). The song opens with a duo routine, and the choreographer J-Black said on Instagram on 14 October that he helped create that opening duo sequence (SBS). Fans call the one-legged turn in it the "killing part".

The NEON dance challenge started the next day. On 14 October dancers posted the first covers: a solo take at night by @way_hyx (266,100 views) and a duo take in a glass-roofed atrium by @wendyyyxi and a friend (92,300 views), the reel this page's Duo copies. The same morning the first AI version took off: "A2O MEOW", two AI-made cats dancing the intro upright in a lavender studio (@rroyhew54, 27,500 views), the reel this page's Pet copies. AI clips of film and TV characters followed (SBS). On TikTok the group's name is often typed A20 LITE, with a zero. By 17 October 2026, #a2olite had 4,656 videos and 136.9 million views.
