## What is the Ryuk AI trend, and where did it start?

Ryuk is the shinigami, a god of death, in *Death Note*, the manga by Tsugumi Ohba and Takeshi Obata that began in 2003 and became an anime in 2006. He drops his notebook into the human world because he is bored, follows its new owner everywhere and loves apples. That is the joke of every Ryuk photo: a tall, grinning death god who stands right behind someone and never leaves.

Fans have put him behind themselves for years. A cosplay clip from April 2023, "Ryuk only wants your apples", has 6.6 million TikTok views, and a CapCut tutorial from November 2025 for the "Ryuk behind you" shadow edit has 4.2 million. From late 2025, people made the photos with AI image tools instead.

The current wave started on 8 October 2026, when @capcut.ed1t5 posted a tutorial "to turn a simple photo into a Ryuk Spotter Portrait" with the prompt on screen. AI template sites added a "Ryuk spotted" template from 13 October, and on 18 October @jted1t posted the same prompt as the "Ryuk Companion AI Trend". With Halloween on 31 October 2026, it is a fast costume-free Halloween post.
