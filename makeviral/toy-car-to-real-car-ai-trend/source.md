## What is the Toy Car to Real Car trend, and where did it start?

Toy cars turning into real cars is an old edit idea. The train window version is brand new. On 19 October 2026 the Turkish video editor @umutcancelikis posted a reel that starts on a train at sunset: a tiny blue sports sedan stands on the window ledge while the hills slide past. The train goes into a tunnel, the toy's tail lights switch on, the window frame slides away, the ledge becomes the road, and the car drives out of the tunnel as the real thing, ending with clips of the real car. The reel got 341,300 likes and 2,400 comments in about a day. Fans asked in Turkish: "bu nasıl geçiş?", how is this transition made?

The set-up itself was already around: toy car fans film their diecast cars on train windows, with the landscape rushing past behind them. The AI part is what is new: the toy car comes to life and leaves the train.
