## What is the Konum Gönderin Captain trend, and where did it start?

The meme comes from a halı saha match video of Kerimcan Durmaz, the Turkish social media star, filmed for the YouTube football show The Big 4. A re-upload from 25 May 2018 has 913,000 views. Before kick-off he hands out the positions with full confidence: "Ben mevkileri belirliyorum. Sen kalecisin. Sen sağ bek." A player asks where that is, and he answers, "Ne bileyim, sağda bir yer" ("how should I know, somewhere on the right"). Then comes the punchline: "Top bana gelirse konum gönderin, ona göre hareket edeceğim" ("if the ball comes to me, send me the location and I'll move accordingly"). A 15-second cut titled "Sen sağbek ben solbek" followed in 2021, and a TikTok repost of the match by @moodluk.x has 9.3 million views.

In October 2026 the scene came back as an AI trend. AI edits put Turkey's national team players into the circle with the original audio, and from 15 October an AI video app ran Turkish ads for a one-photo version of the scene. ## What does "top bana gelirse konum gönderin" mean?

It means "if the ball comes to me, send me the location", as if the pitch were a city and the ball a friend you are meeting. The full line ends "ona göre hareket edeceğim": "and I'll move accordingly". Some captions quote it as "top bana gelirse telefonuma konum gönderin", send the location to my phone.

The joke works because of the build-up. "Ben mevkileri belirliyorum" sounds like a coach's speech, and "sen kalecisin, sen sağ bek" sounds like he knows the game. Then the right back asks "Orası neresi?", and the captain has no idea either. That is why people still quote "sen sağ bek ben sol bek" and "sen sağ bek sen sol bek" years later, and why so many people search what it means (sen sağ bek ben sol bek ne demek). In Turkish football, sağ bek is the right back, sol bek the left back, kaleci the goalkeeper and forvet the striker: the halı saha mevkileri, the positions of a five-a-side team.

Why halı saha? It is the Turkish name for the small artificial-turf pitches, literally "carpet pitch", where groups of friends book an hour after work. Almost everyone in Turkey has played on one, so a Turkish football meme from a halı saha travels fast, and a halı saha komik video lands in every group chat before the weekly match.
