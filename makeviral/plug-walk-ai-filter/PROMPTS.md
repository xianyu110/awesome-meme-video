# Plug Walk Filter: Take the Dollar, Then Do the Plug Walk

- Source: [makeviral](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/plug-walk-ai-filter)
- Slug: `plug-walk-ai-filter`

The plug walk filter is the AI video trend where a hand passes you a dollar out of a car window, you take it with a grin, step back and do the plug walk dance, the loose-knee strut from the plug walk meme.

```text

If [Image 1] shows an animal, the performer is that animal standing upright on its hind legs, with its own face, fur and markings, not a person.

The place: a quiet street corner at a red traffic light at sunset: a gravel strip by the curb, a grey light pole, palm trees and round green bushes, a low wall and a parked car far behind, a warm orange and lilac sky. The car door, the open window frame and the side mirror show at the edges of the frame; no badge on the car.

0-1.5 s: through the windshield, the person stands on the corner by the light pole a few steps away. The camera pans to the open driver's window.
1.5-3 s: the person walks up to the open window. The driver's hand reaches out of the window holding one green one-dollar bill.
3-6 s: the person takes the bill, leans in close with a big surprised grin, looks into the lens and laughs: "No way, thank you!" The whole face is large and sharp: exactly the face from [Image 1].
6-7.5 s: they point at the camera, turn and step back three steps onto the gravel, then turn to face the car.
7.5-12 s: the beat drops and they dance a loose, bouncy side-to-side strut on the beat: knees bent wide, weight rocking from one foot to the other, shoulders rolling, arms swinging low and crossing at the chest, the bill in one hand, a cocky grin. The whole body is in view, feet on the gravel; the camera stays in the driver's seat.

It is a friendly tip at a red light, like a fun dare: golden daylight, an open street, the driver's hand and the bill always in full view, everyone smiling. Only ONE green one-dollar bill changes hands: no stack of cash, no bags, no packages, no envelopes, nothing else.

```
