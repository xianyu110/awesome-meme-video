## What is the Headbop Trend trend, and where did it start?

The headbop is an edit, not an AI effect. Editors cut out heads, line them up on black and keyframe each one to bop on the beat in CapCut or Alight Motion. Anime editors have made them for years: the #headbopanimation tag has been on TikTok since May 2022.

The new wave started on 28 September 2026, when @.carvaa posted a "family" of fighter jets bopping in a row. The post had 1.9 million views and 300,900 likes by 13 October, and its sound was in about 3,500 videos, with about 290 new ones a day. A second sound followed on 5 October with a row of cartoon ponies (1.8 million views), and others posted rows of cars, vegetables and meme cats the same way.

On 10 October @popo_hrtz posted a CapCut template with four meme cats (589,500 views and 107,300 likes by 13 October), and how-to videos showed how to put your own cut-out photos in the row. A "family's headpop" how-to from 4 October had 516,200 views. You still had to cut out every head yourself. This template does the edit with AI, straight from normal photos.
