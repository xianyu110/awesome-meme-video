## What can go wrong, and how to fix it

- **The cartoon does not look like you.** The face is a simple cartoon face by design, so your likeness comes from the hair, glasses, beard and clothes. A photo where those show clearly, in daylight, works best. Run it again if a detail is missing: each run is a little different.
- **Glasses or a beard went missing.** It happens more with a far photo. Use a photo where your face fills more of the frame.
- **Odd letters in the title.** AI pictures sometimes misspell a word. Run it again, or crop the title off.
- **A group photo lost a person.** Use a photo where every face is clear and nobody is cut off at the edge, with four people at most.
- **Your dog looks like the beagle.** A white dog with dark ears can look close to it. The prompt keeps your dog's own colors and ears, but if it still looks off, use a photo where its face and markings show clearly.
