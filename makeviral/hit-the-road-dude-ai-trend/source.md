## What is the Hit the Road Dude trend, and where did it start?

The clip is a phone video from late August 2025. A man who stopped to fish at a pond films an older driver who pulls up in a white pickup truck and asks who told him he could fish there. "I thought it was state property," the man says. The driver answers: "State? This? State? It ain't. Hit the road, dude. This ain't no state property. This is private property."

It spread at once. The #hittheroaddude hashtag was created on 26 August 2025, and two reposts of the clip from early September 2025 now have more than 3 million views each.

A year later the meme came back with pets. On 25 September 2026 @zenoxlayla posted a dog in the driver's seat of a white pickup, acting out the scene to the original audio; it passed 1.6 million views. More dogs followed, then AI versions: on 1 October @oddframeco posted an AI-made bulldog in a camo cap at the wheel, the clip the Pet version copies. By mid-October two remixes of the clip had more than 8 million views each.
