## What is the Mirror Panther trend, and where did it start?

The pantera negra en el espejo photo spread on TikTok in the second half of October 2026, mostly through Spanish-language how-to videos by creators in the US and Mexico. The earliest big one we found was posted on 17 October by @mg8111195, titled "Cómo hacer la foto viral con una pantera negra en el espejo" (86,100 views by 31 October). @migf149 posted the same how-to on 18 October (182,000 views), and @fflora77 in Mexico reached 410,500 views on 20 October, the biggest post so far. No post had passed 1 million views by 31 October.

The caption is the hook. "Si tú eres la que resuelve, ¿a qué le tienes miedo?" means "If you are the one who handles everything, what are you afraid of?", and creators tag it #mujeresfuertes: the panther is the strength behind the woman who solves everyone's problems. ## What can go wrong

Mirrors are hard for image models. Now and then the reflection does not line up with your hand, the panther shows up outside the mirror, or a second hand appears. The prompt bans each of these, but if a run looks off, run it again: each run is a new photo.

If your face looks different, use a sharper photo with your face toward the camera. In the Catrina look, the heavy skull makeup covers much of the face, so your eyes, nose, lips and hair carry the likeness. The prompt keeps them from your photo.
