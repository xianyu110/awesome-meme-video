## What is the Sniffer Dog Meme trend, and where did it start?

The meme comes from one TikTok account, @stitchthesnitchh, which posts as a working dog that "catches ravers". The earliest clip we found is from 20 May 2026. The format took off on 17 July with "Caught this raver in Ibiza…": the dog sits down at a young man's feet, he bursts into tears, and the caption reads "bro is so cooked 😭". By 13 October that post had 64.4 million views and 6.9 million likes, and the account's most viewed clip, from 25 August, had 67.6 million.

None of it is real. On 27 August the AI-spotting creator @jeremyfindsai showed that the dog changes from video to video, with a short tail in one clip and a longer tail in another. His post had 2 million views, and many of the top comments came from people who had believed it. CapCut template versions went up from 19 July, but copies by other people stayed rare, so most viewers have only seen the account's own clips.
