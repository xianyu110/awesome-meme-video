### Which photo works best?

Use **one sharp photo of one pet**, with its face, both eyes and its ears in view. The AI keeps the pose, the room and the light of your photo, so the photo you take is the scene you get.

- **Face to the camera, at its eye level.** The viral posts are front shots of a dog or cat sitting or standing on a couch or the floor.
- **The whole pet in frame** works best for the Santa suit: from the ears to the paws.
- **Daylight, no motion blur.** Long or dark fur needs good light to show the face under the green.
- **Collars, harnesses and sweaters** are replaced by the Santa suit.
- **One pet per photo.** For two pets, make one each.
- **Human version:** one adult, chest up, face clear. Adults and pets only, never a child's photo, and only people who said yes.
