## What is the Bulin 47 Dance trend, and where did it start?

"Dame hombro" means "give me shoulder". The line came out of the Bulin 47 freestyle that went viral in early October 2026, and it became a dembow song by the Dominican artist Bulin 47 with Kevin Wai. On 8 October the artist's own TikTok sound of the song went up, and the Dame Hombro challenge tag started the same day.

The dance came two days later. On 10 October @dir.labourgraph posted a three-man line led by the creator El Pio: a tight routine of hand rolls, shoulder bounces and arm sweeps to the song. It had 1.4 million views by 22 October. Then the Bulin 47 challenge spread to solo takes in kitchens and parks, and couples. The most viewed take we found, a couple's "Dembow desde Corea" from 14 October, had 7.5 million views.

The AI versions started on 11 October. Tutorials called "Baila igual que Bulin 47" (dance just like Bulin 47) and "Pio bailando como Bulin 47" (Pio dancing like Bulin 47) turn one photo into a dancer, or put three photos into the crew line. Searches also spell it Bulin47 baile or Bulin 47 IA baile.

Then the pets joined. AI pet clips of the routine appeared from 11 October (three AI pugs, then three AI cats), and on 20 October @jonnyslife_ posted an AI dog that dances it upright on its hind legs in a kitchen: 170,300 views by 24 October, the most viewed AI pet take we found. Spanish captions call it the perrito bailando Dame Hombro, English ones a dog dancing to Bulin 47.
