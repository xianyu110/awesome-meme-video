## What is the Hop Hop Hop Dance trend, and where did it start?

The sound came first. On 24 September 2026 the small account @venadeaakira posted a CapCut edit of pencil sketches with a clip of the song "Её парень" ("Her Boyfriend") by the Russian artist Shluzov, which is why people search for the hop hop hop Russian song or the her boyfriend Russian song. The clip had 172,900 views by 30 October.

In mid October cosplay and alt creators turned it into the dance: one person in front points back at a friend, the friend hops, and the two swap on a hard cut. Creators credit "gabi" for it, so many call it the gabi trend. @gabilagaba's duo clip "MY TRRENDD W TWIN" (18 October) passed 3.6 million views and 547,800 likes, and her Miku cosplay version (21 October) 1.4 million.

Then it went wide. The artist duo @nirami1 and Cole passed 1.3 million views on 24 October, and an AI-made clip of two folk-hero characters by @pouzato7 reached 2.5 million on 25 October.
