## What is the Day of the Dead Pet trend, and where did it start?

In Mexico, families remember their dead on 1 and 2 November with an ofrenda: an altar with photos, candles, food and orange cempasúchil (marigold) petals that guide the souls home. Many families now make a place on the altar for their pets too, and 27 October has become the unofficial day when pets come back to visit.

The AI version started on 29 September 2025, when @alemerinoj showed how she made a video of her dogs crossing a bridge of marigolds with Google's Gemini, and shared both of her prompts in the caption. Mexican outlets printed her prompts in late October, and her post had 1.5 million views a year later. On 24 October 2025 @petmemoriesai posted a dog on the marigold bridge with the words "This is his first Day of the Dead, don't leave him behind". It reached 10.1 million views and 754,600 likes.

The trend came back in October 2026 as a CapCut template in a 3D animated film look: a skeleton officer at the gate of the land of the dead finds the pet's photo on its family's altar and lets it in. Three days after the first posts, its sound was in 1,209 videos.

For an AI Day of the Dead pet video, use **one clear photo of your pet**, the one that looks most like them.

## Pet ofrenda ideas: how to make an altar for your pet

An ofrenda is the home altar of the Day of the Dead. In Mexico it is said that pets come back to visit on 27 October and leave on the morning of the 28th, so many families set up a small altar just for them, an ofrenda para mascotas in Spanish. A Day of the Dead pet altar can be as small as one shelf. In the US, Google searches for "ofrenda for pets" reached about 40,500 in October 2025, and for "pet ofrenda" about 27,100.

What a Día de los Muertos pet ofrenda, or pet altar, usually has, as El Imparcial listed it in October 2025:

- **A photo of your pet** in the center.
- **Candles** to light the way home.
- **Marigold (cempasúchil) flowers**, often with a little path of petals up to the altar.
- **Water and their favorite food or treats**, for the long trip.
- **A toy, the collar or the name tag**, and a small blanket or cushion to rest on.
- **A short letter** with a few words of love or thanks.

More ideas for an ofrenda for pets: papel picado (cut paper banners) in your pet's favorite color, a sugar skull with its name, or a path of petals in the shape of paw prints.

**When to set it up.** On Día de los Muertos, pets have their own day: 27 October. Families put out the pet ofrenda on October 27, a few days before the Day of the Dead altar for the family's people on 1 and 2 November. In 2026, 27 October falls on a Tuesday.

Your video can be part of it: play it for the family at the altar, or post it on 27 October with a photo of the ofrenda you made.

## Day of the Dead Pet trend questions

### What is the Day of the Dead pet trend?

It is a way to remember a pet that died, which started in Mexico in 2025. People turn a photo of their dog or cat into a short video of its first Día de Muertos: the pet crosses a bridge of marigold petals, or walks through the gate into a glowing land of the dead. ### When is the Day of the Dead for pets?

In Mexico, 27 October has become the unofficial day for pets: it is said that pets arrive that day and leave on the morning of the 28th. The Day of the Dead itself is on 1 and 2 November.

### What is the meaning of the marigold bridge?

In this trend it is the path a pet takes between the land of the dead and the home of its family, covered in orange cempasúchil (marigold) petals. Marigolds are the flower of the Day of the Dead: their color and scent are said to guide the souls, and families often lay a little path of petals up to the ofrenda. A pet crossing the marigold bridge is coming home to visit.

### Is this like the dog crossing Rainbow Bridge videos?

It comes from the same wish. The Rainbow Bridge of English pet-loss poems is where a pet waits for its family; in this Mexican trend the bridge is made of marigolds, and the pet crosses it to visit on the Day of the Dead. Both work as a pet memorial video, and the Marigold bridge version comes closest to a rainbow bridge video, with your pet running toward you.

### What goes on a Day of the Dead pet ofrenda?

A photo of your pet, candles, marigold petals, water, their favorite food or treats, a toy or the collar, and a short letter. A smaller altar than the family's is usual. The list under Pet ofrenda ideas above has the details.
