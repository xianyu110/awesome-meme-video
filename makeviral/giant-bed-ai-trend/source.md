### Which photo works best?

Use a **clear photo with your face and body in view**, standing or sitting. The photo prompt puts you in pajamas at the foot of the bed, so your clothes do not matter, but it needs to see your build and hair to keep you recognizable from a distance.

- A full-body or knees-up photo beats a close-up selfie. You will be small in the frame, and a selfie leaves the model guessing about the rest of you.
- Face the camera in soft, even light.
- For a pet, pick a photo where the whole animal is sharp and visible, from ears to paws.
- One person or one pet per photo.
- Use a photo of yourself, of an adult who agreed, or of your own pet. Never a celebrity or a stranger.

## Why did "the bed of my dreams" go so viral?

The huge bed AI trend is a wish, not a stunt. Almost everyone has wanted a bigger bed, and the clip shows that wish taken to a silly extreme with no drama at all: no cuts, no stunt, just a person crawling and crawling. The length of the crawl is the joke. The viewer keeps watching to see if they ever reach the pillows.

The filming also matters. The original is a wide, static shot that looks like a real home video, not a movie. The bed is impossible, but the room, the light and the camera are ordinary, and that mix is what makes people look twice. It is also calm and cozy, so it sits well next to the loud clips in a feed and gets shared as a mood: "me on Sunday".

## What goes wrong with the giant bed prompt, and how do you fix it?

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The bed looks normal size | Generate the still again. Check that the bed touches both walls and nearly reaches the ceiling before you animate. |
| The person reaches the pillows in two seconds | Keep "for a long time" in the video prompt. The 10-second length gives the crawl room; do not shorten it. |
| The camera moves or zooms | Keep "the camera does not move". A static frame is what makes it look like a home video. |
| Your face is hard to recognize | You are small in a wide shot. Use a sharper, well-lit photo, or ask for "a medium-wide shot" in the photo prompt. |
| Arms or legs blend into the covers | Common with white on white. Ask for dark or colored pajamas in the photo prompt. |
| The video model refuses the photo | Some models refuse some photos. A failed video returns its credits; try Veo 3.1 Fast. |
