## Prompts

### 1. 1. Open [AI Photo with this template](https://www.makeviral.ai/photo?template=giant-bed-ai-trend) and add your photo (your photo). The photo prompt is filled in

*Kind: photo_prompt*

```text
Use the uploaded image as the base. Keep the subject's face, facial features, skin tone, and hair exactly the same. Place the subject in pajamas at the foot of a gigantic, fluffy white bed that fills a whole bedroom wall to wall and nearly touches the ceiling, with a mountain of huge pillows at the far end. Soft morning light from a big window. Realistic amateur phone photo, wide static framing. Vertical 9:16. No text, no logos.
```

### 2. 3. Press the film button on the still to open AI Video with the still as the first frame. Settings: Seedance 2.5, 10 s, 720p, 9:16, sound on. The video prompt

*Kind: video_prompt*

```text
Static wide phone shot, like a real home video. Keep the person's face and pajamas exactly as in the image. The person climbs onto the giant white bed and crawls across it for a long time, sinking into the soft covers. They finally reach the huge pillows, flop face-first into them and sigh happily. The camera does not move. Audio: soft fabric rustling, a happy sigh, quiet room tone. No text, no logos.
```
