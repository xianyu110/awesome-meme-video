## What is the Hand Pinch Filter trend, and where did it start?

The joke is old. The Pinch My Cheeks filter, a TikTok face effect, draws a hand that pinches your cheek or your lip, and people claimed that if you close your eyes, you can actually feel it. In July 2021 a stitch of the effect asking "can you feel it?" got 2.8 million views, and in February 2023 a creator's "Apparently if you close your eyes you can feel it" clip got another 2.8 million. In those clips the hand is a flat blue cartoon hand.

The 2026 wave is different: the hand looks real. On 21 July 2026 the same creator posted the line again, this time as a voice-over, and the clip passed 3.5 million views. From 25 July, the realistic-hand version of the effect took off in South Africa, where one clip passed 5.4 million views in days. This realistic hand pinch filter is the one everyone copies now. People sit still with their eyes closed, a lifelike hand comes in from the side, pinches their cheek and pulls it out far, and they flinch and laugh. From 29 July, how-tos for the "hand pinch filter effect" spread it to Latin America and Europe: one posted from France passed 1 million views on 24 September, and one from Mexico got 931,500 views on 8 October. On 20 October, #pinchfilter showed 5,683 videos and 109.2 million views, with more new posts every day.
