## What is the AI Baby Dance trend, and where did it start?

The AI baby dance meme is not new. A wave of dancing baby AI clips, babies animated to an AI-made song, spread on TikTok in early January 2026, and the Daily Dot covered it on 29 January, with clips at 1.5 million and 1.8 million views. Know Your Meme lists that first wave as the Sway Dance trend: babies wobble in a Bollywood-like dance.

The autumn wave started with an adult. On 9 September 2026 the French creator @bouuhyachaka posted a "dispute" routine in her kitchen to a deep house track: she grabs her head, frames her face, puts a hand on her hip, waves the camera off, then storms up to the lens. It had 17.2 million views and 1.1 million likes by 12 October. AI versions with pets followed, among them an AI cat on 22 September with 2.3 million views.

Then a CapCut template put babies in her place, promoted with the line "Wait until your baby starts dancing...". Its posts from 29 September and 2 October had 5.9 million and 7.9 million views by 12 October, and the sound it uses was in 11,500 videos. A second baby template, "TOC TOC TOC", reached 3.7 million views on 6 October.

People search for it in other languages too: "bebê dançando IA" in Portuguese, "bebé bailando IA" in Spanish and "bébé qui danse IA" in French.

### Which photo works best?

To make your baby dance, AI needs only **one photo of your own baby**. The baby from the photo does the moves, in the photo's own room or garden.

- **Upright works best.** The routine is done standing, so a photo of your baby standing or sitting up straight gives the most natural result. A baby lying down can get odd legs.
- **Head to toes in view** if you can, at least head to waist, with the face turned to the camera.
- **One baby, nobody else.** Crop out a parent's hands, a sibling or a pet.
- **Good light, no blur.** Hats, hoods and sunglasses hide what the model needs.
- **A vertical photo** gives a vertical clip, and a tidy background looks better, because the frame and the scene come from your photo.
- Only your own child, or a child whose parent or guardian asked you to. Never a stranger's photo.
