## Why it doesn't even make sense: how the trick works

The Rollie on my wrist meme is a camera angle, not strength. The lifter turns their back to the camera while the rider lies across their shoulders. Then the rider lifts their head right behind the lifter's head, so the camera sees the rider's face on top of the lifter's back, arms and legs. For a second it looks like the rider was standing there all along, and that is the joke the comments love.

Doing it for real takes a strong lifter, a light rider and a few tries. The AI version keeps the same angle and timing, so nobody has to lift anyone.
