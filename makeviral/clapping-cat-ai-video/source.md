The clip comes from Aleksandr Kim, an AI creator who posts as **@merkurianin** and calls himself the father of two meme cats, a black one named Peep and a ginger one named Poop. On 25 September 2026 he posted the same video on Instagram and TikTok: he hands his black cat a treat, then the cat sits up on the tiled floor, flashes a wide human grin and claps a pair of human hands at him. By 11 October the TikTok post had 43.5 million plays and 6.1 million likes.
