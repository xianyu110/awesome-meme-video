## What is the Venom Car AI trend, and where did it start?

The Venom car AI trend showed up on TikTok in the second week of October 2026 (the earliest reel we found is from 9 October) as a CapCut and Dreamina template: from one photo of a car, a black liquid hand grabs the car's badge off the floor, balls up, bursts into spiky tendrils and rebuilds the car piece by piece, chassis and engine first, then the body.
