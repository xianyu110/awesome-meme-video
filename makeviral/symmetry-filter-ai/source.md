## Asymmetry vs symmetry: what the two twins show

No face is perfectly symmetrical. One eye sits a little higher, one eyebrow arches more, the smile pulls to one side, the hair parts on one side. The face symmetry filter makes that easy to see: when one half of the face is mirrored, those small differences double, so the two twins often look like two different people. That is why the asymmetry vs symmetry face posts get so many comments.

It is a game, not a beauty test. The filter gives no score and says nothing about which side is better. Some creators post it with a kind message: everyone has an asymmetrical face, and it is normal. The symmetrical face vs asymmetrical face question is the fun part, not a grade.
