### Which photo works best?

Upload **one photo of the drawing**.

- **Flat and straight.** Lay the paper on a table and shoot from straight above in daylight, so the whole drawing fills the photo with no shadow of your phone on it.
- **One main subject.** One lion, one car or one dog works best. A busy page with many things gives the AI too many choices.
- **Pick the right version.** Animals, monsters, dinosaurs and dragons go to **Creature**, cars, buses, planes and boats to **Vehicle**, and a drawing of your own dog or cat to **Pet**.
- **Bold colors help.** Crayon, marker and paint carry over as the real colors. A pencil outline works too; it comes out in natural colors.
- **No people in the photo.** Keep hands and faces out of the shot. A person on the drawing becomes a plush toy figure, never a real human.
