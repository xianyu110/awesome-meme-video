## What is the Can Can Dance trend, and where did it start?

The can-can started in the dance halls of Paris in the 1830s and became the show dance of the Moulin Rouge, which opened in 1889: a line of dancers, high kicks and big skirts. The tune everyone knows is the "Infernal Galop" from Jacques Offenbach's operetta *Orpheus in the Underworld* (1858).

On TikTok the can-can turned into a joke. On 15 May 2023, @happykelli posted a trio in a living room, in bright matching sweatsuits, doing claps, hip sways and a kick line with linked arms: 6.9 million views. On 11 April 2024, @thisisnotaprilatall posted her own "silly little dance" to the tune: hops with high knees, kicks and flapping arms. It reached 30.7 million views and 5.0 million likes, and duos, couples and families copied it as the can can trend. By 22 October 2026, the two main can-can sounds had 467,600 and 178,300 TikTok videos, and #cancan had 3.6 billion views.

AI joined too: on 1 June 2025, @morph_verse posted an AI kitten in a red can-can dress dancing on a Paris street (273,200 views), and on 21 October 2026 two AI video sites added a can can dance AI template.
