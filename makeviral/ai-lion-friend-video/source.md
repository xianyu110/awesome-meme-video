### Which photo works best?

Use **one sharp photo of yourself, ideally head to toe**, or at least from the knees up. The still puts you on a dark road beside a car, lit by headlights, so the model needs your face, your build and your clothes.

- Face the camera with your eyes open and no sunglasses. The clip is filmed from a few meters away, so a sharp face matters most.
- Your clothes stay as in the photo, so pick the outfit you want to wear in the clip.
- One person in the photo. With two, the model may merge them or leave one out.
- A calm, relaxed photo fits best: in this trend you are the one who is not scared.
- Use your own photo, or a photo of an adult who agreed. Never a celebrity or a stranger.
