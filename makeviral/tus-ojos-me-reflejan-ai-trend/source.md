## What is the Tus Ojos Me Reflejan trend, and where did it start?

The line "Tus ojos me reflejan el sentimiento, justo en tus pupilas se deduce que es real" ("your eyes reflect the feeling back to me, right in your pupils you can tell it is real") comes from the song "Cafuné" by Micro TDH. TikTok had a small hashtag for it since August 2025, with 58 videos.

The trend itself is new. In the last week of October 2026, people in Colombia and Mexico began posting one kind of photo over that part of the song: a very close shot of the eyes, the face turned on its side, a hand resting on the cheek like a caress, soft warm light, often a red string bracelet on the wrist, and the line written across the middle. Among the earliest posts we found, @zz.tey's on 24 October has 90,700 views and @secrex165's on 25 October has 81,100.

The hashtag #tusojosmereflejan started on 27 October. From 28 October, CapCut and photo app template accounts turned the look into a one-tap plantilla cafune and posted step-by-step guides; the biggest has 3.1 million views. By 7 November the hashtag had 821 videos and 10 million views.
