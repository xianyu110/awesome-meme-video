## What is the TV Show Halay trend, and where did it start?

Halay is the national dance of Turkey: a line of dancers who hold each other by the fingers, hands or shoulders, usually to the davul drum and the zurna pipe. The first and last dancers may hold a handkerchief, the *mendil*.

On Turkish TV music shows, a halay often breaks out on the studio stage. Folk singer Latif Doğan makes it a regular part of *Küstüm Show*, his program on Vatan TV. In "Muhteşem Halay Show", a 2022 clip on his YouTube channel with 2.2 million views, he leads the line with a white handkerchief, arm in arm with singer Uygar Doğanay and another guest, while the davul player stands right in front of them.

In late September 2026, AI creators began to put other faces into this kind of TV halay, and TikTok now files the clips under "latif doğan halay yapay zeka".
