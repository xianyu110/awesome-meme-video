*Kind: video_prompt*

```text
Create a photorealistic vertical brand commercial using the uploaded PRODUCT IMAGE ([Image 1]) as the exact product reference and the uploaded LOGO IMAGE ([Image 2]) only for the final end frame.

Nighttime exterior of a modern apartment building. Start very far away, showing most of the building facade with many windows. Only one apartment window is warmly illuminated and subtly attracts attention. All the other windows are dark.

ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS SHOT, NO CUTS.

The camera begins a slow, smooth, cinematic physical push-in through the night air toward that specific illuminated window. The movement must feel like a real camera physically traveling toward the building: NOT a digital zoom, NOT a sudden crop, NOT a transition. The apartment gradually becomes larger and clearer as the camera approaches.

Through the window, 2 friends are naturally spending time together and casually using / eating / interacting with the exact PRODUCT from the uploaded product reference. If [Image 2] shows a branded package (a bag, a box or a package), that package is on the table. Preserve the product’s real shape, packaging, colors, proportions, branding and label. The product must be clearly recognizable and naturally integrated into the scene.

At first, everyone is completely unaware of the camera. They behave naturally and do NOT look toward the lens.

The camera continues slowly approaching until the people and product are clearly visible through the window.

Then ONE person notices the camera.

IMPORTANT TIMING: He stops what he is doing and looks directly into the camera with a genuinely irritated, suspicious expression, as if realizing a stranger has been watching them.

He does NOT smile. He is NOT surprised or scared. He does NOT immediately close the curtains.

He maintains uncomfortable direct eye contact with the camera for approximately 2 seconds.

Then, still visibly annoyed, he slowly puts down what he is holding, calmly stands up from the table and walks naturally toward the window while maintaining eye contact with the camera.

No rushing. No sudden movements.

When he reaches the window, he pauses briefly and gives the camera one final irritated stare.

Only then, he grabs both curtains and slowly pulls them together from both sides until the window is completely covered.

The curtains physically block the camera’s view of the apartment. The closed curtains stay warmly lit from inside.

After the curtains fully close, hold for a brief beat, then transition naturally to a clean final brand frame using the uploaded LOGO IMAGE exactly as provided. Centered logo, premium minimal presentation. Do not redesign, rewrite or distort the logo. Do not substitute any other version of the brand's logo.

VISUAL STYLE: Photorealistic live-action commercial, realistic apartment architecture, realistic humans, subtle natural acting, premium cinematic lighting, warm interior light contrasted with cool blue nighttime exterior, realistic window reflections, natural depth of field, believable camera movement, high-end advertising cinematography, 9:16 vertical.

CRITICAL: The entire approach from distant building exterior to close window view must feel like ONE continuous physical camera movement. The product must remain faithful to the uploaded reference. The people must interact naturally with the product. The person notices the camera ONLY after the camera gets close. After noticing it, he MUST stare angrily for about 2 seconds BEFORE standing up. He must then physically walk from his seat to the window. He closes the curtains ONLY after reaching the window. Curtains close slowly and completely. As soon as the curtain closes, the logo appears, but the curtain remains softly visible in the background.

NO cuts, NO teleporting camera, NO instant zoom, NO morphing faces, NO distorted hands, NO product redesign, NO fake product text, NO extra logos, NO smiling at camera, NO waving, NO comedy, NO exaggerated anger, NO immediate curtain closing, NO character teleportation, NO sudden movements.

Audio: quiet night city ambience, soft sounds from the room, a quick fabric swish when the curtains close, and a short, warm original music sting when the logo appears. No speech, no lyrics.
```
