## What is the Caught at the Window trend, and where did it start?

The format started in Turkey. On 8 October 2026 the Ankara burger shop Sofa Burger posted an ad where a boy eats a burger, sees the camera and pulls the curtain, with the line "gözün kalmasın" ("don't stare at it"). It reached about 46,000 likes, where the shop's posts usually get about 30.

On 11 October the Russian AI creator @karina.aicontent posted her own take: an unofficial concept ad for a burger chain, made with Seedance 2.5 from a photo of a boy, the branded bag and the burger box. It passed 25 million views and 1.7 million likes. Restaurants copied it the same day.

On 12 and 13 October creators shared prompts. @welamall in Baku posted the first public prompt, and the Turkish art director Ülkü KURT named it the **YAKALANDIN trend** (yakalandın trendi, "you got caught") in a tutorial for Google Flow and its Omni 1.1 Flash model. By 14 October small brands in Turkey, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan and Russia were making their own: burgers, sausages, mineral water, water filters.
