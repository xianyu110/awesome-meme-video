## What is the Ghostface AI trend, and where did it start?

The trend borrows the look of the masked killer from *Scream*, the 1996 slasher film that opens with a phone call. It became easy to make in late August 2025, when Google released its new Gemini image model, nicknamed Nano Banana, which keeps your face the same while it changes everything around you.

On 25 September 2025, Instagram user @cortneydryden posted what Know Your Meme calls the earliest known example: an AI photo of herself on a big 90s phone in a Y2K bedroom, with the masked figure in the doorway behind her. A day later, TikToker @keepingupwithmuva shared a set of these photos together with the prompt, and the post passed 2.7 million views in four days. Most posts used a remix of "Deceptacon" by Le Tigre. Within days people posted Ghostface pictures with their own twists: the figure stealing the popcorn, mirror selfies, couples and versions for men.

Worldwide Google searches for "ghostface ai" (also spelled "ghost face ai") peaked in the week of 28 September 2025. In early October 2026, new Ghostface AI templates and remakes started to appear on TikTok again, and it came back as an AI Halloween trend for a second year.

## The viral Ghostface prompt, and why we wrote our own

The Ghostface trend prompt that spread on TikTok in September 2025, a Halloween AI trend prompt that The Tab published on 29 September 2025, reads like one person's wish list: "a dreamy y2k style portrait of me laying on a shiny pink satin bedding as i hold a large 90s style chorded phone... her long black hair... in loose curls with pink clips on each side... brown lipgloss and brown lip liner", and then names the killer and the film.

We changed three things:

- **It describes one woman's look.** If your hair is short, blonde or curly, or if you are a man, that text pushes the model to change your hair and makeup to match hers. Our prompts dress you in a plain late-90s outfit and leave your own hair alone.
- **It never asks the model to keep your face.** The face is the whole point of the photo, so our prompts start with "Keep the person's face, facial features, skin tone, hair, gender and body shape exactly the same".
- **It names a film character.** Our prompts never name a film, a character or a brand. They describe what should be in the frame instead: a tall figure in a black hooded robe with a long white ghost mask. They leave out knives and blood, so the photo stays spooky rather than violent. Both prompts are on this page to copy, so you can also paste them into the image tool you already use.

We tested the prompts on 8 October 2026 with AI-made people, and the tests changed them twice. Our first version let the model hang posters of the mask on the bedroom wall, so the posters are now soft, blurred colors with no faces and no words. A magazine on the bed showed a real magazine name, so the magazines are gone.
