# AI Dog Video: You, Your Dog and the Snack It Fetches

- Source: [makeviral](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/cutedog-ai-trend)
- Slug: `cutedog-ai-trend`

The cutedog trend is a funny dog AI video: a 10-second home skit where you ask your dog for a snack, it runs to the kitchen and brings back a green snack packet, you laugh and eat one, and it begs for a bite.
