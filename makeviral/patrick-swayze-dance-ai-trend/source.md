## What is the Rasputin Dance & Lift trend, and where did it start?

The moves are not from a film. The Brazilian dancer and dance teacher Julia K Barni made up the routine to Boney M.'s 1978 song "Rasputin" in 2021, on a bus to work, and posted it when she got home. It became one of the best-known routines of the Rasputin dance challenge: @happykelli's version of 4 May 2023 has 4.1 million views, and many of the 16,573 #rasputinchallenge videos credit her.

On 7 March 2026 the Polish AI account @dreamweaver.pl used a motion-control AI video model to put the face of Patrick Swayze, the late star of the 1987 film Dirty Dancing, on the routine. Its post of 16 March reached 2.86 million views, and the name stuck: the Patrick Swayze dance.

From August 2026 Spanish template tutorials sold a one-photo version of it as "la plantilla de baile de Patrick Swayze": the tutorial by @aini18121 of 30 August has 1.19 million views, and the template showed 240,500 uses in a tutorial of 19 October. In the same weeks couples filmed their own Dirty Dancing lift, the running catch and overhead hold: one try in a living room has 2.6 million views (13 September).
