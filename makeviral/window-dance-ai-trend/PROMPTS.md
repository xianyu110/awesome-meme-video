# Window Dance Trend: Get Caught Dancing at the Lit Window

- Source: [makeviral](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/window-dance-ai-trend)
- Slug: `window-dance-ai-trend`

The window dance trend is the October 2026 follow-up to the window peeper meme: at night the camera zooms in to the one lit window of a dark apartment block, where someone dances next to a small speaker, spots the camera and shuts the curtain.

## Prompts

### 1. 1. The video prompt is filled in. Settings: Seedance 2.5, 12 s, 720p, 9:16, sound on. The video prompt

*Kind: video_prompt*

```text
Create a photorealistic vertical video of the person in [Image 1], caught dancing alone at a lit window at night. Never write the words of this prompt, photo labels or file names as text anywhere in the video.

The person is the one in [Image 1], in every frame: copy the exact face from [Image 1] (the face shape, eye color and eye shape, eyebrows, nose, mouth, skin tone, age, hair color, hair length and hairstyle, and any beard, moustache, glasses or freckles), so their friends recognize them at once. The same face from the first frame to the last; never morph. They wear the clothes from [Image 1]. If [Image 1] shows an animal, it is that animal standing upright on its hind legs, with its own face, fur and markings.

Nighttime exterior of an old apartment block of dark brick. Start far away, showing the building facade with many windows. Only one apartment window is warmly illuminated. All the other windows are dark.

ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS SHOT, NO CUTS.

0-2 s: the camera begins a slow, smooth, cinematic physical push-in through the night air toward that illuminated window: NOT a digital zoom, NOT a sudden crop, NOT a transition. Then the window fills the frame: the person stands in a cozy room right behind the glass, seen from the knees up, the face large and sharp. A small wooden table stands at the window with a small pink cube speaker on it, a soft blue light ring glowing on its front, with no logo and no writing. A long dark curtain hangs at the right side of the window; a framed painting and a small chandelier in the warm room behind.

2-7 s: at first, the person is completely unaware of the camera and dances happily to the music from the speaker: bouncing on the knees, fist pumps in the air, swinging the arms side to side, small steps left and right, a big carefree grin. Loose, joyful, a little goofy, like nobody is watching.

7-10 s: then the person notices the camera, slows down to a small shoulder groove, folds the hands at the waist and looks directly into the camera with a cheeky, caught-out smile. They do NOT immediately close the curtain.

10-12 s: only then, still smiling, they turn, grab the curtain and pull it across until the window is completely covered. The closed curtain stays warmly lit from inside until the end.

VISUAL STYLE: photorealistic, like a real phone video filmed from across the street at night, natural acting, warm interior light against the cool blue night, realistic window reflections, 9:16 vertical.

NO cuts, NO instant zoom, NO morphing faces, NO distorted hands, NO second person. NO logos, brand names or text on the speaker, the window or anywhere, no lyrics, no subtitles, no watermark.

Audio: an upbeat instrumental dance beat plays from the small speaker, a little muffled through the glass, with quiet night city ambience and a fabric swish when the curtain closes. No singing, no speech.
```
