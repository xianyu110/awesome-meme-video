## What is the Hideaway Trend trend, and where did it start?

"Hideaway" is the 2014 dance hit by the Canadian singer Kiesza, known for its one-take music video of her dancing down a street. On 4 September 2025 the creator @pivchanskiy.danya posted a clip of the song's "ooh, ah" part, "U? A!", and that sound became the Hideaway TikTok song people use: 1.3 million views on his post, and 304,183 videos on the sound by 26 October 2026. Through the autumn of 2025 and the spring of 2026 it backed car edits, couple clips and party clips, some with millions of views.

The Hideaway trend as people know it now took off in August 2026. On 16 August @lilyrowland1 posted "Made some friends": she sings the hook into a phone on the ground, then runs back and dances wildly in front of a row of friends who do not move. It reached 34.1 million views, and "Started a trend apparently" followed on 15 September. Friend groups, couples, nurses, moms and K-pop groups made their own takes, and on 28 September Kiesza posted "So cool tha Hideaway is trending yet again!". People call it the Hideaway dance trend, or the uuu aaa trend after the hook.

The AI side came next. Phone-editor AI templates of the dance appeared from 19 September, and an AI template for any characters gave the trend its AI scene: an open gravel lot at sunset, apartment blocks and blue tractors behind, one dancer, and a second face that pops into the lens to sing. Its first big post is two AI cats by @ashanolas (7 October 2026, 262,000 views), the reel on this page. That is the Hideaway AI trend this template makes.
