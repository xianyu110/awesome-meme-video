## Yamal Fake Rabona trend questions

### What is a rabona?

A rabona kick is a pass, cross or shot where the kicking leg wraps behind the standing leg, so the player kicks "cross-legged". Rabona football (rabona soccer in the US) uses it for show and for surprise: a rabona pass or a rabona assist from the byline is a classic.

### What is the rabona meaning?

The word is Spanish: in Argentina "hacerse la rabona" means to skip school, and the name stuck to the trick. The fake rabona, or "la no rabona", is the same swing of the leg with no kick at the end.

### What did Lamine Yamal do in the fake rabona?

Against Galatasaray on 13 October 2026, Yamal ran to the byline, crossed his left leg behind his right as if to cross with a rabona, never kicked, dragged the ball back past the defender and set up Anthony Gordon's goal. He called it a "near-rabona" (AP).

### How to do a rabona, and how do you fake one?

For a real rabona, plant your standing foot beside the ball, swing your kicking leg behind it and strike the ball with the inside or the laces. For the fake rabona skill, make the same swing but stop short, then drag the ball back with your sole while the defender commits. A fake rabona tutorial on video is the easiest way to learn the timing; on MakeViral you only need a photo.
