## What is the Kakalika Dance trend, and where did it start?

"Kakalika" is a song by DopeNation (also spelled Dope Nation), the Ghanaian twin duo, and its official video came out on 5 November 2025. The title is Ghanaian slang for a cockroach, which is the Kakalika meaning most people search for. The Kakalika dance, also spelled Kalika dance, spread on TikTok in January 2026: the creators credit @TooRisky07, @Fidelis and @chrissy.com for the Kakalika choreography. @emily.112056 posted it on 10 January (2.0 million views) and @miss1_2step with a group on 20 January (5.9 million views).

The Kakalika dance trend kept growing all year. A solo take by @besishalilaj on 13 February reached 14.9 million views and 1.36 million likes, AI creators made a dancing cockroach in sneakers in March, and a dance battle crew performed it on stage in September (11 million views). Many people film it alone with the phone low on the ground, so the kicks and the baggy trousers look huge: @aimee.alwright's take of 23 June, the one this template copies, had 2.3 million views by 10 November. On that day #kakalika had 55,702 TikTok videos and 884 million views. In early November 2026, AI video sites added Kakalika dance AI templates.
