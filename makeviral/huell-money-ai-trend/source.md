## What is the Huell Money trend, and where did it start?

The scene comes from "Buried", season 5, episode 10 of Breaking Bad, first aired on AMC on 18 August 2013. Saul Goodman's men Huell (Lavell Crawford) and Kuby (Bill Burr) are in a storage unit with Walter White's cash, stacked into a pile the size of a bed. Huell says he has to do it, man, and lies down on it. Kuby tells him they are there to do a job, not channel Scrooge McDuck, then says "Ah, screw it" and lies down too.

The clip went up on YouTube a week later as "Breaking Bad Huell - I Gotta Do It Man" (26 August 2013) and has 4.3 million views. Know Your Meme lists the still as the Huell rolling in money meme, also called Huell sleeping on money: people post it with captions about getting a small amount of money. On TikTok the show's own account posted the money pile clip on 30 March 2024 and got 2.6 million views, and #huell has 310 million views.

The Huell money AI trend is new. In November 2026 the Breaking Bad money pile scene showed up as a two-photo face swap template on AI video sites, and the AI clip on this page, two new people in the same storage unit, is that template's own example.
