## What is the Smash and Grab AI trend, and where did it start?

Smash and Grab is a template format more than a meme. Higgsfield added a "Smash and grab" AI effect in early September 2026: its example clips are dated 8 September, and it takes a character photo and a product photo. The beats never change. The camera sits inside a parked car with the product on the back seat, the character smashes the side window and snatches it, and the clip cuts to a getaway shot with police lights behind. Higgsfield pitched it for "bold product reveals, playful action edits, and dynamic lifestyle ads".

AI creators tried it within days. On 11 September Los Angeles AI creator @billionheirkc posted a version with his own AI character and a red handbag, "created with Higgsfield's Smash and Grab template". A spec soda ad with "the Smash and Grab effect" followed on 17 September. Other AI video sites added their own Smash and Grab templates: Kapwing on 8 September, Pollo AI by 16 September, imageat on 22 September and Media.io, whose trend page with copy-ready prompts went up on 6 October. It is still a niche format: on 14 October TikTok had no #smashandgrabai tag, and Google showed no measurable US searches yet.
