## What is the Atam İzindeyiz trend, and where did it start?

The phrase came long before the AI trend. "Atam izindeyiz" is what Türkiye says to Atatürk every 10 Kasım, the day he died in 1938, and it fills school boards, banners and posts around 29 Ekim, Republic Day. The TikTok tag #atamizindeyiz holds 63,414 videos and 650.8 million views (28 October 2026), most of them older remembrance posts.

The AI look started in mid-October 2026. The earliest post we found is from 15 October: a woman in a black dress walks past the silhouette under "ATAM, İZİNDEYİZ! ♾️". On 20 October @kozmiksef posted "Atatürk'ün gölgesinde büyüyen bir nesil hayal edin…" (11.3K views) and wrote his prompt in the comments when people asked. On 23 October @gizemceblogsunum posted "Atam, izindeyiz!" (17.9K views) with her own "Atatürk'ün gölgesinde Prompt" in the comments, and more creators copied the look. From 27 October an AI photo app ran ads with the same scene for 29 Ekim. Most posts show children; this page is for adults, and it keeps the creators' prompts for an adult photo.

### Which photo works best?

You need **one photo of yourself**, an adult.

- **Head to toe is best.** The picture shows your whole body and your feet next to the silhouette, so a full-body photo keeps your height and your proportions. A waist-up photo works too: the AI adds the rest.
- **A sharp face, turned to the camera,** in good daylight, with no sunglasses, no hat and no filter.
- **Only you in the photo.** The scene has one person and the silhouette.
- **Your clothes change to dark formal wear:** a black suit and tie for a man, a black dress for a woman (the Graffiti version picks a dark suit-style outfit). Your face and hair stay yours.
- Use your own photo, or the photo of an adult who said yes. Never a child, a celebrity or a stranger.

## In English: what does "Atam izindeyiz" mean?

"Atam izindeyiz" means "My Ata, we are on your path" or "we follow in your footsteps". "Ata" means father or ancestor, and "Atam" is how Turks speak to Atatürk, the founder of the Republic of Türkiye. That is the atam izindeyiz meaning, and also the answer to anyone searching atam izindeyiz ingilizce. In the AI trend the words stand on the wall, and the person walks the same way as his silhouette: the picture says the phrase.
