The pixie layered haircut trend started in the Philippines in the first days of November 2026, after a Filipina actress showed a new short, layered pixie cut and fans wanted to see the same look on themselves. Nobody wanted to cut their hair to find out, so they asked AI instead: upload a selfie to ChatGPT, Gemini or the Hypic app with a pixie layered haircut prompt, or use a CapCut template, and post the before-and-after.

### Which photo works best?

Upload **one photo of one person**, face to the camera. It works for Pixie photo and Reveal video.

- **Face the camera in good light,** from the chest up, with the face large and sharp. Daylight shows your true hair color, and the cut keeps it.

- **Reveal video:** take the photo where you want the video to happen. The clip keeps that place and your clothes.
- **Pet:** one pet, head and face in view, in good light. Fluffy and long-haired pets change the most.
- Use your own photo, or a photo of a friend who said yes. Never a child, a celebrity or a stranger.
