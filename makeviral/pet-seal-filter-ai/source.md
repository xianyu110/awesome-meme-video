## What is the Seal Filter trend, and where did it start?

The **seal filter trend** comes from a CapCut AI template with the burned-in line "Trying the seal filter". It started on 3 June 2026, when the template account CAPCUTDAILYUK made the sound that most clips use. On 11 June @zeustlabrottie posted a black puppy on a garden chair turning into a sleek black seal, and it reached 262,400 views. On 6 July @staciepeters3 posted an American bulldog, and that clip reached 1.8 million views. By 30 October the sound had 3,720 videos. The **cat seal filter** clips came soon after: "born to be a seal" by @meowmemeowmemeowmemeow reached 869,500 views (15 July).

The template keeps the pet's own head and fur and gives it a fat seal body with flippers, so a lab, a dachshund or a tabby still looks like itself. Comments fill up with "I don't need the filter", because many pets already look a little like seals.

A second wave started in October with a newer version of the template, on a sound made on 1 September by @d.0.gz. @sablespins (18 October, 399,500 views), @danmorenooo (22 October, 393,300 views) and @tay.kolee (28 October, 242,900 views) brought the **dog seal filter** back to US feeds, and new clips came every day in the last two weeks of October.

### Which photo works best?

Use **one sharp photo of your pet with the whole body in view**, head to tail. The seal body grows out of your pet's real body, so the model needs to see where it sits or lies.

- A pet that sits or lies still works best: on a sofa, a bed, a chair or the floor. Lying down looks most like a seal.
- Daylight, in focus, eyes open. The face stays your pet's own, so a sharp face matters most.
- Leave a little space around the pet: the round seal body is wider than a dog or a cat.
- One animal per photo. With two, the model may merge them.
- Dogs and cats both work, and so do rabbits and other furry pets.
- For the Human version, use a photo of yourself from the waist up, sitting, with your face clear.
