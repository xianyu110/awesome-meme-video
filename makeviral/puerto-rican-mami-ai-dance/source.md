## What is the Puerto Rican Mami Dance trend, and where did it start?

The line comes from a scene of the sitcom Everybody Hates Chris, and a clip of it went around TikTok as a sound in early October 2026; the hashtag #isthatwhatyoutellyourlittlepuertoricanmami started on 13 October. On 16 October the producer Baby On The Track (@babyonthetrack) put the line on a beat, "Keep seeing ts on my fyp": 2.4 million views by 25 October, and the sound people dance to.

The next night, 17 October, @_kenyaad and @brebre, two college friends in North Carolina, posted the dance they made to it ("OUR DC"): 483,700 views by 25 October. The top comment said it first: "This finna be viral and I was here". Within a week the Puerto Rican Mami trend was everywhere as a 2 person dance (#2persondance): friends, roommates and dance teams copied it, and the sound had 8,676 videos on 25 October. From 20 October phone-editor AI templates turned photos into the dance too, which started the Puerto Rican Mami AI trend. Almost every post is from the US.
