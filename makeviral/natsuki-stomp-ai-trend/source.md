## What is the Tsundere Stomp trend, and where did it start?

The sound came first. On 23 October 2026 @theo60473 posted a white bunny bouncing to a cute original sound; the clip had 4.1 million views by 11 November, and by 10 November more than 74,000 TikTok videos used the sound. From 5 November, Russian-speaking creators filmed themselves to it and started the tsundere dance trend (#цундере, #танеццундере): hands on the hips, a pout, a stomp, crossed arms and a sharp turn away.

The same day the anime crowd gave it a face. @cuupcaekmuffs posted a clip of Natsuki, the pink-haired tsundere of the game Doki Doki Literature Club, from a fan animation by E-Bros Animations, doing the hmph pose: 2.1 million views and 565,000 likes by 11 November. That is why the English side calls it the Natsuki trend, the Natsuki stomp or the hmph trend. Adults joined fast: @savanay_60, an older man in a green sweatshirt, posted his take on 7 November (1.6 million views), and @einmann33 and @matheogoose followed on 8 November with 1.1 and 1.5 million views. By 9 November CapCut tutorials taught an AI template called "Tsundere stomps her foot".
