## What is the Drink Fly Bump trend, and where did it start?

The first clip is an AI video by Liza, the AI creator behind @podliskaaaa.ai, made with @yagodalike.saratov, a strawberry dessert shop in Saratov, Russia. They posted it on Instagram on 26 August 2026 with a caption that means "when getting a second one is no problem". In one take, a low camera rises from her gold sneakers to her face, a man in a beige jacket bumps her, and her gold-rimmed sunglasses and her iced coffee with a pink label fly into the lens. The reel passed 715,000 likes and 3,088 comments. Its sound is "Montagem Alquimia (Super Slowed)" by h6itam, n7san7os and Mc Menor Do Alvorada, which is why many people know it as the Montagem Alquimia trend.

The name came five days later. On 31 August Steven Wommack (@stevenwommack) posted a tutorial on how to recreate "this creative drink fly bump video" with yourself in it, credited Liza, and asked viewers to comment "drink" for his prompt. It passed 2.5 million views on TikTok, and his Instagram copy reached 958,000 likes. CapCut templates and more tutorials followed in early September, and the #drinkflybump hashtag started on 1 September.
