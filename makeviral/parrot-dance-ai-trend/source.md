## What is the Parrot Dance trend, and where did it start?

The trend comes from the TikTok creator @kennyslowbird, who makes AI animal clips. On 20 October 2026 he posted a few parrots dancing on a beach at sunset, "Parrots dancing to the big summer hit 'Jamaican'". The next day he posted the clip that took off: a barn full of green parrots in little white diapers, dancing together while one parrot leads the flock up close to the camera. By 28 October it had 7.6 million views, 561,000 likes and 264,600 shares, and the comments asked one thing again and again: why are the parrots wearing diapers?

The song is "Jamaican (Bam Bam)" by Hugel and Solto, released in November 2025 as a new take on Sister Nancy's 1982 reggae classic "Bam Bam". On TikTok the clip uses an "original sound" by MCBARUTI that more than 17,000 videos use.

The creator then made the same dance with baby raccoons (22 October, 645,600 views) and posted the barn parrots again as a **parrot dance class**: "Would you go to a dance class like this?" (26 October, 760,900 views). Most of the comments under the parrot clips are in Spanish.
