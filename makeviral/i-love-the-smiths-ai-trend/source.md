## What is the I Love The Smiths trend, and where did it start?

The I love the Smiths movie is *(500) Days of Summer* (2009). On Day 4 of the story, Tom stands in the office elevator with big headphones on. Summer hears the song leak out and says "I love the Smiths." He does not hear her, so she says it again, louder, sings a line and steps out, and he is left frozen as the doors close. The song is "There Is a Light That Never Goes Out" by The Smiths.

The Tom and Summer elevator scene became a TikTok meme years later. Searchlight Pictures, the film's studio, posted it as "I LOVE THE SMITHS" on 1 February 2024, and that clip has 5.5 million views. People quote the line, use it for the "I love X / Me Too" meme about shared taste, and remake it with friends as an elevator meet-cute meme. In autumn 2026 it came back: a film edit by @michael.5456 on 29 September reached 1.4 million views, and @hanan.selini's park bench remake on 21 October reached 757,100. On 5 and 6 November 2026, AI video sites added the elevator scene as a two-photo template.
