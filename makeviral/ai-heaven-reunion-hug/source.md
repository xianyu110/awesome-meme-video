## What is the Heaven Hug trend, and where did it start?

The heaven hug lives in two CapCut templates. The stairway version comes from "Un deseo", a Father's Day template from June 2026 with more than 820,000 uses: you run up a stairway of golden light, and the person you miss waits at the top. From 1 September 2026 it spread in the US as the heaven reunion. One of the first posts, by @emsmama43 for her late father, has 78,500 views. Other scenes followed: a US creator's run through a field to her grandma "when I arrive to heaven", posted on 10 October, passed 268,000 views.

The cloud version is a Día de Muertos template from Mexico, posted from 13 October 2026, about three weeks before the holiday on 1 and 2 November. It opens with the line "Imagina que vas al cielo y Dios te dice: alguien te está esperando" ("Imagine you go to heaven and God tells you: someone is waiting for you"), and you run across a sea of clouds into their arms. Its sound was in 564 TikTok videos early on 14 October and in 2,790 a day later.

### Which photo works best?

Use **two photos**: one of you, and one of the person you miss. Both versions use the same two photos.

- **Clear, sharp faces** that look at the camera, in good light. A photo from the waist up works best.
- **Your loved one at the age you want to remember.** The video keeps the age in the photo.
- **Old prints are fine.** Lay the print flat and photograph it with no glare. A black-and-white photo comes out in natural color.
- **Two people in photo 2**, like your grandparents, wait together, and you hug them both.
- **Only you in photo 1.** Crop a group photo first.
- A pet in photo 2 runs to you, and you kneel to hold it.
