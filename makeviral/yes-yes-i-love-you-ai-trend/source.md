## What is the Yes Yes I Love You trend, and where did it start?

"Ja tebia liubliu" is Russian for "I love you". Adriano Celentano released the song in 1994 on his album *Quel punto*, and its chorus says "I love you" in several languages, in English as "Yes, yes, I love you, it's so easy to say and to do". That is why people search for it as the yes yes I love you song.

In 2026 it came back as a TikTok sound. A lyric clip of the song posted on 14 August by @_cold_music__ passed 2.9 million views, and from mid-October lyric edits, CapCut templates and a slowed version spread on TikTok and Reels.

On 14 October 2026 the photographer @sasha_ne_revi posted a 13-second reel on Instagram with the caption "Yes yes i love u": she runs down a lamp-lit street of old stone buildings at night in a black off-shoulder dress, looks back with a big smile and walks away, to the slowed song. By 4 November it had 81,112 likes, commenters asked for the "template", and an AI video app turned the clip into a template of its own. This page copies that reel.
