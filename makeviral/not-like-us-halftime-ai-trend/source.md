## What is the Not Like Us Halftime trend, and where did it start?

"Not Like Us" came out in May 2024, and its first TikTok dance wave started that same month, which many called the A Minor dance after a line of the song: the #notlikeusdance tag dates from 5 May 2024.

On 9 February 2025 the Kendrick Lamar halftime show at the Caesars Superdome in New Orleans played the song near the end of the show, and the Not Like Us dance trend came back. Three days later, on 12 February, the German creator @delosaaaa and her friend posted the halftime two-step side by side on a night plaza, captioned "YOU REALLY 'BOUT TO DO IT?". It had 5.5 million views and 554,400 likes by 10 November 2026. Duos copied it all February: the twin dancers of @_luis.lz posted theirs on 20 February and reached 4.9 million views.

In autumn 2026 the stage itself became an AI trend. On 15 October HotNewHipHop reported on a 12-second AI copy of the show made for a US politician's campaign, which drew wide criticism, and from 30 October several AI video sites added Not Like Us halftime templates where you and a friend take the two lead spots. On 10 November 2026 #notlikeus held 289,463 TikTok videos and 10.5 billion views.
