## What can go wrong, and how to fix it

- **The hat hides your eyebrows.** The prompt keeps the trim above the brows, but a photo taken from above can push it down. Take the photo at eye level and run it again.
- **The face looks a little different.** Run it again: each run is a little different. A sharp, well-lit photo facing the camera helps most.
- **The cartoon looks too much like you, or not enough.** That depends on how strong the features are in the photo. Glasses, a beard and the hairstyle carry the likeness in a cartoon.
- **Matching mixed you up.** Check the photo order: photo 1 goes on top, photo 2 at the bottom. One person per photo.
- **A pet hat on the wrong spot.** Use a photo where both ears show; the hat sits between or over them.
