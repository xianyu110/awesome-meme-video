## What is the Money Flex trend, and where did it start?

The money flex trend is a joke about showing off, filmed the cheap way: a phone propped low on a dresser, a ceiling fan above, a stack of cash that is far too big. The base clip shows a young man in a black hoodie who hugs a long accordion chain of banded bundles, throws it at the camera and hides behind a fan of bills. On 9 October 2026 a CapCut template post called it the "Money money money trend", and one-photo AI templates turned the routine into an AI money trend: you upload a photo and the AI puts you in the clip.

The pet version went big first. On 9 October 2026 the Instagram account @clash posted an AI clip of a black-and-white dog doing the whole routine in the same bedroom; it had about 361,000 likes by 19 October, and a TikTok compilation that opens with the dog, posted the next day, had 5.4 million views. Since then the dog with money meme has its own crowd: people ask for a dog money flex, a cat with money, any pet money flex.

The money trend on TikTok grew fastest in Arabic. An AI clip posted on 13 October on the Arabic sound that carries the trend, "فلوسي كلها كاش" ("all my money is cash"), had 1.8 million views by 19 October; its creator wrote that the video is AI-generated. By 19 October that sound had 1,252 videos, and tutorials explained how to do "ترند الفلوس" ("the money trend"). English tutorials call it the Super Money AI Filter, the big money spread trend or the all my money is cash trend, and a CapCut template of it showed 431,900 uses on 17 October. Other sites call it Cash Flex.
