## What is the Le Dije a ChatGPT trend, and where did it start?

The trend took off on TikTok in Mexico and the Spanish-speaking US in late October 2026. Each post has three parts: a normal clip of the creator with the caption "Le dije a ChatGPT...", a collage of what they asked for ("que me pusiera esto": black browline glasses, braces with small gems, long lashes, French-tip nails, a nose stud), and then "lo que me dio", the creator with that whole look on.

The earliest big post we found is by @nahomi.rios65 in Mexico on 21 October 2026: 141,200 views and 155 comments by 29 October, most of them people showing their own result. The next day the how-to posts arrived. A tutorial by @mg8111195 on 22 October is the most viewed post of the trend so far, with 327,500 views by 29 October, and its caption lists what people ask for: braces, long lashes, blue eyes, acrylic and French nails, long hair, a beard and moustache, earrings. By 26 October a men's version with tattoos, a lip piercing and earrings had its own tutorials ("le dije a chat gpt que me pusiera esto tatuaje").

## Is it really ChatGPT?

The trend's name says ChatGPT, and some people do it in a chat app: they send their selfie with the collage and type "ponme esto". Most of the viral how-to posts, though, use a ready-made video editor template, and many people say in the comments that the result was not what they asked for: "no más los braquets no me los puso" (it did not give me the braces) or "me quedó toda rara" (I came out all weird). That is the honest risk of a general AI edit: it can skip one of the asks, or change your face.

MakeViral is not ChatGPT and does not use it.
