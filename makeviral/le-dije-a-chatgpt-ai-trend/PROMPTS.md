# Le Dije a ChatGPT Que Me Pusiera Esto: Your AI Look Change

- Source: [makeviral](https://www.makeviral.ai/templates/le-dije-a-chatgpt-ai-trend)
- Slug: `le-dije-a-chatgpt-ai-trend`

Le dije a ChatGPT que me pusiera esto ("I told ChatGPT to put this on me") is a Spanish-language TikTok trend: you show a selfie, then the looks you asked for, then the AI result of you with glasses, braces, long lashes, blue eyes or tattoos.

### 1. Prompt 1

*Kind: prompt*

```text
Use the uploaded image as the base. This is a photo edit of the same real person in the same photo, not a new portrait: keep the same pose, framing, background, clothes and light. Keep the face shape, eye shape, eye color, eyebrows, nose, lips, skin tone, skin texture and age exactly as in the photo. Add only this new look, as if the person really had it when the photo was taken: (1) black browline glasses with a thick black top rim and a thin silver wire bottom rim, sitting naturally on the nose, with real lens reflections and no distortion of the eyes; (2) metal braces on the teeth that show: small silver brackets joined by a thin wire, with a few tiny clear sparkly gems on the brackets, on the person's own teeth; if the lips are closed, part them into the person's own natural smile so the braces show; (3) long, full, dark lash extensions on the upper lashes; (4) if the hair is long, it stays long and becomes straight, sleek and glossy in its own color, with the same hairline and parting; short hair keeps its length; (5) if a hand shows in the photo, short almond nails with a black French tip and a tiny black star on two nails; never add a hand that is not in the photo; (6) a small sparkling silver stud on one side of the nose. Nothing else changes. A real phone photo with natural skin: no beauty filter, no smoothing, no slimmer face, no bigger eyes, no other makeup. Exactly one person. No text, no captions, no stickers, no emojis, no logos, no app interface.
```
