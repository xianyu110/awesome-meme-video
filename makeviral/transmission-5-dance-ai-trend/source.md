## What are the Transmission 5 dance moves?

The video copies 11 seconds of the original reel, one fixed shot with the camera low on the ground:

| Time | What the two dancers do |
|---|---|
| 0 to 3 s | A wide stance, sharp arm locks and hand passes at chest height. |
| 3 to 6 s | Arms thrown overhead, then a deep low squat with the arms up. |
| 6 to 8 s | Fast hand passes, arm swings and footwork side by side. |
| 8 to 11 s | More arm locks and footwork in sync, an arm up overhead at the end. |

These are electro dance moves: the arm work of tecktonik, close and fast, with small steps underneath. The two dancers never touch, but their arms pass right next to each other, so the timing is the hard part, and the AI copies it from the reel.
