## What is the Rick Roll trend, and where did it start?

The song came out in July 1987 and topped the charts in 25 countries, the US Billboard Hot 100 among them. Its video, directed by Simon West, shows the singer at a vintage microphone, dancing in a light trench coat under blue-lit brick arches at night and along a sunny chain-link fence.

The Rickroll began on 4chan in about May 2007, as Know Your Meme records it: a link that promised a hyped video game trailer opened the music video instead. Rick Rolling spread fast. On 1 April 2008 every featured video on the YouTube front page led to it, and the official upload passed one billion views on 28 July 2021.
